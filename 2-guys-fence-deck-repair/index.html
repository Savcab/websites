Common questions

Do you repair fences, or only install new ones?
Repair is what we're known for. Leaning sections, rotted or snapped posts, sagging gates, storm damage — we fix it. We also do full rebuilds and brand-new installs when that's the better call, and we'll tell you honestly which one your situation needs.
My fence blew down in a storm — how fast can you come?
Storm repair is a big part of what we do, and we move on it. Give us a call at (850) 305-7957 — we'll get out to assess the damage and get you a timeline. We've handled plenty of blow-downs and post failures around Crestview.
Do you work on decks too?
Yes — deck repair and rebuilds are right in our wheelhouse, from soft boards and loose railings to wobbly steps and failing framing. We also build new decks and pergola covers, like the custom deck in our gallery.
How much will my repair cost?
It depends on what's involved — the length of fence, how many posts are affected, materials and access. That's why we come look and give you an honest, upfront quote before any work starts. No surprise charges after the fact.
What areas do you cover?
We're based in Crestview and serve the surrounding Panhandle — including Niceville, Fort Walton Beach, Baker, Laurel Hill and nearby communities. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask when you call.
