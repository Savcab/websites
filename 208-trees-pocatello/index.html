Good to Know

Questions We Hear a Lot

How much does tree or stump removal cost?

It depends on size, location, and how tricky the access is — so our estimates are free and done on-site. You'll get a clear price before any work starts, with no surprises.

How fast can you get to my job?

We're known for being quick. Customers have had a stump ground the same day they called, and even when we're booked a couple weeks out we'll try hard to fit urgent work in.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes — stump grinding is one of our most-requested services. We grind it out below grade so you can reclaim the space. Ask us to quote it together with the removal.

What's the sawmill about?

We can mill your removed logs into live-edge slabs or lumber instead of hauling them off. If you'd like to keep the wood, mention it when you book and we'll talk through what's possible.

Will you clean up afterward?

Always. We buck the rounds, haul the debris, rake up grindings, and leave the site tidy — even setting pavers back in place. It's part of the job, not an add-on.

Can you take a tree down close to my house?

Yes. We bring it down in controlled sections to protect roofs, fences, and lines, including big pines tight against a structure.
