How the design happens

Your property, recorded in detail — then rendered.

A landscape design is a large decision made from a drawing. We take that guesswork out of it by building your yard as a photoreal 3D model of your own property, so you can look at the finished result and make the changes on a screen instead of in the ground.

01

We record the site

We bring 3D imaging equipment to the property and record it in detail — the house, the grades, the existing planting, the boundaries.

02

We design in 3D

The new landscape is modelled as a photoreal 3D design over that record, so the materials, the planting and the lighting are shown the way they will actually read.

03

You see it first

You review the rendered design before any ground is broken — front yard, backyard or the whole exterior, in one picture you can hold up against the real thing.
