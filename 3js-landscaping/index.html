Questions, answered

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Baldwin and serve Habersham County and the nearby parts of Banks and Hall County — including Cornelia, Demorest, Mount Airy, Alto, Clarkesville and the surrounding area. If you're close by and not certain, give us a call and we'll let you know right away.

How do I get a quote?

Call us at (706) 968-5634 or fill out the quick quote form on this page with your address and what you need done. We'll look it over and get a fair, upfront price back to you, usually within the same week.

Do you offer recurring lawn service?

Yes. Most homeowners have us mow on a regular weekly or bi-weekly schedule through the growing season, with hedge trimming and cleanups added as needed. We'll set up a routine that suits your yard.

Is pressure washing safe for my siding and deck?

We match the method to the surface — softer washing for siding and roofs, more pressure where it's appropriate for concrete and driveways. The before-and-after photos on this page are real jobs we've done around Baldwin.

What are your hours?

We're out working Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, and Saturday mornings 8:00 AM to noon. Closed Sundays. If we're on a job when you call, leave a message and we'll get right back to you.
