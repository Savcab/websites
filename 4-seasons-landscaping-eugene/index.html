Who we are

Transforming Eugene-area landscapes since 1993

4 Seasons Landscaping & Maintenance is an owner-involved crew led by Gary. For more than thirty years we've designed, planted and irrigated yards across the greater Eugene area — the kind of hands-on, do-it-right work our clients keep calling back for.

Whether you need a new sprinkler zone, a backyard water feature, a paver patio or a complete landscape redesign, we handle the whole job — design, install and the seasonal care that keeps it looking its best.

— Gary & the 4 Seasons crew

What we do

Full-service landscaping, start to finish

A few of the services Eugene homeowners call us for most.

Landscape Design & Installation

Custom plans, planting and full yard installs — from a single bed to a whole-property redesign.

Irrigation & Sprinklers

New sprinkler systems, drip lines, timers and repairs that keep every zone watering evenly.

Water Features

Ponds, garden bridges and flowing water — the finishing touch that makes an outdoor space feel alive.

Hardscape & Patios

Paver patios, walkways, seating walls and fire-pit areas built to last through Oregon seasons.

Retaining & Stonework

Stone and block retaining walls that hold slopes, define beds and shape usable outdoor rooms.

Seasonal Maintenance

Ongoing care — cleanups, bed refreshes and irrigation checkups — to keep the whole yard sharp.
