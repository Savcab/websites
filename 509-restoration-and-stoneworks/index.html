What we do

Masonry that holds up — and looks like it belongs.

From a cracked chimney to a full stone façade, every job gets the same careful prep, clean joints and proper sealing. Here is the work we take on most.

01

Chimney & brick restoration

Repointing & tuckpointing Cracked / spalled brick Cleaning & sealing

Loose, chipped or weathered brick rebuilt and re-laid so the wall is sound again. We clean every brick, replace what's failed, and seal the finished work — the kind of small repair that's hard to get a mason out for, done properly.

02

Natural & manufactured stone veneer

Exterior wainscot Columns & entries Fireplaces

Stone veneer that transforms a plain elevation — house skirting, entry columns, window surrounds and interior fireplaces. Tight, level coursing and consistent grout lines, finished to last in the Valley's hot summers and cold winters.

03

Stone & brick walkways, steps & walls

Walkways & patios Garden & retaining walls Front steps

Herringbone brick paths, stone steps, garden walls and block retaining walls — built on a proper base so they stay flat and don't shift. Equally at home on a backyard project or a larger commercial wall.

04

Concrete & flatwork

Patios & slabs Walkway pads Repair & pour

Fresh concrete patios, pads and walkways poured and finished to pair with the masonry — a clean, level surface that ties the whole project together.

A focal point worth getting right

Stone that turns a room.

A stacked-stone fireplace or a stone-clad entry is the first thing people notice — and the kind of detail that's unforgiving if the coursing is off.

We take the time to dry-lay, match the stone, and keep the lines true, so the finished feature looks intentional from every angle. The same eye goes into exterior columns, chimneys and façades.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

Do you take on small repair jobs?

Yes — small repairs are some of our favorite work. A few feet of garden wall, a set of front steps, a single chipped or cracked column, a chimney that needs repointing. These are exactly the jobs that are hard to get a mason out for, and we're glad to do them.

What kinds of masonry do you handle?

Brick, block and natural or manufactured stone — both repair and new build. That covers chimney and brick restoration, stone veneer (house skirting, columns, fireplaces), walkways and steps, garden and retaining walls, and the concrete flatwork that goes with them. Residential and commercial.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (509) 759-8492, or send the quick form below with a few details and a photo if you have one. We'll arrange a time to come look at the project in person and give you a clear estimate.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Naches and work throughout the Yakima Valley — Yakima, Selah, Tieton, Cowiche, Gleed and the surrounding area. Not sure if you're in range? Give us a call and we'll let you know.

Do you do both restoration and new work?

Both. We restore and repair existing brick and stone — cleaning, repointing, replacing failed pieces and sealing — and we build new stone veneer, walls, walkways and fireplaces from scratch.
