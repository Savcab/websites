What We Do

Weed Control

Targeted treatments that knock out the weeds taking over your turf — then keep them from coming back, season after season.

Fertilization Program

A recurring feeding schedule tuned to Texas lawns and seasons — the steady nutrition that turns a thin yard into thick, deep-green grass.

Questions

Good to know before you call

How fast will I see my lawn improve?

It depends on the starting condition, but many of our customers see a real difference in just a few service calls — weeds clearing and grass thickening within weeks, not a whole season. We'll give you an honest timeline when we assess your lawn.

Do you offer recurring programs or just one-time visits?

Both. Most lawns do best on a recurring program where fertilization, weed control, and pest treatments are scheduled through the year — that's what keeps a lawn healthy long-term. We're happy to start with a single service too.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in New Braunfels and serve the surrounding area, including Schertz, Cibolo, Seguin, and San Marcos. If you're nearby and not sure, just call and ask.

Do you handle fire ants and pests too?

Yes. Along with lawn care we treat fire ants, grubs, and mosquitoes. We're a member of the Texas Pest Control Association, so lawn health and pest control are handled together under one plan.
