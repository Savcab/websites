Good to know

Common questions.

Do you service second homes when the owner isn’t in town?

Yes — that’s a big part of what we do. Plenty of Ruidoso owners only get up here now and then, so we keep the grass mowed and the property tidy in between visits. You don’t need to be on-site for us to take care of it.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Ruidoso and work throughout the surrounding area, including Ruidoso Downs, Alto, Capitan and nearby parts of Lincoln County. If you’re close by and not sure, give us a call and we’ll let you know.

Can I get one-time cleanup, or only recurring service?

Both. We do one-time cleanups — tall grass, pine needles, brush and debris — as well as recurring mowing and maintenance on a schedule. Tell us what you need and we’ll set it up either way.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the property and the work involved, so we give you a free estimate up front before anything starts. Call or send the form with a few details and we’ll get you a straight quote.
