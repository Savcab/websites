What we do

One crew for the trees, the stumps, and the ground underneath.

Most Lake County properties need more than a saw. A. Nichols pairs professional tree work with excavating equipment, so removals, clearing, and cleanup get handled by the same trusted team.

Tree Removal

Hazardous, dead, or storm-damaged trees taken down safely — including the big oaks and pines that threaten homes, sheds, and power lines on wooded lots.

Trimming & Pruning

Canopy reduction, deadwood removal, and shaping that keeps trees healthy, clears your roofline, and opens up defensible space — with bucket-truck reach for tall work.

Stump Grinding

Old stumps ground out below grade so you reclaim the space — ready for replanting, fence lines, or fresh ground without a trip hazard left behind.

Fire-Debris Cleanup

Burned trees, ash, and debris cleared and hauled from fire-affected properties — the specialized recovery work Cobb homeowners have trusted us with after the Valley Fire.

Land & Brush Clearing

Excavator-assisted clearing of overgrowth, brush, and small trees — for building pads, driveways, firebreaks, and reclaiming a lot that's gotten away from you.

Excavating & Hauling

Compact excavator work and debris hauling to finish the job — grading rough ground, digging out roots and stumps, and getting the mess off your property for good.

Good to know

Common questions

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We come look at the tree, stump, or property, talk through what's needed, and give you an honest, written quote before any work starts — no obligation.
Can you remove large or dangerous trees near my house?
That's a big part of what we do. With a bucket truck and the right rigging, we handle tall oaks and pines in tight spots — including trees that have already fallen on or are threatening a home, shed, or power line.
Do you help with fire-debris cleanup and defensible space?
We do. We've cleared burned trees and heavy debris from fire-affected Cobb properties and cut defensible space around structures. Bring us the property and we'll tell you the most sensible way to tackle it.
What's the difference between your tree and excavating services?
Tree work is removals, trimming, and stump grinding. Excavating is the ground work — clearing brush and small trees, digging out roots and stumps, grading, and hauling debris off-site. Having both means one crew can finish the whole job.
What areas do you cover?
Cobb and the surrounding southern Lake County communities — Kelseyville, Lower Lake, Middletown, Clearlake, Hidden Valley Lake and nearby. If you're close by, just call and ask.
