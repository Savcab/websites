Fence types we install

Pick your material — we build it to last

Every yard and budget is different, so we install fences in all four common materials. Tell us the look you're after and what the fence needs to do, and we'll point you to the right one.

Wood privacy fences

Classic board-on-board and stockade privacy fencing with clean, even cap rails — the long, straight runs you see on our homepage. Built solid for Florida yards.

Most-requested

Vinyl fencing

Low-maintenance white vinyl privacy, picket and ranch-rail — great around pools and side yards, where it stands up to sun and weather without painting or staining.

Aluminum & ornamental

Sleek black aluminum fencing and matching gates that dress up an entry or pool enclosure — rust-free, see-through, and built to code without the upkeep of iron.

Chain-link fencing

Practical, budget-friendly chain-link for yards, pets and property lines — including kennel-style enclosures. The economical way to fence a lot of ground.

Ranch & farm fencing

Two-, three- and four-rail ranch fencing and cross-buck for acreage and front pastures — long property lines fenced cleanly, the way Central Florida land calls for.

Custom gates

Walk gates and wide drive gates built to match your fence and swing true for years — including the 5-ft and 10-ft gates that go with our bigger installs.

Good to know

Common questions

What kinds of fence do you install?
We install all four common materials: wood (privacy and ranch), vinyl (privacy, picket and rail), aluminum/ornamental, and chain-link. We also build matching walk gates and wide drive gates. If you're not sure which material is right for your yard or budget, we'll walk you through the trade-offs when we come measure.
Do you take on large jobs and long runs?
Absolutely — big jobs are some of our favorite work. We've done long privacy runs and ranch lines across acreage, including a recent install of around 300 feet of fence with a 5-ft walk gate and a 10-ft drive gate. We crew up so large projects get finished fast instead of dragging on.
How fast can you get my fence done?
Fast turnaround is something our customers mention a lot. Once your estimate is approved and we're scheduled, most installs — even sizable ones — are measured in days, not weeks. Give us a call at (863) 832-8114 and we'll give you a realistic timeline for your project.
Do you build custom gates?
Yes. Walk gates and wide drive gates are part of most of our installs — built to match your fence, hung so they line up, and set to swing true and latch right. If you need a wide drive gate for vehicles or equipment access, just let us know the opening when you call.
What's the estimate process, and what does it cost?
Estimates are free and no-obligation. We come out, walk the fence line, talk through materials, height and gates, and take measurements — then give you a clear, upfront price. The cost depends on length, material, height and how many gates, which is exactly why we measure in person rather than guess over the phone.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Zolfo Springs and serve Hardee County and the surrounding Central Florida area — including Wauchula, Bowling Green, Ona, Sebring, Avon Park and Arcadia. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask when you call.
