What we do

Tree work, start to finish

From the cut to the cleanup, you deal with one crew. Here's what we handle most for homeowners around Cassatt.

Tree Removal

Hazardous, dead, leaning or just-too-close trees taken down safely and in sections when space is tight — right down to the ground.

Stump Grinding

We grind the stump below grade so you can replant, mow over it or reclaim the space — no leftover trip hazard, no slow rot.

Trimming & Pruning

Storm & Hazard Cleanup

Debris Haul-Off

Logs, limbs and grindings hauled away and the site raked clean. You come home to a finished yard, not a pile of work.

Common questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We'll come out, look at the tree or the lot, and give you an honest, upfront price before any work starts — no obligation.

Do you grind the stump too?

We do. Stump grinding is one of our core services — we take the stump down below grade so you can replant, mow over it, or build on the spot.

Absolutely. We regularly take on multi-tree jobs and full lot clearing — we've cleared upwards of 30 trees on a single property.

Do you clean up the debris?

Always. Logs, limbs and grindings get hauled off and the area raked clean. We don't leave you with a pile to deal with.
