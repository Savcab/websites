Good to know

Common questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out, look at the tree in person and give you a clear price with no charge and no pressure to book.

How big a job can you handle?

From a single limb to a whole property full of mature trees. With our bucket truck and dump trailer we regularly take on large multi-tree jobs that other crews turn down.

Do you clean up and haul off the wood?

Always. We clear the limbs and debris, rake up after ourselves and haul everything away. You won't be left with a pile to deal with.

Can you trim limbs over my roof or fence?

Yes — clearing limbs that hang over roofs, fences and outbuildings is some of our most common work. We take care to protect what's underneath.

Do you grind stumps too?

We do. After a removal we can grind the stump down below grade so you can reclaim that part of your yard.

What areas do you cover?

Borger and the surrounding Texas Panhandle — Fritch, Stinnett, Sanford, Skellytown, Pampa, Panhandle and the rest of Hutchinson County. Call if you're not sure you're in range.
