What We Do

From a challenging lot to a finished landscape

Aba Landscaping is a design-build crew — we handle the heavy grading and stonework most lawn services won't touch, then dress it in plantings, mulch and pine straw that hold up in North Georgia clay.

Landscape Design & Install

We plan the whole space, then build it — turning raw or overgrown ground into a backyard you actually use.

Stone Walkways & Patios

Natural flagstone paths, patios and steppers laid to last — the kind of stonework that makes a yard feel finished.

Retaining Walls

Stacked-stone and block walls that tame a slope, hold back the hillside and create usable, level ground.

Firepits & Outdoor Living

Stone firepits and seating areas that turn the space behind the house into somewhere you want to spend evenings.

Light Grading & Drainage

Reshape and grade the lot, build dry creek beds and gravel drainage so water runs where it should — not toward the house.

Planting, Mulch & Pine Straw

Trees and shrubs planted to thrive in hard Georgia clay, finished with fresh mulch and pine straw beds.

Tree Removal & Cleanup

Fallen and overgrown trees taken down, shaped and chipped — clearing the way before the real landscaping starts.

Good to Know

Questions homeowners ask

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. David comes out to your property, walks the space with you and gives you a fair, upfront price at no charge. You only move forward if the number works for you.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Full landscape design and installation, stone walkways and patios, retaining walls, firepits and outdoor living areas, light grading and drainage, planting, mulch and pine straw, and tree removal and cleanup — for both residential and commercial properties.

Do you work on steep or wooded lots?

Yes — that's much of what we do. North Georgia ground means slopes, drainage challenges and hard clay, and we plan grading, retaining walls and planting specifically for it.

Are you insured?

Yes. Aba Landscaping LLC is fully insured and works on both commercial and residential properties.
