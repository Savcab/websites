Anyone can set stone. Setting it so it still sits flat after ten New England winters — that's the whole job.

Able Landscapers is a Pepperell design-build crew that shapes the outside of a home in stone and brick — paver patios and walkways, cobblestone driveways, fieldstone walls, and outdoor fireplaces across northern Middlesex County and over the New Hampshire line. We treat the yard as one project: how the hardscape meets the house, where the water needs to go, and how it will look once it has weathered in.

Then we build it the slow way — excavation, a compacted base, the right pitch for drainage, and each stone set by hand. It's the part no reviewer photographs, and it's why a driveway that used to pool with every storm now sheds water clean, and why the patios stay flat season after season.

How we work

Designed first, then built to stay.

A straight path from the first look at your yard to the day you start using it.

Walk the property

We come out, read the grade and the drainage, and talk through what you're picturing for the space — patio, wall, driveway or repair.

Design & estimate

You get a clear written estimate — layout, materials and price laid out plainly, with no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons once we start.

Build on a real base

We excavate, compact the sub-base, set the pitch so water drains away from the house, and lay the stone and pavers by hand.

Clean and hand it back

We finish the edges, clean the site down, and leave you a patio, wall or driveway that's ready to use and built to stay put.

What we build

One crew, the whole hardscape.

Outdoor stone and brick, start to finish — from a single repair to a full patio, driveway and wall. The same people who quote your job are the ones who build it.
