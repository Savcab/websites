Good to know

Pressure washing questions, answered

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high water pressure and is right for hard surfaces like concrete driveways and walkways. Soft washing uses low pressure with a cleaning solution and is the safe choice for siding, roofs and wood, where high pressure can cause damage. We use whichever the surface calls for — and most homes need a mix of both.

Will washing damage my siding or roof shingles?

Not when it's done correctly. Delicate surfaces like vinyl siding and asphalt shingles get a gentle soft wash rather than high pressure, so the dirt, algae and black streaks come off without harming the material underneath.

How much does a house wash cost?

It depends on the size of the home and which surfaces you want cleaned. We give a clear, reasonable quote up front — reviewers regularly mention how fair and prompt our pricing is. Call (518) 212-2024 or send the quote form and we'll get you a number quickly.

Can you wash the whole exterior in one visit?

Yes — that's how most of our jobs go. Siding, roof, gutters, porch, deck and walkways can all be handled in a single appointment, each with the right method, so your whole property comes clean at once.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Hagaman and serve Gloversville, Amsterdam, Johnstown, Broadalbin, Mayfield, Perth, Fonda and the surrounding Fulton and Montgomery County communities. Not sure if you're in range? Just give us a call.

How often should I have my house washed?

For most homes in our area, once a year keeps siding, roofs and walkways ahead of the algae and mildew that build up in our humid summers and snowy winters. Homes under heavy tree cover or on the north side may benefit from a touch-up sooner.
