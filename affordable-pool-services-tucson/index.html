What we do

Seven Service Lines

Built and maintained pools across Tucson since 2004. Pick a line — or call and we'll sort the whole pool out.

Service 01

Weekly Pool Service

Scheduled weekly route: skim, brush and vacuum, empty baskets, test and balance chemistry, and check equipment so the water stays clear all season.

Service 02

Equipment Repair & Replacement

Pumps, filters, heaters, automation and plumbing — diagnosed and repaired, or swapped out when it's past saving. No guesswork.

Service 03

Solar Heating

Solar pool heating supplied and installed so you get a longer swim season on Tucson sun instead of a gas bill.

Service 04

Salt Water Conversions

Convert a chlorine pool to a salt-water system — softer water, lower upkeep, fewer chemical runs. Done end to end.

Service 05

Drain & Cleans

Full drain, acid wash or chlorine bath, and refill to clear out stains, scale and old water when a balance reset isn't enough.

Service 06

Pool Inspections

Buying or selling? A full inspection of structure, equipment and plumbing with a plain-English report on what's solid and what isn't.

Service 07

Tile Cleaning

Calcium and scale stripped off waterline tile so it looks like new — without scratching or etching the surface.
