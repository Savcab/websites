Why rock landscaping, here

El Paso's own water rules make the case for us.

El Paso Water assigns homes a fixed watering schedule by address — even-numbered addresses on Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday; odd-numbered on Wednesday, Friday or Sunday — with no watering on Monday, and daytime watering shut off from April through September. City code also caps traditional turf grass at half of a landscape's area.

That's less lawn than most homeowners plan for, and it's exactly what our rock and xeriscape work is built around.

Sources: El Paso Water · City of El Paso landscape code
