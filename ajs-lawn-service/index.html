Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

What does A.J.'s charge for mowing?

Every yard is a little different — size, slope and how often you want it cut all matter. The honest answer is we'll give you a clear price after we see it. Call us with your address and we'll set up a free, no-pressure estimate.

Do you do more than just mow?

Yes — that's a big part of what we do. Beyond regular mowing we handle grading, leveling, topsoil, reseeding and yard repair, including putting ground back together after a water-line fix. If a yard needs more than a cut, that's right in our wheelhouse.

How fast can you come out?

Often the next day. We keep hours seven days a week, so if the grass has gotten ahead of you or you've just bought a place that needs attention, give us a call and we'll get out to look as soon as we can.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in New Richmond and work the surrounding Clermont County area — Bethel, Amelia, Felicity, Moscow and nearby. Not sure you're on the route? Just call and ask; we'll tell you straight.

Can I get on a regular schedule?

Absolutely. Plenty of neighbors keep us on a weekly or biweekly rotation so they never have to think about the lawn. We also do one-time cuts and clean-ups whenever you need them.
