Good to know

Common questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Call or send the quote form and we'll walk the site, talk through materials, and give you a written estimate at no charge.

What kinds of masonry do you take on?

Stone and brick fireplaces, brick and paver patios, outdoor kitchens, retaining and seat walls, mailbox and entry columns, brick veneer, and German Schmear / whitewash. If it's brick or stone, ask.

Is the owner actually on the job?

Yes. All American Masonry is owner-operator Dallas with a small crew, so the person you talk to is the one laying the work.

What areas do you serve?

Cleburne and the surrounding Johnson County towns — Burleson, Joshua, Keene, Alvarado, Godley, Grandview — plus the south Fort Worth metro for the right project.

What does German Schmear do for my house?

It's a mortar wash troweled over existing brick to soften and lighten the look — an affordable way to give a dated exterior a fresh, custom feel without re-siding.
