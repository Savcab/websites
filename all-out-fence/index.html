Good to know

Common questions

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free. We’ll come out, look at the property, talk through your options, and give you a clear price with no pressure.

How fast can you repair a fence after a windstorm?

We move quickly on storm and wind damage. Call us, tell us what happened, and we’ll get out to assess it and onto the schedule as soon as we can — our crews are on the road by 6 AM.

What kinds of fence do you install?

Wood and privacy fencing, vinyl, steel and ornamental, and ranch-style post-and-rail or wire fencing — plus matching gates. Not sure what fits your yard? We’ll help you decide based on wind, slope, privacy and budget.

Will you repair a fence you didn’t build?

Yes. We repair leaning posts, blown-out panels and damaged gates on existing fences, whoever installed them — and we rebuild the weak spots to hold up better next time.

What areas do you serve?

Wellington, Fort Collins, and the surrounding Northern Colorado front-range communities including Timnath, Severance, Windsor and Loveland. If you’re close by, just ask.
