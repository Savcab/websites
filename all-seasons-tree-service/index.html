Good to know

Common questions

A few things people ask before booking tree work.

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. Call and tell us what you're dealing with, and we'll come look at it in person and give you an honest, no-cost estimate before any work starts.
Can you take down a big tree close to my house?
That's a lot of what we do. Trees too close to homes, fences or power lines are climbed and rigged down in controlled sections rather than felled — it's slower and safer, and it's how dangerous removals get done without damage.
Do you clean up afterward?
Always. Every job ends with the limbs, brush and debris hauled off and the ground raked down. We don't consider a job finished until your property is cleared.
Do you handle storm-damaged or fallen trees?
Yes. After a Blue Ridge storm we clear downed and split trees, free up driveways and access, and make hazardous situations safe. Call as soon as you can so we can get you on the schedule.
What areas do you cover?
We're based in Independence and work throughout Grayson County and the surrounding southwest Virginia mountain communities — Galax, Sparta, Elk Creek, Fries, Mouth of Wilson and nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Just call and ask.
How do I get on the schedule?
The fastest way is to call (276) 952-8228. Tell us about the tree or the job and we'll set up a time to come take a look.
