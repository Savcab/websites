Good to know

Fence questions, answered

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We come to your property, measure the run, talk through materials and gates, and give you a clear written price with no obligation and no pressure.
Which fence material is right for me?
It depends on your goal. Wood and vinyl give you full privacy; chain-link is the most budget-friendly way to enclose a yard or contain pets; ornamental aluminum looks sharp around front yards and pools. We'll walk you through the trade-offs during your estimate.
How are your fence posts set?
We dig each post hole to proper depth and set the posts in concrete footers. It's the part you never see, but it's what keeps a fence standing straight for years — so we don't cut corners on it.
Do you repair existing fences?
We do. Leaning sections, rotted or broken posts, storm damage and sagging gates are all common fixes — including re-setting footers in concrete so the repair lasts.
What areas do you cover?
We serve Hot Springs and the surrounding Garland County communities, including Hot Springs Village, Lake Hamilton, Royal, Pearcy and Mountain Pine. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call and we'll let you know.
