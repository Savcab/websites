Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us

Do you handle trees right next to my house or power lines?

Yes — tight-quarters removals are a big part of what we do. Trees between the house, fencing, a shed or a propane tank get taken down in controlled pieces, roped and lowered so nothing below is at risk.

How fast can you come out for a quote?

Often the same day you call. We'll look at the tree, talk through the plan, and give you a clear price — and we can frequently get the work itself scheduled as soon as the next day.

Will the price change once you start?

No. The number we quote is the number you pay. Even when a job turns out bigger than it first looked, we hold to what we agreed on.

Do you respond to storm damage and emergencies?

We do. When heavy wind and rain bring trees and limbs down, we take emergency calls and prioritize getting hazards off your home. Call (530) 559-1856 and tell us what's happening.

Do you clean up and haul everything away?

Yes. We remove the material and tidy the area before we leave — even on properties with limited access. You're left with an open, usable space, not a pile of debris.

Can you clear trees and brush for fire safety?

Absolutely — clearing and thinning around structures to improve defensible space is common work for us up here, and it often opens up the views at the same time.
