What we do

Ground-level work, top to bottom.

Two machines, one operator who runs them. Tell us what's in the way and we'll quote the right job — not a bigger one.

01

Stump Grinding

We grind stumps several inches below grade so there's no trip hazard and nothing sprouting back. One stump or twenty — we bring the grinder to it.

02

Tree Removal

Hazardous, dead, or just in the way. With a bucket truck on site we take trees down in sections, safe over roofs, driveways and fence lines.

03

Brush & Cleanup

Once the tree is down we don't leave a mess behind. Limbs, brush, and grindings cleaned up so the spot is ready to seed, mulch, or build on.
