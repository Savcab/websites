What We Build

One Team, Every Phase of the Yard

From the first planting plan to the last light fixture, Aloha ‘Aina handles the full design-build scope — no subcontractor hand-offs.

Landscape Design & Planting

Custom planting plans built for your yard’s light and soil — tropical, Japanese-style, xeriscape or screening.

Irrigation Systems

Custom drip, soaker and rotary systems on a timer, so your new landscape takes care of itself.

Lawn Installation

Hydroseed, plug, seed or sod — with full soil prep — matched to your budget and grass type.

Drain Construction

Drainage piping and graded swales that redirect standing water and runoff clear of your foundation.

Masonry Construction

Driveways, stone walls, CMU block, sidewalks, steps and foundations, built to last.

Fencing Design & Installation

Vinyl, redwood, chain-link and metal fencing in a style to match your home.

Landscape Lighting

Accent lighting to show off the finished yard at night, plus motion-sensor security lighting.

Grounds Maintenance & Yard Care

Ongoing upkeep — cutting, edging, weed control, pruning and seasonal feeding — on a schedule that fits your yard.
