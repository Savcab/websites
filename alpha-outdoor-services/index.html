Frequently asked questions

How do I get a price?

Estimates are free. Call or text (316) 559-6686, or fill out the form on this page with a little about your yard. We’ll take a look and give you a clear, no-pressure price before any work begins.

Do you offer regular mowing, or one-time visits?

Both. We can set you up on a weekly or every-other-week mowing schedule, or handle a one-time job like a bed cleanup, mulch refresh or seasonal cleanup. Whatever fits your yard.

Can you really clear an overgrown flower bed quickly?

Often, yes. With a hard-working crew we can clear a full bed of weeds in a single visit — one customer noted we cleared all of her flower beds in one morning and afternoon. We’ll give you a realistic timeline when we look at the job.

What areas do you serve?

Rose Hill and the surrounding southeast-Wichita-metro communities — Derby, Mulvane, Andover, Augusta, Haysville, Douglass and nearby. If you’re close by, give us a call and we’ll let you know.

How do I reach you?

Phone or text is easiest: (316) 559-6686. We’re generally reachable Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. Leave a message anytime and we’ll get back to you.
