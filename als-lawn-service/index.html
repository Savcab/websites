Frequently asked questions

How do I get a price for my lawn?

Just call us at (864) 992-4102 or send a quick request through the form below with your address and what you need. We'll look at the property and give you a straightforward, fair quote — no pressure.

Do you offer weekly recurring service?

Yes — weekly recurring maintenance is what we do best. Most of our customers start with a single cut and move to a regular weekly or bi-weekly schedule so their lawn always stays sharp.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Saluda, SC and serve the surrounding Saluda County area, including Batesburg-Leesville, Ridge Spring, Ward, Johnston, Prosperity, and Ninety Six. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call.

What are your hours?

We're out working Monday through Saturday, 7:00am to around 3:00pm (until 3:30pm on Fridays), and closed Sundays. The fastest way to reach us is a call or text to (864) 992-4102.

Besides mowing, what else do you do?

Along with regular mowing, we handle edging and trimming, pine-straw and mulch beds, and seasonal yard cleanups and leaf removal — everything that keeps a property looking cared-for.
