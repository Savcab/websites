The process, explained

What is hydroseeding?

Hydroseeding sprays a slurry of grass seed, mulch, fertilizer, and a tackifier binder directly onto prepared soil in one even pass. The mulch layer holds moisture and shields the seed while it germinates — which is why it's a fast, even way to establish a brand-new lawn from bare ground.

One recent client reported seeing grass emerge by day 8 — "It's day 8 and we officially have grass growing!!!" — Cassandra Burkum, Google review, 2024-05-26.
