The Studio

One team for the whole property — from the first grade to the final planting.

Most Long Island yards are handed off between a designer, a mason, and a lawn crew — and the seams show. Andrew Martone Landscape Design does the whole transformation under one roof: the design, the stonework, the sod, and the plantings that pull it together. Stoops and steps are set true, lawns go in clean, and beds are laid out to look right in every season.

The result is the kind of finished property you see below — designed, built, and maintained by the same hands.

What We Build

Services

Design-build, masonry, and grounds care for residential properties across Nassau County.

Landscape Design & Build

Full-property plans drawn and installed in-house — layout, grading, and structure that make the whole yard read as one design.

Masonry & Stonework

Stoops, steps, walkways, and leveling — set square and built to hold their line through Long Island winters.

Sod & New Lawns

Fresh sod and full lawn installs — graded, laid, and rooted for an even, dense lawn from the first week.

Plantings & Garden Beds

Layered beds, borders, and evergreen privacy screening — chosen to look right in bloom and hold structure off-season.

Tree & Shrub Removal

Overgrown, storm-damaged, or crowding growth taken out cleanly to open the yard up for what comes next.

Seasonal Maintenance

Ongoing care that keeps a finished property looking the way it did on day one, through the full growing season.
