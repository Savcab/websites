Good to know

Common questions

A few of the things homeowners ask us before a project. Have another? Just call.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come to your property, look at the site, talk through options, and give you a clear written quote at no cost and with no obligation.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job, but we keep our schedule moving — many homeowners are booked within a week or two of accepting the quote.

What kinds of concrete finishes do you offer?

Broom, smooth-trowel and decorative stamped finishes in a range of patterns and colors — we'll help you match the look to your home and budget.

Will my new concrete crack?

All concrete moves, which is why we prep a solid base, reinforce the slab, and cut control joints to guide where any hairline cracking goes. Proper prep is how we keep your surface looking good for the long haul.

Do you handle the dirt work and grading too?

We do. We grade and prep the base, build up retaining walls where a slope needs holding, and make sure everything drains the right way before anything is poured or set.

What areas do you serve?

Goshen and the surrounding Elkhart County communities — Elkhart, Middlebury, New Paris, Wakarusa, Nappanee, Bristol and nearby. If you're close, just ask.
