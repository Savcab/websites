Good to know

Common questions

Do you take on large or agricultural fencing jobs?

Yes — long perimeter runs, cross-fencing, field and high-tensile fence, and tall deer fencing on acreage are some of our favorite work. We’ve rebuilt full fence lines running thousands of feet, so the scale of the job isn’t a problem.

What fence materials do you install?

Wood (including cedar), vinyl, chain-link, aluminum and ornamental, plus agricultural wire, field and high-tensile fencing. We’ll talk through which material fits your property, your goal and your budget before we recommend anything.

Can you fix or rebuild an existing fence?

Absolutely. Whether it’s a few storm-damaged sections or an old fence that needs to come out and be rebuilt from scratch, we can repair to match what’s there or rebuild it better.

How do estimates work?

Estimates are free. We come out to the property, walk the line with you, discuss materials and layout, and then provide a clear written quote. Sharing a rough budget and timeline up front helps us tailor the right options for you.

What areas do you serve?

We work throughout the Eastern Panhandle of West Virginia — Martinsburg, Falling Waters, Inwood, Hedgesville and surrounding areas — and into nearby Washington County, Maryland, including Hagerstown and Williamsport. Not sure if you’re in range? Give us a call.
