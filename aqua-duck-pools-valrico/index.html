What we do

Everything your pool needs — and nothing it doesn't.

Take on part of the job or hand us the whole thing. Either way you spend less weekend time on chemistry and more of it in the water.

Weekly Pool Cleaning

Skim, brush, vacuum, empty baskets and balance the chemistry on a dependable weekly visit so the water stays clear and swim-ready.

Maintenance & Repairs

Pumps, filters, timers and the small fixes that keep a pool running — caught early on the weekly visit before they become big bills.

Tile Repair

Cracked or popped waterline tiles re-set and re-grouted so the edge of your pool looks finished, not patched.

Pool Painting

Tired, chalky interior surfaces prepped and repainted to bring back a clean, even finish and a pool that looks cared for.

Opening & Closing

Seasonal start-ups and shut-downs handled properly, plus a thorough initial clean-up to bring a neglected pool back to life.

One-Time Party Clean

A single deep clean before a party or special event — perfect when you just need the pool sparkling for one big weekend.
