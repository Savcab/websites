Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out, look at the tree, the access and any hazards, and give you a clear price before any work starts — no charge and no obligation.

Can you drop a tree near my house or fence?

That's our specialty. With proper rigging and rope work we section and lower trees in tight spots, so they come down exactly where we intend — clear of structures.

Do you handle emergencies after hours?

We do. If a tree comes down on your home, driveway or the road, call us any hour and we'll get there to clear the hazard and open access back up.

Will you clean up afterward?

Always. Limbs get chipped, wood is handled per what you'd like, and we rake the ground before we leave. A clean finish is part of the job, not an add-on.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes — we can grind the leftover stump down below grade so you can reclaim the spot, plant over it or lay sod without a trip hazard left behind.

What areas do you cover?

Baldwin, Cornelia, Demorest, Clarkesville, Alto, Mount Airy and the rest of Habersham County and nearby Northeast Georgia. Call if you're not sure you're in range.
