Questions homeowners ask

Do you really build from just a photo or an idea?

Yes — that's how a lot of our projects start. Bring a reference photo, a rough sketch, or just describe what you have in mind. We'll talk through what will actually work on your property and build it from there.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so we give you a clear estimate after seeing the space in person. The on-site estimate is free and there's no obligation — you'll know what it costs before any work begins.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Fire-pit and outdoor living areas, patios and walkways, land clearing and grading, tree and brush removal, fencing, and general property work. If it's outdoor and it needs building or clearing, ask us — we'll tell you straight if it's a fit.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in McAlpin and work throughout Suwannee County and the surrounding North Florida area — including Live Oak, Wellborn, Branford, O'Brien and Lake City. Call us if you're nearby and not sure.

Do you do the work yourselves?

We do. The same hands-on crew handles design, prep and the build — so the person who quotes your job is the one on site getting it done. Nothing gets lost in translation.
