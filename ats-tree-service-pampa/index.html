What we do

Full-service tree care for the Panhandle

From a single overgrown limb to a storm-snapped trunk over your roof, we handle the whole job — cut, haul, grind and clean up.

Tree Removal

Dead, dangerous or in the way — we take it down safely, even the big ones near houses and lines, then grind the stump if you want it gone.

Tree Trimming & Pruning

Clear deadwood, lift canopies off your roof, and shape trees so they grow strong and look right — cleanup included.

Stump Removal & Grinding

Grind the stump below grade so the spot blends back into your yard — no leftover hump, no tripping hazard.

Storm & Disaster Recovery

Tree down after a Panhandle storm? We respond fast, clear what fell on your home, fence or driveway, and haul it off.

Fence Removal & Replacement

Brush & Lot Cleanup

Limbs, brush piles and overgrowth hauled away so your property is left tidy — we don’t leave a mess behind.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you give free estimates?+

Yes — we come out, look at the job, and give you an honest, upfront price at no charge. No pressure either way.

How fast can you come out after a storm?+

For trees down on a house, fence or driveway we move as fast as we can — often same-day. Call us and we’ll tell you straight when we can be there.

Do you grind the stump and haul everything away?+

We can. Stump grinding and full cleanup — wood, limbs and brush hauled off — can all be part of the job. Just let us know what you want gone.

What areas do you cover?+

Pampa and the surrounding Texas Panhandle — Borger, White Deer, Skellytown, McLean, Lefors and nearby Gray County communities. Not sure if we reach you? Just call.

Can you take down a big tree close to my house?+

Yes. With proper rigging and a bucket truck we take down large and tricky trees near homes, fences and lines safely — that’s a big part of what we do.
