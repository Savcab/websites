A Front Range specialty

Synthetic turf that looks real and lasts

Water restrictions and clay soil make a perfect lawn hard to keep on the Front Range. Synthetic turf gives you a clean, even green surface — without the watering, mowing or muddy patches.

  • Properly compacted, free-draining base so it stays flat and dry
  • Great for back lawns, play areas, dog runs and shady spots
  • Pairs cleanly with paver patios and natural plantings
  • Cuts your outdoor water use and weekend yard work

Good to know

Common questions

Do you handle the whole project, or just part of it?

The whole thing. We take yards from demo through hardscaping, irrigation, sod or turf and plantings — all with our own crew, so you only deal with one team from start to finish.

What does synthetic turf cost to install?

It depends on the size of the area, the prep involved and the turf you choose. We'll measure your space and give you a clear, written estimate during a free visit — no obligation.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We come out, look at your space, talk through what you want and give you an honest scope and price before any work starts.

Which areas do you serve?

Dacono and the surrounding north Denver metro — Frederick, Firestone, Erie, Longmont, Mead, Brighton and nearby towns. Call us if you're not sure whether you're in range.

Can you replace an existing lawn with turf or new sod?

Absolutely. We can demo a tired old lawn and rebuild it with fresh sod or synthetic turf, including the grading and base work underneath so it holds up.
