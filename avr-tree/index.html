What we do

Tree work for Clearlake homes and lots.

Large Tree Removal

Hazardous, dead, and oversized trees taken down safely — including tall pines and big oaks tight against homes, fences, and power lines. Rigged and lowered in sections, then cleared away.

Trimming & Pruning

Crown thinning, deadwood, and clearance cuts that keep a tree healthy and your roof, driveway, and lines clear — shaped with care, not topped and butchered.

Stump Grinding

Grind the leftover stump down below grade so you get your yard — and your mower line — back, with the chips cleaned up or left to mulch, your call.

Storm & Lot Cleanup

Trees down across a drive, broken limbs after a storm, overgrown lots and tree lines — cut up, piled, and hauled so the property is safe and clear again.
