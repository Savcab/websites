Good to know

Questions Corpus Christi pool owners ask

How often should my pool be serviced?

For most Corpus Christi backyards, a weekly visit keeps the water balanced and clear — especially through the hot months and the salt-air season. We'll recommend a schedule that fits your pool and how much you use it.

My pool turned green — can you fix it?

Yes. Our green-pool rescue shocks the water, balances the start-up chemicals and gets it back to clear. Give us a call and we'll take a look.

Do you repair pumps, filters and heaters?

We do — diagnosis, repair, replacement and new installs on pumps, filters, heaters and salt systems. Equipment work is one of the things customers call us for most. If something isn't running right, we'll figure out why.

Are you certified and insured?

Yes. We're CPO-certified (a Certified Pool & Spa Operator), water-chemistry certified, a member of IPSSA, and insured — so the work is done to standard and you're protected.
