Who we are

A side job on weekends that grew into Gilbert’s go-to crew.

What began as weekend work is now a full-service yard maintenance and landscaping company serving Gilbert and the surrounding East Valley. The foundation hasn’t changed: integrity, discipline and a strong work ethic carried over from 12 years in law enforcement and the military.

Owner-operated by Charles and Brittany, Catalina stays small enough that an owner is on your job and quick enough to answer when something can’t wait. We’d rather build a lasting relationship and a yard that thrives than chase the next ticket — and our reviews say it’s working.
