A finished yard is the easy part to see. The design, grading and drainage underneath are what make it last.

AZ Modern Landscapes is a Prescott Valley design-build studio shaping front and back yards across Yavapai County's high country — paver patios, block and retaining walls, iron fencing, artificial turf and desert xeriscape. We start every project as a design problem: how the hardscape meets the house, how water moves across the lot, how it will read once the plantings settle in.

Then we build it the slow way — excavation, a compacted base, the right grade for drainage, walls set plumb and pavers laid by hand. It's the part no one photographs, and it's why the work still sits flat and true through the summer monsoons and the high-desert freeze.

How we work

Designed on site, built to last, left clean.

A clear path from the first walk of your lot to the day you start living in the yard.

Walk the lot

We come out, look at your space, talk through what you're picturing, and see how the hardscape, the grade and the drainage want to work together.

Design & estimate

You get a clear plan and a written estimate — layout, materials and price laid out up front, with no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons.

Build on a base

We excavate, compact the sub-base, set the grade for drainage, and lay pavers, block and turf by hand — the slow part, done right.

Hand it back

We finish the details, clean the site down, and leave you a yard that's ready to use and built to hold up in the high desert.
