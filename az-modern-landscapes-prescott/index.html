A yard should look designed — and stand up to the high desert. We handle both.

AZ Modern Landscapes is a design-build studio in Prescott Valley, planning and building paver patios, walls, iron fencing, artificial turf and xeriscape across the central Arizona high country. We treat every project as a design problem first — how the hardscape meets the house, how water moves off the lot, how it will read once the plants fill in.

Then we build it the right way: grading, a compacted base, drainage and irrigation, and stone, block and turf set by hand. It's the part no one photographs, and it's why the work still sits flat and looks sharp seasons later.

How we work

Designed, built, and a site left clean.

A straightforward path from the first look at your lot to the day you start using it.

Walk the lot

We come out, look at your yard, and talk through what you're picturing — how the space should flow, how it drains, and how it holds up in the desert.

Design & estimate

You get a clear plan and a written estimate — layout, materials and price laid out up front, no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons.

Build it right

We grade the site, set a compacted base, run drainage and irrigation, and lay the pavers, block, turf and iron by hand — the slow part, done right.

Hand it back

We finish the details, clean the site down, and leave you a yard that's ready to use and built to last through the seasons.

What we build

One crew, the whole yard.

Design through build, all in-house — from a single wall or turf lawn to a full front-and-back landscape. The same people who plan your project are the ones who build it.
