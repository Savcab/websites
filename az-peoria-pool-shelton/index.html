FAQ

Questions homeowners ask

How often should my pool be serviced?

In the Arizona heat, most pools do best with weekly service to keep chemistry balanced and water clear. We’ll recommend a schedule that fits your pool and how you use it.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Shelton Pool Service & Repair is licensed under Arizona ROC# 351128, bonded and insured, and CPO (Certified Pool Operator) certified.

Can you fix my pump, filter or heater?

Absolutely. We diagnose, repair and install pumps, filters, heaters, salt systems and automation — and we’ll always explain repair-vs-replace honestly before any work.

My pool turned green — can you help?

Yes. Our green-pool rescue shocks, clears and rebalances neglected or algae-filled pools back to swim-ready, then we can keep it that way on a maintenance plan.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Peoria, Surprise, Glendale, El Mirage, Sun City and Sun City West, plus the surrounding Northwest Valley. Call us to confirm your neighborhood.
