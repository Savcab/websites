What we do

One crew for the whole property.

From weekly maintenance to the irrigation main line under the turf, Oxygen Landscape covers the grounds work a commercial property needs to stay presentable year-round in the Sonoran heat.

Commercial Grounds Maintenance

Scheduled mowing, edging, bed care and seasonal cleanups that keep common areas, entries and turf looking sharp every visit.

Water-Smart Irrigation

Drip and spray systems designed to save water — helping properties cut waste and annual cost while keeping landscapes healthy through a desert summer.

Tree & Shrub Care

Trimming, shaping and health care that promotes the safety, structure and curb appeal of the trees and bushes across your site.

Sprinkler & Main-Line Repair

Complete sprinkler repairs and pressure main-line work — diagnosed and fixed in the trench so breaks don't sit and run up the water bill.
