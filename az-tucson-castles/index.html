Why desert landscaping

Beautiful in Tucson means built for the heat — and the water bill

Out here, a thirsty green lawn is a losing battle against the Sonoran sun. We build yards that look sharp year-round on a fraction of the water — so you spend less time and money keeping them alive.Out here, a thirsty lawn is a losing battle. We build Tucson yards that stay sharp year-round on a fraction of the water.

Drought-tolerant by design

Native and low-water Sonoran plants — cactus, agave, palo verde and desert spoon — set in gravel and decomposed granite, chosen to handle full Tucson sun.

Lower water bills

Trade thirsty turf for desert beds, rock and efficient drip irrigation with smart controllers. Your yard stops drinking through the meter every summer.

Low-maintenance, year-round

No weekly mow, no reseeding. A gravel-and-desert yard holds its look through every season — and the monsoon — with a quick seasonal cleanup.
