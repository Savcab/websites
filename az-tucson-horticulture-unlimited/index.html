A Tucson institution

Four decades of caring for the Sonoran Desert's grounds

Horticulture Unlimited has been part of the Tucson community since 1979, building an extensive portfolio of creative, high-quality landscape designs across the valley.

From HOA entryways and commercial common areas to residential desert gardens, our experienced, licensed team handles design, installation and ongoing maintenance — with certified arborists and irrigation specialists working in-house rather than subcontracted out.

It's the kind of depth a property manager or association board is looking for when they vet a vendor: a firm that has tended some of these grounds for the better part of two decades and knows exactly how Tucson's soil, monsoon and heat behave.

What we do

Design, build and care — under one roof

A full-service Tucson landscape firm: we design it, install it, and keep it healthy season after season.

Commercial & residential design

Custom landscape design for businesses, HOAs and homes — desert-adapted plantings, hardscape and outdoor living planned around how the space is really used.

Grounds & HOA maintenance

Scheduled commercial and residential maintenance — mowing, bed care, seasonal cleanups and common-area upkeep that keeps a property looking vetted and cared-for.

Certified arborist tree care

In-house certified arborists for pruning, health, removals and cleanups — including large clean-ups of trees, grounds and water-retention areas.

Irrigation & monitoring

Drip and irrigation design, installation and ongoing monitoring — tuned for Tucson's monsoon swings so plants thrive and water isn't wasted.

Native & desert installation

Native and low-water plantings, decomposed-granite yards and xeriscape — landscapes built to belong in the Sonoran Desert and hold up to the heat.

Pavers, patios & water features

Paver walkways and patios, river-rock swales, fountains and other hardscape that turn a desert yard into a usable, beautiful outdoor room.

Built for the desert

Water-smart landscapes that thrive in Tucson's heat

Designing for the Sonoran Desert is its own discipline. We build with native and low-water plantings, then keep the irrigation tuned and monitored so grounds stay healthy through monsoon and triple-digit summers alike.

Native plant palette

Desert-adapted trees, accents and ground cover chosen to belong here — not to fight the climate.

Monitored irrigation

Stonework & swales
