What we do

Full-service desert landscaping

From an empty lot or a tired yard to a finished outdoor space you actually live in — plus the upkeep that keeps it looking new.

01

Landscape design & build

Collaborative design through full installation — we plan the layout with you, then build it: grading, hardscape, plantings, lighting and the finish details.

  • Concept design
  • Full installation
  • Project management
02

Patios, pavers & outdoor living

Paver patios and walkways, curved seat walls, built-in BBQ islands, outdoor kitchens and fire features — the hardscape that makes a backyard a place to gather.

  • Paver patios
  • Outdoor kitchens
  • Fire pits & walls
03

Desert plantings & xeriscape

Low-water desert landscapes built for Tucson — decomposed granite and rock, cactus and agave, plus crisp artificial turf and putting greens that stay green.

  • Xeriscape
  • Artificial turf
  • DG & rock beds
04

Landscape maintenance

Keep your finished yard sharp year-round — seasonal cleanups, plant and tree care, and irrigation checks tuned to the desert's monsoon and heat cycles.

  • Seasonal cleanups
  • Plant & tree care
  • Irrigation checks
