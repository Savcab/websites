What we do

Full-service pool care for El Paso backyards

From the weekly skim to a full replaster, we handle every part of keeping your pool clean, balanced and running — one trusted crew you can call all year.

Weekly Cleaning & Maintenance

Skim, brush, vacuum, basket-empty and a full chemical balance on a set schedule — your pool stays swim-ready without you lifting a net.

Equipment Repair & Install

Pumps, filters, heaters and salt systems — we diagnose, repair or replace your equipment and get it dialed in right.

Green-Pool & Start-Up Cleanups

Neglected, cloudy or green? We shock it back, balance the start-up chemicals and bring the water back to clear.

Tile Cleaning & Acid Wash

Questions El Paso pool owners ask

How often should my pool be cleaned?

For most El Paso backyards, a weekly visit keeps the water balanced and clear, especially through the hot months and dust season. We'll recommend a schedule that fits your pool and how much you use it.

My pool turned green — can you fix it?

Yes. Our start-up and green-pool cleanups shock the water, balance the chemicals and get it back to clear. Give us a call and we'll take a look.

Do you repair pumps, filters and heaters?

We do — diagnosis, repair, replacement and new installs on pumps, filters, heaters and salt systems. If something's not running right, we'll figure out why.

Do you offer free estimates?

Always. Call or text (915) 219-9633, or email us, and we'll give you a straight quote with no pressure.

Can you replaster an older pool?

Yes — our finish arm, Majestic Pool Plastering, handles complete chipouts and fresh plaster with access to a range of materials for a brand-new surface.
