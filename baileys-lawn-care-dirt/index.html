Questions, answered

Do you do both lawn care and dirt work, or just one?

Both — that's the whole idea behind Bailey's. We keep lawns mowed and tidy week to week, and we also handle grading, excavation, retaining walls, drainage and landscape installs. One crew you can call for the mow on Tuesday and the wall next month.

How much does an estimate cost?

Estimates are free. We come out, look at the lawn or the project in person, and give you a clear price before any work starts. Call (270) 873-9550 or send the form and we'll set up a time.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Murray and serve Murray, Mayfield, Benton, Paducah and the surrounding Calloway County area. If you're nearby but not sure you're in range, give us a call and we'll let you know.

Are you insured?

Yes. Bailey's Lawn Care & Dirt Service is fully insured and owner-operated, with 22 years of work behind us in West Kentucky.

Can you set up recurring mowing?

Absolutely. We handle weekly and bi-weekly mowing for both homes and commercial properties, so your lawn stays on a steady schedule without you having to think about it.
