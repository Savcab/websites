We design every yard before we plant a single thing.

Barefoot Bradley's is a landscape design-build studio based in Wellington, serving Palm Beach County's equestrian estates and family yards alike. Bradley designs each yard personally — choosing the plants, stone and lighting so it works with the house and the light, not against it.

Then we install it ourselves: beds prepped and mulched, irrigation run to keep it healthy, lighting wired in, sod laid tight. Reviewers call Bradley a landscape artist for the way he layers color, texture and height — we just call it doing the design work first.

How we work

Design, then install, done right.

A straightforward path from the first look at your yard to the day it's finished.

Walk the yard

We come look at the space, talk through what you're picturing, and see how the plants, the light and the layout want to work together.

Design the plan

Bradley designs the layout — what goes where, what stays, what's new — before anything is ordered or dug.

Install it right

Beds are prepped and mulched, irrigation and lighting run, sod laid tight — installed by our own crew, not subcontracted out.

Hand it back

We finish the details, clean the site down, and leave you a yard that's ready to use.
