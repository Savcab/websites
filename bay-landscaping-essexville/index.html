The garden is what you see. What lasts is the design underneath it — and the nursery behind it, growing since 1948.

Bay Landscaping has been designing and building landscapes across the Great Lakes Bay Region since 1948. We take on the whole project — design, plantings, patios and walls, grading and drainage — for homes throughout Essexville, Bay City, Midland and Saginaw.

Much of what we plant is grown right here, on our own 70-plus-acre nursery in Essexville. That means we can match the right plant to your yard, stand behind the way it goes in the ground, and still be here to help years later.

How we work

Design, build, and grow it in.

A straightforward path from the first look at your yard to a landscape that's ready to grow into.

Walk your property

We come look at the space, talk through what you're picturing, and figure out what will grow and last in your yard.

Design & estimate

You get a plan and a clear written estimate — the layout, the plantings and the price laid out before we start.

Build it right

We install the plantings, hardscape and grading — much of it grown at our own nursery — set on a proper base by hand.

Grow it in

We finish the details, clean the site down, and leave you a landscape chosen to fill in and last for years.
