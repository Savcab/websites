What We Build

Ground-up outdoor work,
done to spec.

One crew for the whole job — we move the dirt, solve the water, clear the lot, and finish the ground. Every service below is work Bay Root does across Columbus and the Valley.

SERVICE 01

Landscape Design–Build

Full outdoor builds — planning the layout, planting beds and trees, and installing the hardscape that ties it together.

SERVICE 02

Grading & Site Prep

Shaping and leveling the ground so a lot drains right and builds right — pads, slopes, and rough-to-finish grade.

SERVICE 03

Sod Installation

Fresh, rolled sod laid over prepped ground for an instant, established lawn — front yards to full properties.

SERVICE 04

Drainage & Stormwater Control

Moving water off the property with graded swales, river-rock beds, and drainage lines that keep yards from washing out.

SERVICE 05

Brush & Tree Clearing

Clearing overgrowth, brush, and trees to open a lot up — the first step on cleanup, expansion, and new-build sites.
