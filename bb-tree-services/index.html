Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us

A tree just came down on my property — what do I do?

Stay clear of it, especially if it’s on or near power lines, and call us. We respond quickly to storm and emergency calls to make the property safe and clear the tree. If a line is involved, also report it to your utility.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. We’ll come look at the tree, talk through what it needs, and give you a free, up-front quote with no obligation to book.

Can you remove a tree right next to my house?

Yes — tight removals next to homes and cottages are a big part of what we do. We rig the tree down in sections, or bring the crane in when there’s no room to drop it, to protect the structure.

What happens to the brush and the stump?

We chip the brush on site and clean up the debris. Logs can be left cut where you want them, and we can grind the stump below grade so you can reclaim the spot.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Nobleboro and work throughout Midcoast Maine and Lincoln County — Damariscotta, Newcastle, Waldoboro, Jefferson, Bristol and the surrounding towns. Not sure if you’re in range? Just ask.

When are you available?

Our regular hours are Monday through Friday, 9 to 5. For storm and emergency situations, call — we do our best to respond quickly when a tree can’t wait for normal hours.
