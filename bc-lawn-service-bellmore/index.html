About B.C. Lawn Service

Bellmore-based, on Long Island for 30+ years.

B.C. Lawn Service Inc. has worked on Long Island for over three decades, run by owner Marc Bogen out of Bellmore. It runs as two sides of one business — a lawn-care crew and a masonry & paving crew — so the same company that maintains your lawn can also build the paver driveway, patio and stone steps around it.

Across their Google reviews, Bellmore homeowners point to the same things again and again: Marc walking them through the design and estimate before work starts, crews that arrive on time, and a job site left clean at the end of each day.
