What we do

One crew, from grading to the final planting.

BDS handles the full build in-house, so the design, the hardscape and the details all line up the way they were drawn.

Landscape Design & Installation

A plan for the whole yard — beds, grading, plant selection and layout — then installed by the same crew that drew it.

Hardscaping & Masonry

Stone and block work built to hold up to Georgia weather — walls, steps, columns and outdoor living features.

Paver Patios & Walkways

Patios, walkways and pool decks laid on a proper base so the surface stays level and true for years.

Retaining Walls

Engineered block and stone walls that hold a slope, level a yard and turn a grade problem into usable space.

Irrigation Systems

Sprinkler and drip systems zoned to your beds and lawn, so watering happens on schedule without the guesswork.

Drainage Solutions

French drains and grading that move water away from the house and keep the yard from turning to mud.
