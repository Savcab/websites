What we do

Full-service pool care, done right

From weekly cleanings that keep your water swim-ready to equipment repairs that save your summer, Bertino handles it all — and we’re system and equipment specialists when something breaks.

Weekly Pool Maintenance

Brush, vacuum, skim, water testing and chemical balancing on a dependable weekly schedule — your pool stays ready every day of the week.

Equipment Repair & Upgrades

Pumps, filters, heaters and salt systems diagnosed and repaired — or upgraded to run quieter, cleaner and more efficiently.

Filter Cleaning

Deep filter cleans and cartridge service that restore flow and clarity — the difference between a cloudy pool and a sparkling one.

Green Pool Rescue

Neglected, algae-green water brought back to clear and swimmable — we tackle the worst pools the Inland Empire heat can create.

Tile & Acid Wash

Calcium-scaled tile lines and stained surfaces cleaned and acid-washed back to a fresh, like-new finish.

One-Time Service & Inspections

Buying or selling a home, or just need a one-off clean? We’ll get the pool inspected, serviced and ready — no contract required.
