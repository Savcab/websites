What we do

Complete tree work, start to clean finish

From a single overgrown pine to a whole lot full of them — here's how we help around Sagle and Bonner County.

Tree Removal

Safe, controlled takedowns of dead, dying, leaning, or in-the-way trees — including the tall conifers in tight spots near homes, outbuildings, and power lines. We crane it down, haul the wood, and leave the area clear.

Our most-requested job

Trimming & Pruning

Crown thinning, deadwood removal, and shaping that keeps your trees healthy, balanced, and clear of the roofline. Pruned with the tree's long-term health in mind, not just hacked back.

Healthier, safer trees

Stump Grinding

That leftover stump ground down below grade so you can mow over it, replant, or just be rid of the trip hazard. We grind, rake the chips, and tidy the spot.

No more eyesore

24/7 Emergency & Storm Cleanup

After North Idaho wind and snow, we clear fallen trees and hanging limbs off houses, driveways, and fences and get your property safe again. Day or night — call us and we'll get to you.

Around-the-clock response

Lot & Land Clearing

Clearing trees and brush for a new build, a driveway, a defensible-space buffer, or just to open up the view. We take down what needs to go and haul it off, acreage included.

Crane & Logging Removal

The big stuff — large-diameter conifers, hazard trees, and timber other crews can't reach. Our crane and grapple loader handle weight and height safely, even in snow.

Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We'll come out, look at the tree, and give you an honest, up-front price before any work begins — no obligation. Call or text us a photo to get started faster.

Do you handle the really big trees?

That's our specialty. We run a crane, bucket truck, and grapple loader, so the tall conifers up here — even ones leaning over a house, outbuilding, or power line — can come down in a controlled way without tearing up your property.

Can you come out for a storm emergency?

Yes — we run emergency response 24/7. When a tree or large limb comes down on your home, driveway, or outbuilding after North Idaho wind or snow, call us any time and we'll prioritize getting your property safe and clear.

Do you grind the stump and clean up afterward?

Cleanup is part of the job. We haul off the wood and rake the area, and we'll grind the stump down below grade whenever you'd like that done. We aim to leave your yard tidier than we found it.

Do you clear lots and acreage?

We do. Whether it's a few trees for a new build or driveway, brush and trees for defensible space, or a whole lot full of timber, we'll scope it, quote it honestly, and haul it off.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Sagle on US-95 and serve Bonner County and the surrounding North Idaho area — Sandpoint, Ponderay, Dover, Kootenai, Cocolalla, Priest River and beyond. Not sure if you're in range? Just give us a call.
