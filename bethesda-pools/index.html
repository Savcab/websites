What We Do

Complete pool care for the desert

From a standing weekly route to an emergency green-pool rescue, Bethesda Pools handles every part of keeping your water clean, balanced and swim-ready in the Nevada heat.

Most Popular

Weekly Cleaning & Maintenance Plans

A dependable weekly route: skim, brush, vacuum, empty baskets, test & balance chemistry, and a full equipment check — so your pool is always ready and you never think about it.

Equipment Repair & Install

Pumps, filters, heaters and salt systems diagnosed, repaired or replaced. We get failing equipment running again and recommend upgrades only when they truly pay off.

Green-Pool Rescue

Murky, green or neglected pool? We shock, filter, balance and clear it back to swim-ready blue — then keep it that way with a maintenance plan if you want it handled for good.

Acid Wash, Calcium & Tile Cleaning

Stained plaster, heavy calcium scale on the tile line and waterline buildup removed with a professional acid wash and bead-blast tile cleaning that brings the finish back to new.

Chemistry & Salt Systems

Precise water balancing and salt-cell service to protect your plaster and equipment and keep the water gentle on skin and eyes — no guesswork, no algae blooms in the heat.

Drain, Clean & Refill

When water gets too old or hard to balance, we drain, deep-clean the surfaces and refill — resetting your pool for a fresh, easy-to-maintain season.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What's included in weekly pool service?

Every visit we skim the surface, brush walls and steps, vacuum, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, test and balance your water chemistry, and check that your equipment is running right.

Do you fix pumps, filters and heaters?

Yes. We diagnose and repair or replace pumps, filters, heaters and salt systems. We'll tell you honestly whether a repair makes sense or a replacement is the smarter long-term move.

My pool has turned green — can you save it?

Almost always. We shock, filter and rebalance neglected and green pools back to clear, swim-ready water, then keep it that way on a maintenance plan if you'd like it handled going forward.

Do you handle the heavy calcium on my tile?

Yes — hard Vegas water leaves calcium scale on the tile line. We professionally clean the tile and can acid-wash stained plaster to bring the whole finish back to looking new.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Henderson and service pools across the Las Vegas valley — Green Valley, Anthem, Seven Hills, Las Vegas, Paradise, Boulder City and the surrounding communities.
