How we work

Design it, build it, keep it developing.

A straightforward path from the first look at your yard to the season after this one.

Walk the site & plan

We come look at the space, talk through what you're picturing, and figure out how the patio, water feature or rock work fits the yard.

Design the layout

We lay out the patio, beds and paths so the pieces work together now and leave room for what you add later.

Build on a proper base

Pavers, flagstone and boulders go in on a base that's graded and compacted to hold up through Wyoming winters.

Develop it season by season

Most yards aren't a single project. We come back to add the next piece when you're ready for it.
