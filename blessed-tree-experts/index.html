Good to know

Common questions

Do you clear large acreage and overgrown lots?

Yes — large-lot and acreage land clearing is what we’re built for. We bring a grapple truck, chipper, and track loader to handle cedar, brush, and undergrowth that’s too much for a saw-and-trailer crew, and we haul the debris off so the land is left usable.

How fast can you get the job done?

It depends on the size and condition of the property, but the right equipment lets us move quickly — one customer’s 8-acre job that “would normally take far longer” was done in a single day. We’ll give you an honest timeline when we quote it.

Do you handle emergency and storm-damaged trees?

We do. If a tree comes down on your property or storm damage leaves something unsafe, call anytime — we respond around the clock to clear what’s blocking access or hanging dangerously.

Do you grind stumps and clean up afterward?

Yes. We grind stumps below grade so you can mow, build, or replant, and we chip and haul the brush and limbs — we don’t leave piles behind. A cleaned-up site is part of the job.
