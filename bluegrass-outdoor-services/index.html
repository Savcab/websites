What we do

Property care that keeps up with every Minnesota season.

A straightforward list of the work we actually do — the mowing, feeding and cleanup that keeps south-metro lawns sharp, plus the plowing that keeps commercial lots and drives open all winter.

Weekly Mowing

Weekly mowing, edging and trimming that keeps your lawn striped and even from the first cut of spring through the last of fall.

Spring & Fall Cleanup

Full spring and fall cleanups — leaves, debris and beds reset — so your property starts and ends the season looking its best.

Fertilization

A seasonal fertilization program built for Minnesota lawns, timed to feed the grass when it actually needs it.

Dethatching

Power dethatching to clear the built-up layer choking your lawn’s roots, so water, air and nutrients can actually reach them.

Weed Control

Targeted weed control that keeps dandelions and crabgrass out without stressing the rest of your lawn.

Commercial Snow & Ice Removal

Parking lots, drives and walkways plowed and treated all winter, per-storm or on a full-season contract. See winter services →
