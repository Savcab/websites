Questions we hear a lot

Do you take down big trees?

Yes — removal is what we're known for. Oversized mulberries, tall pines, dead or leaning trees, and trunks crowding a house all get taken down in a controlled, piece-by-piece way, with the wood and debris hauled off afterward.

Can you bring an overgrown tree back to size?

That's one of our favorite jobs. We reduce and reshape trees that have taken over a yard so they look right and stay healthy — one Camp Verde customer had three overgrown mulberries transformed in a single visit.

How fast can you come out?

We're a local Camp Verde crew, so most quotes happen quickly — often within a few days. For storm damage or a hazardous tree, call us and we'll do our best to prioritize getting out to make it safe.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the tree and the access, and give you an honest price at no cost — with no obligation to book the work.

Do you grind the stump and clean up after?

We can grind the stump down well below ground level so there's no regrowth or tripping hazard, and we chip branches and haul off the wood and debris so your yard is left clean.

What areas do you cover?

Camp Verde and the wider Verde Valley — Cottonwood, Clarkdale, Cornville, Rimrock, Lake Montezuma and the towns nearby. If you're close and unsure, just give us a call.
