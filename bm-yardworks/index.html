What We Do

Full-service irrigation, start to finish

From a brand-new system to a stubborn leak in a 20-year-old one, we handle every part of keeping your landscape watered the right way.

New System Install

Custom-designed sprinkler and drip systems laid out for full, even coverage and water efficiency — built right the first time.

Most Requested

Diagnostics & Repair

Zones not firing, heads spraying the driveway, mystery wet spots? We track the real problem down and fix it — instead of pushing a full replacement.

Backflow & Valves

Backflow preventer install and testing, valve and manifold work, and pressure issues handled to keep your system safe and code-correct.

Smart Controllers

2-wire and multi-wire controller upgrades and wiring repair, including Wi-Fi smart timers that water on a schedule that actually fits your yard.

Spring Start-Up & Winterization

Seasonal turn-ons, full system blow-outs before the freeze, and tune-ups that catch small problems before they become expensive ones.

Sod & Landscape

Fresh sod, rock beds, and clean planting work to round out the yard — so your new lawn and the system that feeds it go in together.
