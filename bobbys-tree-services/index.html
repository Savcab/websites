Frequently asked questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We'll come out, look at the tree or stump, and give you a clear, no-pressure price before any work starts. There's no charge to get a quote.

Do you clean up afterward?

Always. Hauling off the debris and leaving your yard tidy is part of every job — it's one of the things our customers mention most in their reviews.

How soon can you come out?

It depends on the season and how full the schedule is, but we work to get to you quickly — especially for hazardous trees or storm damage. Call and we'll find a time.

Can you remove a large or tricky tree?

Removals are what we're best known for, including big trees and ones in tight spots near homes, fences and power lines. Tell us what you're dealing with and we'll handle it safely.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes — we can grind the stump below grade so you get your yard back with nothing left to mow around. Just let us know you'd like the stump handled when you call.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Geneseo and cover Henry County and the surrounding Quad Cities area, including Atkinson, Annawan, Cambridge, Colona and Kewanee. Not sure? Give us a call.
