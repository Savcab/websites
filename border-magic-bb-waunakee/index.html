What is Border Magic?

A continuous concrete border, shaped to your landscape on-site.

Instead of laying brick by brick or stone by stone, we machine-extrude one solid concrete edge that follows every curve of your beds. It's then colored and stamped to look like stacked stone, brick or cobble — without the joints that let pavers shift, separate and let weeds and mulch creep through.

  • No gaps to shift or scatter. One seamless pour means nothing to pop loose, sink unevenly or wash out over the seasons.
  • Cleaner beds, less work. A raised lip holds mulch in and keeps grass out, so trimming and edging takes minutes.
  • Made to match your home. Choose the color and stone pattern on-site from real samples — you see exactly what you're getting.

FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

How is this different from brick or plastic edging?

Border Magic is a single continuous concrete edge poured on-site — there are no joints or sections to shift, separate, heave or rot. Brick and pavers settle unevenly over time and plastic edging works its way loose; one solid border doesn't.

Can I choose the color and pattern?

Yes. At your free estimate you'll see real color samples and stone patterns — grey stacked stone, terracotta, sandstone, brick red and more — so you pick exactly what suits your home before we start.

How long does installation take?

Most residential projects are installed in a single day. We extrude, shape, color and stamp the border in one continuous run, then let it cure.

Do you serve my town?

We're based in Waunakee and cover Dane and Columbia counties — Madison, Middleton, Sun Prairie, DeForest, Verona, Portage and the surrounding area. If you're nearby, just ask.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the length and layout of your beds, so we quote each project after seeing it. The on-site estimate is always free and comes with pricing laid out in an easy-to-read way.
