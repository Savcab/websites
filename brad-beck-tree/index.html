Good to know

Common questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Tell us what you need over the phone, and in most cases we'll come look at the job and give you a clear, fair price before any work starts — no pressure.

A tree just fell or is leaning — can you come fast?

Call us right away. We're known for responding quickly to storm-damaged and leaning trees — one homeowner had us out within minutes of a snapped Douglas fir. The sooner you call, the sooner we can make it safe.

Can you remove a tree close to my house?

Yes — that's a big part of what we do. Using proper climbing and rigging, we take down trees right next to homes, fences, and power lines in a controlled, safe, and clean way.

Do you handle defensible-space and fire clearing?

We do. We're familiar with Cal Fire standards and regularly help homeowners clear brush, reduce ladder fuels, and tidy up to protect their property — often as part of a larger tree job.

Do you clean up and haul away the wood?

Always. Cleanup is part of the job — we cut and move the wood, haul the debris, and leave your yard tidy. Customers regularly mention how clean we leave the property.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Willits and work throughout inland Mendocino County — Brooktrails, Redwood Valley, Ukiah, Laytonville and the surrounding communities. Call and we'll let you know.
