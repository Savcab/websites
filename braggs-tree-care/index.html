Good to Know

Tree Service Questions, Answered

Do you handle large or dangerous removals near the house?

Yes — that's a big part of what we do. With crane and tracked-lift access we can take down trees over roofs, driveways and shorelines in controlled sections, so there's nothing left to chance even in tight spots.

Do you clean up and haul everything away?

We do. Brush is chipped on-site, the wood is handled, and stumps can be ground out — the job isn't finished until your yard is left tidy. One of our reviewers had us remove and chip 10 trees and grind 15 stumps in a single visit.

Can you come out after a storm?

Absolutely. Storm damage is one of the most common reasons people call us. Reach out anytime and we'll respond promptly with a clear, reasonable estimate to get downed and broken trees cleared safely.

How much does tree work cost?

It depends on the size of the tree, the access, and the hazards involved — which is exactly why estimates are free. Call or text us a few photos and we'll give you an up-front price before any work begins.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Appleton and work throughout the surrounding Midcoast — towns like Searsmont, Union, Hope, Camden, Rockport, Lincolnville and the broader Knox and Waldo County area. If you're nearby and not listed, just ask.
