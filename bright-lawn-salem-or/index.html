What we do

From bare ground to finished yard — one crew, start to finish.

Hardscape, construction and ongoing care, all handled in-house. Here's what Bright Lawn builds and maintains across the mid-Willamette Valley.

Paver patios & walkways

Interlocking paver patios, walkways and driveways laid on a proper compacted base so they stay level and true for years.

Retaining walls

Block, timber and stone retaining walls that hold back slopes, carve out usable yard and stand up to Oregon rain.

Wood fencing & decks

Cedar privacy fencing and wood decks built to line, level and last — clean posts, tight boards, square corners.

Landscape & lawn construction

Full yard builds — grading, hardscaping, planting beds and concrete driveways & walkways designed to work together.

Sod installation

Graded, prepped and rolled sod that gives you an established, even lawn in a day instead of a season of seed.

Lawn & garden maintenance

Mowing, edging, bed care and garden & yard work that keep the whole property looking sharp week after week.
