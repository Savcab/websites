Good to know

Stump grinding questions, answered

What's the difference between stump grinding and stump removal?

Grinding chews the stump down into chips, several inches below ground level, so grass grows over it and you can use the spot again. Full removal digs out the entire root ball and leaves a big hole. For almost every yard, grinding is faster, cheaper, and less of a mess — and it's all we do.

Do you only work for homeowners?

No. About half of what we do is grinding stumps for other tree services, landscapers, and lot-clearing contractors who'd rather sub it out than tie up a crew. Homeowners and pros both call the same number.

Will grinding tear up my lawn?

We work to avoid it. Customers with zoysia and other delicate grass have noted we're careful not to tear up the surrounding yard. We grind the stump, clean up, and leave the area as tidy as the job allows.

How fast can you come out?

It depends on the schedule, but several reviewers mention same-day service — called in the morning, ground out by the afternoon. Call and we'll tell you the soonest we can get to you.

What happens to the wood chips?

Grinding leaves a pile of chips. We can rake them back into the hole to settle and break down, or clean them off the spot — whatever you'd prefer. Just let us know.

How do I get a price?

Call (985) 974-9896 and tell us how many stumps, roughly how big, and where they are. A photo by text helps us quote it.
