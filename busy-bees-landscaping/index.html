Good to know

Lawn care questions, answered

A few of the things Tooele Valley homeowners ask us most.

Do you offer recurring service or just one-time visits?

Both. Most customers go with a recurring seasonal plan so their lawn stays on a steady schedule, but we're happy to do one-time mows or cleanups too.

How do I get a price?

Just call (435) 850-9415 or send the quick form on this page. We'll look at your yard and give you a clear, no-pressure estimate — there's no charge to ask.

My lawn is in rough shape — can you help?

Very likely. We've brought back lawns that were nearly lost and treated grub and weed problems for years. Tell us what's going on and we'll put together a plan to nurse it back.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Erda and serve Tooele, Grantsville, Stansbury Park, Lake Point and the surrounding Tooele County area. Nearby but not listed? Give us a call.

When's the best time to start a fertilization program?

Spring green-up is ideal, but a good program runs through the whole season. The sooner we start, the sooner the lawn thickens up — so it's worth a call any time.

What are your hours?

We're reachable Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Leave a message any time and we'll get right back to you.
