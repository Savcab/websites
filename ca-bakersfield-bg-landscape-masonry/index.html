Design-build, start to finish

One local crew for the whole transformation.

Bobby G. started BG Landscape & Masonry in Bakersfield back in 2007, and the work has spoken for itself ever since. We take backyards that "literally started as just dirt" and turn them into finished outdoor living spaces — grass, concrete, retaining walls, pavers and the masonry features most landscapers sub out.

Because we self-perform the masonry and the landscape, you deal with one accountable crew from the first estimate to the final clean-up — no juggling subcontractors, no surprises. It's craft-built work, priced fair, for homeowners and businesses across Kern County.
