Our story

A Bakersfield name in landscaping for 35+ years

Penney Lawn Service has cared for Bakersfield's lawns, estates and commercial grounds since the 1980s. Owner Dan Penney built the business on a simple idea his customers still talk about: that the landscaping of your property is the first thing clients and visitors notice — so it had better be done right.

"You only have one shot at that critical first impression. Make it a great one."

From a complete design-and-install to keeping a property sharp all year, we work with each client on a long-term plan, install quality plants and materials, and hold every job to a high standard of workmanship. Same name, same valley, same standard — for over three decades.
