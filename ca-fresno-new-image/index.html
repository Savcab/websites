Design-build, start to finish

One crew, one vision — from concept to the finished reveal

New Image Landscaping is a Fresno design-build landscaping company. We don’t just mow and go — we plan the whole yard, render it in 3D so you can see it before we build, and then transform it: grading, planting beds, lawns, hardscape and irrigation, all by the same team that drew it.

Homeowners come to us with bare dirt, tired front yards and blank back yards. We listen first, design around how your family actually lives outside, and hand it back finished. That care is why our Google reviews read the way they do.
