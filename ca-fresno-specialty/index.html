How we work

Designed, built, and left clean.

A straightforward path from the first look at your yard to the day you start using it.

Walk your yard

We come look at the space, talk through what you're picturing, and figure out how the patio, the turf and the drainage want to work together.

Design & estimate

You get a clear written estimate — materials, layout and price laid out up front, with no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons once we start.

Build on a base

We grade for drainage, compact a proper sub-base, and set the concrete, pavers and turf by hand — the slow part, done right for valley soil.

Hand it back clean

We finish the details, haul off the debris, and leave you a yard that's ready to use the day we pull out of the driveway.
