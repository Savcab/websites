Who we are

Three decades of steady, hands-on landscape work

JG Becerra Landscape Management has been enhancing commercial and residential landscapes since 1995. Led by Jose Becerra, we handle the full arc of a property — from a fresh front-yard makeover to the ongoing grounds care an HOA board can count on.

We keep it straightforward: real crews, real follow-through, and the same attention on a weekly maintenance route as on a paver-and-planting build. That consistency is why property managers and homeowners across Petaluma, Marin and Sonoma keep us on year after year.
