03 — Who we are

A licensed Riverside crew, owned by Freddie Paz.

Paz Landscape & Concrete is a local, owner-run firm. For more than twenty years, Freddie Paz and his seasoned crew have transformed the exterior environments of residential and commercial properties throughout the Inland Empire — from full landscape renovations to the concrete and masonry that anchors them.

Our designer listens to your ideas first, then builds a clear plan of action. Because we hold our own C-27 license and run our own crew, the team that draws your project is the team that pours it — on time, organized, and accountable from the first visit to the final walk-through.
