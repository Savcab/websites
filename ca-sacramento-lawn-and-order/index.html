What we do

One Sacramento crew for the whole property

Lawn & Order started as a commercial maintenance company in 1988 and grew into a full-service landscape firm — design, installation, tree care and ongoing grounds maintenance, all handled in-house.

Landscape Design & Installation

Complete outdoor builds — from concept to the finished yard you picture, including drought-conscious xeriscape conversions for our long, dry valley summers.

  • Paver patios & walkways
  • Retaining walls
  • BBQ islands & outdoor kitchens
  • Wood fences
  • Custom ponds
  • Xeriscape

Tree Care & Certified Arborist

An ISA-certified arborist on staff guides every job — from routine pruning to hazardous-tree evaluations and deep-root health programs.

  • Trimming & pruning
  • Removal & stump grinding
  • Deep-root fertilization
  • Insect & disease management
  • Hazard evaluations

Commercial Grounds Maintenance

Dependable, scheduled site care for apartments, office parks and shopping centers — one accountable contractor for boards and property managers.

  • Mowing & edging
  • Seasonal cleanups
  • Shrub & bed care
  • Multi-family & HOA
  • Office & retail sites

Irrigation & Drainage

Full-service irrigation, drainage and excavation that keeps water where it belongs — efficient systems built for water-restriction-conscious Sacramento.

  • System install & repair
  • Drainage & grading
  • Excavation
  • Water-efficient upgrades
