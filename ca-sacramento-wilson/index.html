Welcome to Wilson Landscaping

A trusted partner for your yard — start to finish

We transform Sacramento outdoor spaces into yards that feel calm, cared-for, and built to last. From the first sketch to the final cleanup, every detail carries our eye for natural beauty and clean, lasting work.

Homeowners come to us with a rough idea — sometimes just a sketch on paper — and we help shape it into a plan that fits the property, the Central Valley climate, and the budget. Renovations, curbing, turf, irrigation, drought-tolerant planting: one team that handles the whole transformation.

What we do

Services we provide

A full range of residential landscaping for Sacramento homes — designed, installed, and maintained by one accountable team.

Landscape Renovations

Full front- and back-yard redesigns — grading, planting, pathways, and a complete refresh that brings tired yards back to life.

Custom Decorative Curbing

Stamped and colored concrete curbing that frames beds and lawns with crisp, durable borders built to handle Sacramento summers.

Artificial Turf & Putting Greens

Low-water, always-green turf and custom putting greens — clean edges, proper drainage, and a surface that holds up year-round.

Sprinklers & Drip Systems

New irrigation, drip conversions, and sprinkler repairs that keep your landscape healthy while cutting wasted water.

Drought-Tolerant Landscapes

Water-wise gardens with succulents, native plants, rock, and flagstone — beautiful, low-maintenance, and built for our dry summers.

Yard Maintenance & Cleanups

Mowing, shrub trimming, seasonal cleanups, and the regular care that keeps a finished yard looking sharp through every season.
