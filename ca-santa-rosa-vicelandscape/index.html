Vice Landscape Management has been shaping Santa Rosa's outdoor spaces since 1989. Under owner Scott Vice — an award-winning contractor recognized by the California Landscape Contractors Association — we design and build residential landscapes from the ground up, from new construction to full renovations.

It's one studio for the whole project: design, hardscape, water features, irrigation and planting. The people who draw your plan are the same ones who build it — which is how the finished yard ends up even better than the sketch.

How we work

From the first walkthrough to the reveal.

One studio handles design and build, so nothing gets lost in the handoff between a designer and a crew.

Design consult

We walk the property with you, talk through how you want to live outside, and look at the grade, sun and drainage.

Design & estimate

You get a plan and a clear written estimate — layout, materials and price laid out before any work starts.

Build it right

One crew handles the whole build: grading, base, hardscape, water features, irrigation and planting, done the proper way.

The reveal

We finish the details, clean the site down, and hand back a yard that's ready to live in for years to come.

What we do

Design and build, under one roof.
