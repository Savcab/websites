Our Story

Four decades of craft, now in its second generation.

Founded in 1978 by Harry and Deborah North, Creative Environments has grown from a small, family-run operation into one of the North Bay’s enduring landscape contracting firms — bringing beauty, function, and lasting value to residential landscapes across the region for more than four decades.

In 2024, the company entered a new chapter as Lindsey North — daughter of the founders, a licensed C-27 contractor and experienced landscape designer — took ownership. Her leadership continues a legacy built by a deep bench of designers, masons, carpenters, welders, and seasoned foremen, many of whom have been with the firm for more than 25 years.

How We Work

Concept through construction — and beyond.

A single team carries your project from the first site walk to the final planting, then keeps it thriving for years.

01

Consultation

We walk the site, listen to how you want to live outdoors, and assess grade, drainage, and existing plantings.

02

Design

Our designers translate the vision into a buildable plan — layout, materials, planting, and hardscape detail.
