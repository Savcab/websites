Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom pool?

Every project is different, but most custom inground pools move through consultation, design, and construction over several weeks. Because Robert and J'men personally supervise each phase, you'll always have a clear picture of where your project stands.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. 20th Century Pools is a licensed California pool and spa contractor — CSLB License #700700 — and we are fully insured.

Do you remodel existing pools, or only build new ones?

Both. Alongside new construction, we resurface and remodel existing pools — including our Mini Pebble interior finishes, new lighting, waterfalls, beach entries, and hardscape upgrades.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Temecula, Murrieta, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Menifee, and the surrounding Riverside County communities. Call us to confirm we cover your neighborhood.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (951) 757-2347 or request a free design consultation through the form below. All of our estimating is provided in writing for your protection.
