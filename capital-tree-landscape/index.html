Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to remove a tree?

It depends on the tree's size, location, and how close it is to the house or power lines. We give a free, no-obligation estimate up front — and our customers consistently tell us our bids come in below other companies.

How fast can you come out?

We're known for getting out quickly for the bid and getting jobs on the schedule fast. Call us with the details and we'll let you know our soonest availability.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes. We grind stumps down below grade so you can reclaim the lawn and mow right over the spot. We can do it as part of a removal or as a standalone job.

Do you clean up the debris?

Always. Limbs, brush, and debris are hauled away and the site is left tidy — cleanup is part of the job, not an extra you have to ask for.

Can you handle large or hard-to-reach trees?

Yes — large-tree removal is what we do most. With bucket lifts and proper rigging, we safely take down big trees, including ones leaning over a house or in tight spots.

What areas do you serve?

Cape Girardeau and the surrounding Cape Girardeau County area, including Jackson and Scott City. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call to check.
