Our story

A local crew you can count on, year after year

We're a locally run lawn care and tree service based right here in Ruckersville. What started as mowing for a few neighbors has grown, by word of mouth, into full-property care across Greene County and the Charlottesville area.

The reason is simple: we treat every yard like it's our own, we show up when we say we will, and we do the work right. A lot of the homes we care for, we've been looking after for well over a decade — and that repeat trust is something we're proud of.

From a weekly mow to bluestone steps and stump grinding, you get one dependable crew for the whole property. No chasing down three different contractors.
