What we do

Honest yard work, start to finish

We're a hands-on landscaping crew. Whether it's a weekly cut or reclaiming a corner that's gone to weeds and rock, we do the work ourselves and we don't disappear halfway through.

Lawn mowing & maintenance

Clean stripe-cut lawns on a schedule that fits you — mowing, string trimming, edging and blow-down so the whole yard looks finished, not just mowed.

Mulch beds & design

Fresh-cut beds, crisp edges and rich mulch around the front and sides of your home — reshaping tired beds into clean lines that make the whole house look cared for.

Grading & land reclaiming

Got an area that's nothing but red clay, rock and leaves? We clear it, grade it, rake it smooth and bring in rich topsoil so something good can actually grow there.

Planting & plant selection

New shrubs, trees and full-sun-tolerant plants picked for your soil and light — we'll help you choose what will actually thrive, then plant it right.

Leaf removal & cleanups

Spring and fall cleanups, leaf blow-and-haul, brush and debris removal — getting an overgrown or neglected property back to a clean slate.

Edging & stone borders

Defined bed edges, river-rock and stone borders, and stepping-stone paths that hold their shape and keep mulch where it belongs.

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. Estimates are free. We'll come look at your property, talk through what you want, and give you an honest price with no pressure to book.

What kind of work do you take on?

Lawn mowing and maintenance, mulch beds and bed design, grading and reclaiming rough areas, planting, leaf removal and seasonal cleanups, plus edging and stone borders. If you're not sure whether your project fits, just ask.

Can you fix an area that's just clay, rocks and weeds?

That's some of our favorite work. We clear it out, grade and rake it down, and bring in rich topsoil so the area can support a healthy lawn or planting bed. Take a look at the before-and-after above.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Bahama and serve northern Durham County and nearby communities like Rougemont, Durham, Stem, Creedmoor and the surrounding area. If you're close by, give us a call and we'll let you know.

How do I get started?

Call or text us at (984) 243-1158, or fill out the quick estimate form below. A photo of the area helps, but isn't required. We'll get back to you as soon as we can.
