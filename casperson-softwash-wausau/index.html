Why soft wash?

Soft wash vs. pressure washing

A high-pressure power washer can gouge siding, force water behind it, and strip the granules off your shingles — and it only blasts the surface, so the algae grows right back.

Soft washing uses low pressure and a biodegradable cleaning solution that kills algae and mildew at the root. Your roof and siding stay undamaged, and because we treat the cause, the clean lasts far longer.

  • Low pressure, no damage

    Safe for shingles, vinyl, stucco and painted surfaces.

  • Kills algae at the root

    Biodegradable solution treats the cause, not just the surface.

  • Stays clean longer

    Because the cause is gone, the result lasts — not just until the next rain.
