About C & D Fencing

A Howell fence shop built on word of mouth

C & D Fencing is a local, family-run business right here in Howell. We're a husband-and-wife team — one of us is out building the fence, the other answers the phone and walks you through every step.

Most of our work comes the best way it can: a neighbor sees a fence we built and asks who did it. That only happens when the line is straight, the gates swing true and the property looks better than it did before — so that's the standard on every job, big or small.

Whether you want privacy in the backyard, a clean rail line along the drive or aluminum around the pool, we'll help you pick the right fence and build it to last through Michigan's seasons.

Good to know

Fence questions, answered

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out to your property, walk the line with you, talk through materials and layout, and give you a clear written price — at no cost and with no pressure to sign anything.

What types of fence do you install?

Wood and cedar privacy, vinyl, chain-link (galvanized and black vinyl-coated), and aluminum/ornamental — plus matching walk and drive gates. If you're not sure what fits your yard or budget, we'll help you decide during the estimate.

Can you repair my existing fence instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. Leaning posts, storm damage, a sagging gate or a few broken boards usually don't need a full tear-out. We'll take a look and tell you honestly whether a repair or a replacement is the better call.

What areas do you serve?

Howell and the surrounding Livingston County communities — including Brighton, Pinckney, Fowlerville, Hartland and Hamburg. If you're nearby and don't see your town, just call and ask.

Do you handle the property line and layout?

We lay out the run with you so the fence sits where you want it, and we'll talk through where your line is before we dig. We recommend confirming property boundaries on larger jobs so the finished fence is exactly where it should be.
