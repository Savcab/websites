What we do

Complete tree care for high-desert properties

One experienced crew for the whole job — taking the tree down, shaping what stays, and hauling off every last branch.

Tree Removal

Safe takedown of large, dead, leaning or storm-damaged trees — including tight residential pines roped down piece by piece over your home and yard.

Trimming & Pruning

Thinning, shaping and deadwood removal that keeps your trees healthy, clears your roofline and lets more light into the canopy — done with the right cuts, not topping.

Dead-Tree & Fire Mitigation

Removing dead, dying and overgrown fuel close to the house, clearing defensible space and limbing up — practical wildfire-readiness for high-desert lots.

Stump Removal

We take the stump down with the tree or bring in our trusted grinding partner to finish the job — so you're left with clean, usable ground, not a leftover stump.

Cleanup & Hauling

Every job ends with the site cleaned up — limbs, debris and brush hauled away, the yard raked, and your property left tidier than we found it.

Firewood

Usable wood from removals can be cut and left as seasoned firewood — ask about availability when you book your job.

Frequently asked questions

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. Call us and we'll come out to look at the trees, the access and what you want done, then give you a clear, fair price before any work starts.
Can you take down a large tree close to my house?
That's a lot of what we do out here. Tall ponderosa pines and trees over structures come down in controlled pieces, roped and lowered so your home, fence and yard stay protected.
Do you handle dead trees and fire mitigation?
Yes — removing dead and dying trees, clearing brush and limbing up to open defensible space around the house is a regular part of high-desert tree work, and we're glad to help you get a property cleaned up.
What about the stump and the mess afterward?
We clean up every job — limbs and debris hauled off, the yard raked. For stumps we'll either take it down low with the tree or bring in our trusted grinding partner so you're left with clean, usable ground.
When are you available?
We're reachable seven days a week — Monday through Saturday 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM and Sunday 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call (928) 899-0875 and we'll get you scheduled.
