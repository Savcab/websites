Good to know

Common questions

Do you charge for an estimate?
No. Estimates are free. We come out, look at the yard, talk through materials and the line you want, and give you a clear written price before any work is scheduled.
What fence materials do you install?
Wood (privacy, picket, stockade and split-rail), vinyl, aluminum and chain-link. We'll help you weigh look, privacy and upkeep so you pick the right material for your property.
Do you build decks as well as fences?
Yes — fences and decks are both core to what we do. Single-level or multi-tier decks, stairs and railing in pressure-treated lumber or cedar, built off the back of the house.
Can you fence in a pool or a yard for pets?
Absolutely. We install pool enclosures in aluminum, chain-link and wood, plus fully fenced yards so pets and kids have the run of the place. Just let us know the goal when you call.
What areas do you cover?
Oxford and the surrounding Chenango County towns — Norwich, Greene, Bainbridge, Sherburne and nearby. If you're a short drive out, call and we'll let you know.
How do I get started?
Call or text (607) 244-4595, or fill out the estimate form below with a few details. We'll get back to you to set up a time to look at the project.
