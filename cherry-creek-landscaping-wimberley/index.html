Cherry Creek Landscaping is a Wimberley-based hardscape and landscape crew building paver driveways, flagstone patios, stone walls and full-yard makeovers across the Texas Hill Country. We treat every project as a design first — how the stone meets the house, how water moves across our rocky soil, how it will read once the plantings fill in.

Then we build it the right way: excavation, a compacted base, the grade set for drainage, and every paver and stone laid by hand. It's the work no one photographs, and it's why our driveways and patios stay flat and true season after season.

How we work

Designed, built, and the site left clean.

A straightforward path from the first look at your property to the day you start using it.

Walk the property

We come out, look at the space, and talk through what you're picturing — the driveway, the patio, the whole yard — and how the grade and drainage need to work.

Plan & estimate

You get a clear written estimate — materials, layout and price laid out plainly, with no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons once we start.

Build on a base

We excavate, compact the sub-base, set the grade for drainage, and lay every paver and stone by hand — the slow part, done right.

Hand it back clean

We finish the details, haul off the debris, and leave you a driveway, patio or yard that's ready to use and built to stay put.
