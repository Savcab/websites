Good to know

Common questions.

Still wondering about something? Call (563) 321-0444 and we’ll talk it through.

Do you handle both lawn care and snow removal?

Yes — that’s the whole idea. We mow, edge, mulch and tidy your property through spring and summer, then clear your snow and ice through fall and winter. One local crew covers you all year, so you only have one number to call.

How soon do you clear snow after a storm?

We get out promptly once the snow falls so your driveway, walks and lot are passable before you need to head out. Our customers specifically mention how quickly we show up after a snowfall.

Do you offer one-time visits or only regular service?

Both. Many customers set up recurring mowing or seasonal snow service, but we’re glad to handle a one-time clean-up or a single push after a storm. Tell us what you need and we’ll make it work.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Maquoketa and serve homes and businesses around town and the surrounding Jackson County communities. If you’re close by, give us a call — we’re probably already working nearby.

How do I get a price?

Call us or send the quick quote form below with your address and what you’re after. We’ll look it over and give you a clear, fair quote up front — no obligation.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. Alongside residential lawns and driveways, we keep commercial parking lots and entryways mowed in summer and cleared in winter so your property stays safe and presentable for customers and staff.
