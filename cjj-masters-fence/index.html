Good to know

Fence questions, answered

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We come out to your property, measure the run, talk through materials, styles and gates, and give you a clear written price with no obligation and no pressure.
Do you handle large or custom fence projects?
That's our specialty. Long board-on-board and stockade privacy runs, ranch and crossbuck fencing across acreage, and custom gates built and hung to match — the bigger custom jobs are exactly the work we're known for.
Which fence material is right for me?
It depends on your goal. Wood and vinyl give you full privacy; chain-link and welded no-climb wire are the budget-friendly way to enclose a yard, pets or livestock; aluminum and ornamental look sharp around front yards and pools. We'll walk you through the trade-offs during your estimate.
How are your fence posts set?
We dig each post hole to proper depth and set the posts in concrete. It's the part you never see, but it's what keeps a fence standing straight for years through Florida weather — so we don't cut corners on it.
Do you repair existing fences?
We do. Leaning sections, rotted or broken posts, storm damage and sagging gates are all common fixes — including re-setting posts in concrete so the repair lasts.
What areas do you cover?
We serve Orange City and the surrounding west Volusia County communities, including Deltona, DeBary, DeLand, Lake Helen and Enterprise. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call and we'll let you know.
