Good To Know

Common questions

Q. Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out to the property, look at the trees in person, and give you a no-obligation estimate. For large or multi-tree jobs, seeing it on site is the only way to quote it honestly.

Q. Can you handle big or hard-to-reach trees?

That’s our specialty. Tall pines, big oaks, steep lots and tight access — we bring a bucket truck and the right crew rather than forcing a job that needs more equipment.

Q. Do you clean up and haul off the debris?

Always. The job isn’t done until the limbs are chipped, the debris is hauled away and your property is left clean. We can grind the stumps too if you’d like.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Oakhurst and the surrounding eastern Madera County foothills — Coarsegold, North Fork, Bass Lake, Ahwahnee and the communities in between. Not sure if you’re in range? Just give us a call.

Q. How fast can you come out for a hazard?

If a tree is down or leaning over a structure, call us right away. We’re open seven days a week and will get out to assess and clear the danger as quickly as we can.

Q. What are your hours?

We’re open Monday through Saturday from 7am to 7pm, and Sunday afternoons from noon to 7pm. The fastest way to reach us is a call to (559) 760-0073.
