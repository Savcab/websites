What we build

Eight jobs, one Denver crew.

Hardscape & patios

Paver patios and walkways, set on a real base so they don't heave or settle.

Retaining walls

Walls that hold a slope and read as designed, not just stacked block.

Grading & drainage

Grading that moves water away from the house instead of pooling against it.

Sod & lawn install

Fresh sod, laid and rolled in, for a lawn that's ready the day we leave.

Planting & mulch beds

Planting beds and fresh mulch that frame the rest of the yard.

Rock, edging & cleanup

Decorative rock, clean bed edging, and yard cleanups that keep it looking finished.

Irrigation & sprinklers

Sprinkler systems set up, or repaired, so water lands where the lawn actually needs it.

Fencing & artificial turf

Fencing and artificial turf for the spots real grass won't hold up.

How we work

Design-build, from the ground up.

We plan the hardscape and the planting together, so a retaining wall, a patio, and the beds around it read as one yard — not a few separate projects bolted on over different summers.

Denver's clay soil and freeze-thaw winters are hard on work that skips the base. We treat grading and prep as part of the job, not an extra — for the walls, the patios, and the lawns we put in.
