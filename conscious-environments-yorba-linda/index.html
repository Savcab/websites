About Us

A licensed design-build team, not a subcontractor shuffle

We're a design-build firm based in Yorba Linda, serving Orange County and the Inland Empire since 2009. As a licensed general contractor as well as a specialty landscape (C27) and swimming pool (C53) contractor — CSLB #902395, current and active — one team carries your project from the first sketch through construction and beyond.

The firm is led by Joshua Duncan, Principal Officer & Designer (BSLA, Cal Poly Pomona), with Matt Rodriguez as Operations & Project Manager (Landscape Architecture, Cal Poly Pomona) overseeing projects in the field.
