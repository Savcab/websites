Good to know

Fence & deck questions, answered

How much does a new fence cost?

It depends on the material, length and any gates or grade changes — a treated-pine fence and a custom cedar privacy fence aren't priced the same. That's exactly what the free on-site estimate is for: we measure, talk through options, and give you a clear, honest number with no obligation.

What fence materials do you install?

Wood (privacy, board-on-board, picket and ranch/split-rail), vinyl, and aluminum/ornamental fencing. For wood, the lumber is hand-selected before it comes to your job so you get sound, good-looking boards.

Do you build decks too?

Yes. We build custom decks — sized and laid out for how you use your backyard — including railings, steps and skirting. Fence and deck on the same project is no problem; it's one local crew for both.

Do you remove and replace an old fence?

We do. Whether it's a leaning section or a decades-old picket or chain-link fence that's reached the end of its life, we tear out the old fence and build the new one cleanly.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Carey and serve Wyandot County and the surrounding northwest-Ohio area — including Upper Sandusky, Findlay, Tiffin, Fostoria and Bucyrus. A short drive past that is usually fine; just ask.
