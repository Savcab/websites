What we cover

One vendor for the grounds, start to finish.

From regular grounds maintenance to a full desert build, you get it all from one crew that knows your property — planned, scheduled and desert-smart.

Grounds maintenance

Mowing, trimming, cleanups and monthly grounds care on a set rotation — for both residential and commercial properties, kept sharp without you chasing us down.

Desert xeriscape & plantings

Low-water desert landscaping with the right plants for the heat — cactus gardens, ornamental grasses, boulders and beds designed to thrive here and stay easy to keep.

Turf & play areas

Artificial turf lawns and backyard putting greens — clean, green year-round with almost no water, built over a proper base to drain and hold up.

Backyard & hardscape builds

Full property projects — paver patios, walkways, patio covers, fire pits and cactus gardens — designed and built to turn a bare yard into a finished space.
