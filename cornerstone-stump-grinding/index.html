What we do

Stump grinding, done thoroughly

We focus on one thing and do it well — grinding stumps out of the ground and cleaning up after. Here's how that breaks down.

Below-grade stump grinding

The core job: we grind the stump several inches below the surface so the spot can be sodded, seeded or planted over — no leftover hump, no trip hazard.

Multi-stump & lot clearing

Got a whole row, a fence line, or a lot full of stumps? We handle large multi-stump jobs efficiently — residential yards and commercial properties alike.

Tight-access grinding

Our compact tracked grinders fit through a standard gate and work around fences, sheds and tight backyards where a big machine simply can't go.

Bush & shrub removal

Stubborn shrubs and overgrown bushes get pulled and ground out too, so you can reclaim the bed or the corner of the yard for good.

Grinding cleanup & haul-off

We don't leave a mess. Grindings get raked back into the hole or hauled away on request, so the area's ready to use the day we finish.

Free, no-pressure quotes

Good to know

Common stump-grinding questions

How deep do you grind the stump?

We grind several inches below the surrounding grade — deep enough that you can rake soil over it and lay sod, seed or plant. No leftover hump and nothing to trip over.

Do you clean up the grindings?

Yes. We rake the grindings back into the hole to settle, or haul them off if you'd rather — just let us know. We leave the spot tidy.

Can you get to a stump in a tight backyard?

Usually, yes. Our compact tracked grinders are built to fit through a standard gate and maneuver around fences, sheds and landscaping that larger equipment can't reach.

Do you handle more than one stump at a time?

Absolutely — anything from a single backyard stump to a full fence line or a lot full of them, for both homeowners and commercial properties.

How soon can you come out?

Often within the same week. Scheduling can shift with weather and workload, but fast turnaround is something our customers consistently mention.
