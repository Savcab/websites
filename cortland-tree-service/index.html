Good to know

Tree service questions, answered

Do you handle emergency tree removal after hours?

Yes. Storms and falling trees don't wait for business hours, and neither do we. If a tree comes down on your home, shop, vehicle, or driveway, call us — customers have had us on site the same morning, and the same day for trees blocking a driveway.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. We'll come look at the job, talk you through what's involved, and give you a clear, reasonable estimate before any work starts — so you know the price up front.

Can you remove a tree close to my house or fence?

That's most of what we do. With our climbing crew and crane setup, we take down trees in tight spots — over fences, garages, sheds, and near power lines — and bring them down in a controlled way without damaging what's around them.

Do you grind the stump and clean up afterward?

We can grind the stump below grade so you get your yard back, and cleanup is always part of the job — limbs handled, wood hauled or stacked, and the site raked clean before we leave.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Cortland and work throughout Cortland County and the surrounding Central New York towns — including Homer, Cortlandville, McGraw, Preble, Marathon, Virgil, and nearby. Not sure if you're covered? Just call and ask.
