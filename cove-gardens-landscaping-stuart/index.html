What we do

Design it, plant it, and keep the plants coming.

We're two things at once — a crew that designs and installs tropical gardens, and a garden center you can walk through. Here's where folks around Stuart put us to work.

Landscape & garden design

Tell us the yard you're picturing and we'll design it — beds, borders, palms and tropical plantings laid out for your space, then built the way we drew it up.

Planting & bed installation

New beds, remove-and-replace, fresh mulch and the right plants for the spot — we plant it, shape it, and leave everything clean and tidy when we're done.

Garden center & plant nursery

Come browse our lot on SE Dixie Hwy. We keep a deep, always-changing selection of tropical plants, palms and color — the spot to visit when your garden's missing that one plant.

Container & tropical gardens

Potted and container plantings styled for patios, pool decks and entryways — the finishing touch that makes an outdoor space feel planted, not just paved over.

The finished look

Where the whole yard comes together.

This is the Florida garden we're always working toward — a lawn kept sharp, palms and tropical beds framing the space, every corner planted with intent. Design, install and the right plants get you here.
