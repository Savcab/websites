Common questions

What areas do you serve?

We're based on Independence Blvd in Washington, PA and serve homeowners across town and the surrounding Washington County communities — places like Canonsburg, Houston, Meadow Lands and South Strabane. If you're nearby and not sure, just call and we'll let you know.

Do you handle both lawn care and snow removal?

Yes — that's the whole idea. The same family-run crew that mows and maintains your lawn in season clears your driveway and walks in winter, so you never have to line up a separate company when the snow comes.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (724) 557-2560, or fill out the quick form below. Tell us a little about your property and what you're looking for, and we'll get back to you — usually within a few hours.

How often will you service my lawn?

Whatever fits your property — most homeowners are on a weekly or bi-weekly schedule through the growing season. We'll recommend a rhythm when we look at the yard, and we remind you when it's time to start up in spring and wind down in fall.

What are your hours?

We're available 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM, seven days a week. Call or text any day and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
