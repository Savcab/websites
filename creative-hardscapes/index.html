Questions, answered

How much does a paver patio cost?

Every project is different — size, materials, site conditions and features like walls or fire pits all affect the price. The best way to get a real number is a free on-site estimate, where we measure, talk through options, and give you an honest, detailed quote with no obligation.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We come to your property, look at the space, and put together a detailed estimate at no cost. Call (330) 763-1874 or use the form below to set one up.

Why does base preparation matter so much?

A patio is only as good as what’s underneath it. Proper excavation, a compacted aggregate base and good drainage are what keep pavers from settling, shifting or heaving over time. It’s the work you never see — and the reason our projects stay flat and tight for the long haul.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Millersburg and serve Holmes County and the surrounding Amish Country communities, including Berlin, Walnut Creek, Sugarcreek and nearby towns. Not sure if you’re in range? Give us a call and we’ll let you know.

How far out are you booking?

It depends on the season and the size of your project. The sooner you reach out, the easier it is to get you on the schedule for the timeframe you have in mind — so it’s worth calling early.
