Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

Do you handle dangerous or leaning trees?

Yes — that’s our specialty. Dead tops, leaning trunks and limbs hanging over roads, lines or buildings are exactly the removals we’re known for. We bring the rigging and experience to take them down in control.

How much does tree removal cost?

Every tree is different — size, lean, access and what’s underneath it all matter. We come look at it in person and give you a straight, no-pressure quote before any work starts.

Do you clean up afterward?

Always. Brush gets chipped, the wood is handled however you’d like, and we rake out the work site before we leave. We don’t consider the job done until your yard is clean.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Orleans and work throughout the Northeast Kingdom — Barton, Newport, Derby, Glover, Irasburg and the surrounding Orleans County towns. If you’re nearby, just call.

How soon can you come out?

It depends on the season and the schedule, but we’re a local crew and try to get to you quickly — especially for hazard trees. Call and we’ll let you know our next opening.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes. We can grind the stump below grade after the tree is down so you can reclaim the spot for lawn, garden or driveway. Just mention it when you call.
