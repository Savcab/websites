Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

How do I get a price?

Call or text (717) 348-6552, or send the quick form on this page with your address and what you're after. We'll take a look and give you a free, no-pressure estimate.

Do you handle one-time cleanups, or just regular mowing?

Both. We keep weekly and bi-weekly mowing customers, and we also take on one-time jobs — overgrown shrubs, a neglected bed, a path or wall. If it's been a few seasons since it was touched, that's a job we're glad to take.

Can you keep up a property while I'm away?

Yes — a good share of our customers travel or work away from home. We'll keep the lawn cut and the beds tidy on a set schedule so the place looks cared for whether or not you're there.

What days do you work?

We're out Monday through Saturday, 7am to 6pm. Sunday we're closed. Leave a message any time and we'll get back to you.

What areas do you cover?

Thompsontown and the surrounding Juniata County towns — Mifflintown, Mifflin, Port Royal, McAlisterville, Richfield — plus a short drive into Perry County around Millerstown and Newport. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

Is the owner the one doing the work?

Cameron is on the job and handles the detailed trimming and shrub work himself. You get the same hands on your property each visit, not a rotating crew.
