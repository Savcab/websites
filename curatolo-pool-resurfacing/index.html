Good to know

Pool resurfacing questions, answered

How long does resurfacing take?

Most residential replasters are finished in about two to three days, plus time to refill and balance the water. Larger or more complex pools may take a little longer — we'll give you a realistic timeline with your quote.

How do I know my pool needs resurfacing?

Common signs are a rough or chalky surface that scrapes skin, persistent stains, plaster flaking or "spalling," and exposed patches. If you're noticing any of these, it's worth a look — the assessment is free.

What's the difference between Marcite and Diamond Brite?

Marcite is the classic white plaster — affordable and clean. Diamond Brite and quartz finishes blend in aggregate that resists staining, feels smoother underfoot and keeps its color much longer. We'll help you weigh cost against longevity.

Do you handle the tile and coping too?

Yes. We can replace waterline tile, set new mosaics and redo coping as part of the same project, so the pool comes together as one finished piece rather than a patchwork.
