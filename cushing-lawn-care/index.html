Frequently asked questions

How do I get a quote?

Just call us at (918) 697-1077 or send a message through the estimate form below with your address and what you'd like done. We'll get you a straight, no-pressure price — estimates are always free.

Do you really send photos when the job's done?

Yes — it's one of the things our customers mention most. When we finish a mow or job, we send a photo update so you can see the finished work, even if you're out of town or managing the property from a distance.

Can you handle large lots and acreage?

Absolutely. We run commercial equipment built for bigger properties, so rural lots, large yards, and managed acreage are no problem — we get it all done in one trip.

Do you do tree work too, or just mowing?

Both. Alongside mowing and seasonal maintenance, we handle tree trimming, pruning, and removal of dead, overgrown, or storm-damaged trees and limbs — with the debris hauled off when we're done.

Do you offer regular, recurring service?

Yes. Most of our customers are on a regular mowing schedule through the season so their yard always stays sharp. We'll set up a routine that fits your property — weekly, bi-weekly, or as needed.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Cushing and serve the surrounding communities including Drumright, Yale, Stillwater, Perkins, Ripley, and Oilton. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call — we'll let you know.
