Frequently asked questions

Do you mow large lots and acreage?

Yes — acreage and large rural lots are some of our favorite work. Whether it's a couple of manicured acres or an overgrown field that needs reclaiming, we have the equipment to handle it. Just tell us the size and condition and we'll quote it.

Can you clear overgrown brush, trees and stumps?

Absolutely. We clear overgrown juniper, mesquite and brush, trim or remove trees, grind down stumps and haul off the undergrowth so you're left with usable, open land.

Do you build retaining walls and patios?

We do. Along with lawn care we build natural-stone and block retaining walls, flagstone walkways, patios and fire-pit areas — practical hardscaping that holds the land and looks great.

How do I get a price?

Call us at (940) 683-3121 or send the quick quote form below. We'll look over the property, go over exactly what you need, and give you a fair, upfront price before any work starts.

How do I pay once the work is done?

It's easy — once the job is complete and you're happy with it, you can pay online. No chasing invoices or mailing checks.
