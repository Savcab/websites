Good to know

Questions, answered

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Bluefield and serve Princeton, Bluewell, Green Valley, Athens and the rest of Mercer County. Just outside that? Call and we'll let you know.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We come walk the property, talk through what you're after, and give you a straightforward written quote at no cost or obligation.

Do you handle more than mowing?

Absolutely. Alongside lawn maintenance we do mulch and flower beds, hardscaping, grading, brush hogging and full land clearing with our own equipment.

Do you take residential and commercial?

Both. We care for homes as well as businesses, ball fields and larger properties on regular, scheduled service across the area.

Can I set up recurring service?

Yes — most clients are on a weekly or bi-weekly schedule so the property stays consistently sharp through the season. We'll build a plan that fits.
