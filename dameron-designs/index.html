Our approach

Design-led, and involved to the last plant.

Dameron Designs was founded by landscape designer Daniel Dameron, who came up through horticulture rather than an office. Every project starts on site — reading the light, the soil and the way you actually use the yard — before a single plant is specified.

01

Design & master plan

A site study, plant selection for the Florida climate, and a plan you can see before anything is built.

02

Sourcing & bids

We line up the right contractors and source stone, mulch and sod at the right price.

03

Build & oversight

On site through installation, so the finished landscape matches the plan.

What we do

Landscape design & build for Central Florida.

One studio for the whole outdoor project — from the first plan to the finished garden, patio and irrigation.

Landscape design & planning

Custom plans from concept to installation, drawn around your site and how you live outdoors.

Tropical & Florida-Friendly planting

Palms, color and low-water plantings suited to Central Florida’s climate and soil.

Paver patios & travertine

Patios, walkways and pool decks in pavers and travertine, laid to last.

Retaining & garden walls

Tiered walls and raised beds that shape a slope and hold a landscape in place.

Irrigation & landscape lighting

Efficient irrigation and lighting designed in alongside the planting, not added after.

Project management

Bidding, sourcing and on-site oversight so one team owns the outcome from start to finish.
