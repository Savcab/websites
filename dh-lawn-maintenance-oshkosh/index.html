What we do

Property care that keeps up with the seasons.

A short, honest list of the work we actually do — the mowing, mulch, grading and snow that keep Oshkosh yards looking sharp from the first cut to the last storm.

Weekly mowing & lawn care

Weekly mowing, edging and trimming that keeps your lawn clean, even and striped — plus spring lawn rolling to smooth out the bumps and frost heave.

Mulch & stone installation

Fresh mulch, decorative stone, clean edging and new plantings — the finishing work that makes a yard read cared-for instead of overgrown.

Grading, drainage & site work

Regrading, drainage, soil and stump work — leveling low spots and window wells, and moving water away from the house so the yard drains right.
