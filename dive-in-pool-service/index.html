Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to sign a contract for weekly service?
No. Our maintenance plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. You stay because your pool stays clean, and you can cancel anytime.
Do you repair equipment, or only clean pools?
Both. Equipment repair is one of the things we're best known for — pumps, filters, heaters, and salt systems. We diagnose the real problem and fix what can be fixed before recommending a replacement.
My pool turned green — can you bring it back?
Yes. Green-pool rescue is a regular part of what we do. We shock and treat the water, deep-clean, and get filtration back on track until it's clear and swim-ready again.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve the greater Las Vegas valley, including Spring Valley, Enterprise, Summerlin, Henderson, and the southwest. Not sure if you're in range? Send a quick request and we'll let you know.
How do I get started?
Call (702) 279-9453 or fill out the service request form below. We'll talk through what your pool needs and get you scheduled.
