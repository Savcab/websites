01 — What we build

Heavy stonework, done with a craftsman's eye.

From a lakeshore that's washing away to a backyard that needs a patio — the same crew handles the rip rap, the walls, and the finish work.

01
Our specialty

Shoreline Rip Rap & Stabilization

Erosion-proof your waterfront. We set graded rip rap and natural stone along the shore to stop the bank from washing out — sized and placed to handle wave action and ice, and to look like it has always been there.

  • Rip rap & erosion control
  • Natural fieldstone shorelines
  • Lake & cabin waterfronts
02
Built to grade

Retaining Walls

Fieldstone, boulder, and segmental block walls that hold a slope and look the part. Built with proper base prep and drainage so they stand straight for the long haul — "built with such precision," as one client put it.

  • Fieldstone & boulder walls
  • Segmental block (SRW)
  • Proper base & drainage
03
Outdoor living

Paver Patios, Walkways & Steps

Patios, walkways, steps, and stone fire features that turn the yard into a place you actually use. Tight joints, clean edges, and a layout built around how you live outside.

  • Paver patios & walkways
  • Steps & flagstone paths
  • Outdoor fireplaces & fire pits
04
Design & install

Landscape Design & Install

Grading, planting beds, boulders, mulch, and fresh sod or seed to tie the whole project together. We'll bring a real vision for what the space can become — and make it happen.

  • Grading & full regrade
  • Planting beds, boulders & mulch
  • New sod & seed
