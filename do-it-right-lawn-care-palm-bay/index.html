How we work

From first walk-through to the finished yard.

A straightforward path from the first look at your property to the day it's done and dialed in.

Walk the property & talk design

We come look at the space, talk through what you're picturing, and figure out how the beds, rock and hardscape should come together.

Plan & estimate

You get a clear estimate — materials, layout and price laid out plainly, no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons once we start.

Build it right

We prep the ground, set clean edges and borders, and install the beds, rock, pavers or irrigation by hand — done the slow way so it holds.

Keep it maintained

When you want it kept up, we handle the mowing, trimming and seasonal cleanups so the property keeps looking right.
