Who we are

A design/build landscape company, rooted in Durango.

Down to Earth is a design/build landscaping and gardening company serving residential and commercial clients across the Durango area. We handle the whole arc of a project — from the first sketch of a garden to the rockwork, planting and irrigation that bring it to life — with a real emphasis on xeriscape principles that fit our high-desert climate.

That means landscapes that look intentional and lush without fighting the seasons: native and adapted plantings, smart water use, and stonework built to hold up to Colorado winters. Whether it is a full backyard transformation or seasonal care for the garden you already love, the same crew sees it through.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle both design and installation?

Yes. Down to Earth is a design/build company, so the same team designs your landscape and then builds it — stonework, planting, irrigation and all. You're never juggling a separate designer and crew.

What is xeriscaping, and why do you emphasize it?

Xeriscaping is landscape design built around low water use — native and adapted plants, efficient irrigation and smart soil and mulch choices. In our high-desert climate it means a beautiful yard that needs less water and less ongoing maintenance.

Do you work with both homes and businesses?

We do — Down to Earth serves both residential and commercial clients across the Durango area, from backyard gardens to larger commercial grounds.

Can you maintain the landscape after it's built?

Absolutely. Alongside installation we offer ongoing garden and lawn maintenance — spring and fall cleanups, weeding, deadheading, mowing, fertilization, aeration, and irrigation start-ups and winterization.

How do I get started and what does it cost?

Reach out by phone or the form below and we'll set up a visit, talk through your goals, and put together a design and a free estimate. Every project is different, so pricing is based on the scope we agree on together.
