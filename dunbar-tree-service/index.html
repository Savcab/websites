Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We’ll come look at the tree or the job, talk through your options, and give you a clear price — no charge and no pressure. Call or send the form and we’ll set up a time.
How careful are you around the house and yard?
It’s the whole point of how we work. We rig and lower limbs so nothing drops where it shouldn’t, protect lawns and beds, and rake the site clean before we leave. Our customers tell us they couldn’t tell we’d been there — that’s the goal every time.
Do you handle storm damage and emergencies?
Yes — a tree on the house or a limb blocking the drive can’t wait. We take emergency and storm calls around the clock and get out to clear the hazard as quickly as we safely can.
What happens to the wood and brush?
Your call. We chip the brush and can haul everything off, or cut and stack usable firewood wherever you’d like it on the property. Either way the ground gets cleaned up after.
Do you remove the stump too?
We can grind stumps down below grade so you can mow, replant or build over the spot, and we clean up the grindings. Just let us know when you book the removal so we plan for it.
Which towns do you cover?
Friendship and the surrounding midcoast and Knox County towns — Cushing, Waldoboro, Thomaston, Warren, the St. George peninsula, Rockland and nearby. Not sure if you’re in range? Just call and ask.
