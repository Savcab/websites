What we do

Full-service tree care for homes and property

Tree Removal

Trimming & Pruning

Good to know

Common questions

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the tree's height, the species, how close it is to the house or power lines, and whether you want the stump ground. The honest answer is we'll give you a firm price once we see it — estimates are free and usually given right on the spot.

Can you reach really tall trees?

Yes — that's a big part of why customers call us. Our bucket truck and crane let us work the tall pines and big hardwoods that some crews can't get high enough to reach safely.

Do you handle emergencies and storm damage?

We do. If a tree comes down on your house, car or driveway, call any hour — clearing downed and hazard trees quickly and safely is exactly what we're set up for.

Will you clean up afterward?

Always. We haul the debris, grind the stump if you'd like, and rake the site clean before we leave. You get your yard back, not a mess.

What areas do you serve?

Uncasville and the surrounding southeastern Connecticut towns — Montville, Norwich, Waterford, New London, Gales Ferry and nearby. Call if you're close by and don't see your town.
