Frequently asked questions

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We come out to your property, look at the space, talk through your options, and give you a clear written price at no charge and with no obligation. Call (864) 328-8647 or send the quote form to set it up.
What kind of work do you take on?
Paver patios, fire pits, retaining and seat walls, sod installation, planting and beds, drainage and grading, fence installation, and brush/lot cleanups. If it’s shaping or building out your yard, it’s in our wheelhouse — just ask.
Do you handle drainage and erosion problems?
Yes — it’s some of our most common work. We regrade problem areas and install rock swales, riprap and sod to move water away from your home and stop a slope from washing out. We’ll assess the cause on the estimate visit.
Which areas do you serve?
We’re based in Anderson and serve Anderson, Belton, Honea Path, Pendleton, Williamston and the surrounding Anderson County area. Not sure if you’re in range? Give us a call and we’ll let you know.
How soon can you start?
It depends on the season and the size of the job, but we’ll give you a realistic timeline with your estimate — and our reviewers will tell you we show up when we say we will and finish in the time we quote.
