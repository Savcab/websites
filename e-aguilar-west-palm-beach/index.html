What we do

One crew for the whole yard

E. Aguilar Landscaping handles the hardscape and the greenery — so your patio, beds, lawn and drainage all come together the way you pictured.

Our specialty

Paver & brick hardscaping

New paver patios and driveways, re-leveling and repairing existing pavers, polymeric sand and sealing — the work reviewers rave about.

Lawn maintenance & design

Regular mowing, edging and cleanups, plus fresh bed design with mulch, river rock and the right plants for South Florida.

Grass & sod installation

Full sod installation and lawn renovation to turn tired, patchy yards into a healthy, even green from the curb back.

Drainage & sprinkler systems

Drainage installation to move water off your property and sprinkler systems that keep everything irrigated through the dry season.

Tree trimming & stump removal

Trimming, shaping and full tree services, plus stump removal to clear the way for new beds, sod or hardscape.

Engineered landscaping

Planned, built-to-last landscaping — grading, structure and layout designed around how your property actually drains and lives.
