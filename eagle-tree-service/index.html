Good to Know

Common questions

How often should palm trees be trimmed?

For most Arizona fan and date palms, once a year is plenty — usually late spring through summer after the seed pods form. If the skirt has been left for several years, we’ll often do a heavier catch-up trim first, then keep it on a simple yearly schedule.

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. We come out and look at the trees at no charge, often the same day you call, and give you a clear price before any work starts.

Can you remove a tree close to my house or pool?

Yes. We take trees down in controlled pieces using our bucket truck and rigging, so we can work safely around homes, fences, pools and tight desert lots without the mess or damage.

Do you work with HOAs and 55+ communities?

We do — including catching up whole neighborhoods of palms that haven’t been trimmed in years. We can schedule the work in an orderly way and keep it tidy from start to finish.

Do you haul away the debris?

Always. Fronds, branches and trimmings go straight into our chipper and get hauled off. Cleanup is part of the job — we leave your yard clean.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Toltec and serve Casa Grande, Arizona City, Coolidge, Eloy, Maricopa and the surrounding Pinal County communities. If you’re nearby, give us a call and we’ll let you know.
