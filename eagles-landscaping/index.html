Good to know

Common questions

Do you offer regular lawn maintenance, or just one-time work?

Both. Many neighbors keep us on a weekly or recurring schedule through the growing season, but we’re glad to handle one-time cleanups, repairs and projects too.

Can you fix a sprinkler system that someone else installed?

Yes. We troubleshoot and repair existing irrigation — leaks, broken heads, zones that won’t fire — even when the system is a tangle. We’ll sort it out and make it work.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your property and the work involved, so we give you a clear estimate before we start. Reviewers consistently mention our pricing being fair and affordable.

Do you handle snow removal in the winter?

We do. We clear driveways and walkways for area homeowners through the winter, so the same crew that keeps your lawn green keeps your driveway clear.

Ready for a yard you don’t have to think about?

Call or message Eagles Landscaping for a free, no-pressure estimate. We’ll take a look and tell you exactly what it’ll take — for the lawn now, or the driveway this winter.
