Good to know

Questions Snohomish homeowners ask

Do you do both design-build projects and regular maintenance?

Yes. We design and install new landscapes — dry river beds, planting beds, paver walls, driveways — and we also keep yards maintained with mowing, trimming, edging and seasonal cleanups. Many clients have us build it, then keep it on a schedule.

Are estimates free?

They are. We come out, look at the space, talk through what you want and give you a clear written estimate at no cost. Call (425) 559-8808 or use the form below to get on the schedule.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Snohomish and serve the surrounding area including Lake Stevens, Monroe, Everett, Mill Creek and nearby communities in Snohomish County. If you're close by, just ask — we'll let you know honestly.

Can you fix drainage and soggy spots in the yard?

Often, yes. Dry river beds and proper bed grading are two of the things we do well — they move water away from the house while looking like a designed feature instead of a drainage fix.

What are your hours?

We're reachable Monday through Friday 7:00 AM–7:00 PM and Saturday 10:00 AM–4:00 PM. Leave a message any time and we'll get back to you.
