What we do

Full-service landscaping & irrigation

One crew for the whole yard — the water that keeps it alive and the hardscape and planting that make it yours. Every job we take, we aim to get done right the first time.

Sprinkler & Irrigation

Diagnosis, repair and smart automation of sprinkler and drip systems, so every zone waters the right amount, right on schedule.

Landscape Installation

Full front- and back-yard installs: fresh lawns and artificial turf, planting beds, mulch and grading — built to look finished from day one.

Pavers & Hardscape

Paver patios, walkways and borders set on a proper base and edged clean, so they stay level and true for years to come.

Drainage & French Drains

French drains and re-grading that move water away from your home and foundation and keep the yard dry through wet Sacramento winters.

Water Mainline Service

Clean installation and repair of water mainlines — locating the problem, fixing it properly, and restoring the ground behind us.

Gate Valves & Controls

Gate valve installation and repair to give you reliable shut-off and control over your water supply when you need it.
