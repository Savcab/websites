Services & cycles

What we run, and how often.

Most of what we do is a standing cycle — the same yard, the same week, no phone call needed. The rest is one-time work: the clean-outs, and the jobs a yard only needs once or twice a year.

ServiceCycle

Lawn care & mowing

Cut and edged on a set schedule. One reviewer has us out on the first day of the month, once a month or once every two weeks — you pick the rhythm and we hold it.

Monthly · biweekly

Sprinkler repair

Reviewers describe us repairing sprinkler systems on the same visits as the mow — so a broken head or a dry corner gets dealt with while we’re already on your street. Tell us what isn’t watering.

On the route

Yard clean-outs & haul-away

Overgrowth cut back, beds cleared, sod and old turf taken out — and the green waste leaves with us instead of sitting at the curb. One reviewer had a complete clean-out done the same day as the first phone call.

One-time

Weeding, trimming & seasonal

Weed removal out of lawns, beds and gravel, bush and shrub trimming, edging, aeration, seeding, mulching and leaf removal. The jobs a yard needs once or twice a year, not once a week.
