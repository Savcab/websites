The Work

Real gardens, reshaped.

Every project starts on-site — reading the soil, the light, the slope of the land — before a single plant goes in the ground. The work stays organic and holistic from plan to planting, so a bed we build keeps working for the property long after we've left it.

Organic & Holistic Garden Design

Plans built from the soil up — right plant, right place, no synthetic shortcuts.

Landscape Design

From a single bed to the whole property, drawn and installed to fit how you actually use the yard.

Planting & Land Stewardship

Ongoing care that treats a property as a living system, not a maintenance checklist.

02

Approach

Grown, not manufactured.

We design gardens the way we'd want one designed for us: organically, thoughtfully, one property at a time. Every bed starts with the land itself — soil, light, water, what already wants to grow there — and the plan follows from that, not the other way around.

We've been shaping gardens around Hudson since at least 2017, working side by side with homeowners from the first sketch to the last plant in the ground.
