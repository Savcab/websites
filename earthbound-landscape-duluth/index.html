What We Build

Design-build, from grade to garden.

One crew for the hardscape bones and the living details — planned, installed and cared for with an environmentally conscious approach.

Hardscape & Stonework

Boulder retaining walls, natural stone steps, flagstone and bluestone patios, fire pits and accent rock.

Landscape Design & Installation

Beds, trees, shrubs, sod and seasonal color — graded, planted and mulched to a finished plan.

Artificial Turf & Putting Greens

Low-maintenance synthetic turf and backyard putting greens installed for year-round green.

Landscape Lighting

Low-voltage lighting that carries the design into the evening and lights paths, walls and trees.

Drainage Solutions

Dry creek beds, regrading and water management that protect the slope and the soil after every storm.

Horticultural Care

Shrub & tree pruning, weed control, turf fertilization, mowing, edging and seasonal cleanups.

About Earthbound

An ecologist's eye on every yard.

Earthbound Landscape Services is owner-led by Victor Vazquez, who studied Ecology and Horticulture at UGA and brings over 17 years of experience to the work. The result is landscaping built with an environmentally conscious approach — the right plants and the right drainage, set on stonework made to last.

From a sloped, unusable backyard to a finished woodland retreat, the goal is the same: a space that looks the part today and holds up for years.
