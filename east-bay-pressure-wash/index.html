Soft wash vs. pressure wash

The right pressure protects your home

Not every surface should be blasted. Knowing when to soft-wash and when to pressure-wash is the difference between a clean home and a damaged one — so here's exactly how we decide.

Soft Wash

Low pressure plus professional cleaning solutions that kill algae and mildew at the root and rinse clean. The gentle choice that does the heavy lifting chemically, not mechanically.

Best for: roofs · vinyl & cedar siding · painted surfaces · screens & soffits

Pressure Wash

Higher pressure for hard, durable surfaces that can take it — clearing away years of ground-in dirt, oil and growth that soft washing alone won't reach.

Best for: concrete driveways · brick & pavers · walkways · patios

Frequently asked questions

Will pressure washing damage my roof or siding?

Not the way we do it. Roofs and delicate siding are cleaned with a low-pressure soft wash — cleaning solutions do the work, not brute force — so there's no risk of stripping shingle granules or driving water behind your siding. We only use high pressure on hard surfaces that can take it.

What's the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure plus professional cleaning solutions to safely remove algae, mildew and black streaks from surfaces like roofs and siding. Pressure washing uses higher water pressure for tough, durable surfaces like concrete driveways and walkways. We choose the right one for each surface on your property.

Can you get my house ready before I list it for sale?

Absolutely — it's one of our most-requested jobs. We clean the roof, siding, walkways and driveway so your home looks its best in the listing photos and at every showing. Clean curb appeal helps a home sell.
