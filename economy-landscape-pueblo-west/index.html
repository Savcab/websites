How we work

A sketch, then the build, one phase at a time.

A clear, no-surprises sequence from your first call to the last day on site.

Walk the yard

We come look at the space, hear what you're picturing, and talk through how the drainage, hardscape and planting want to work together.

Sketch the plan

You get a sketch of the design before anything is built, so you can see the project — and the phasing — before we start.

Build it in phases

Drainage first, then hardscape in crushed asphalt, pavers, block or stone, then the planting — each phase sequenced so the budget and the yard both make sense.

Hand it back

We finish the details, clean the site down, and leave you a yard that's ready to use.
