Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

How do I get a price for my tree?

Call (760) 793-4501 and describe the tree — size, type, where it sits. Ed will give you a fair, upfront quote for the job.

Do you remove the stump too?

Yes. We can grind the stump below grade after a removal so you're left with usable ground, not a trip hazard.

Do you haul away the debris?

We do — cleanup is a big part of what customers thank us for. We clear the branches, wood and brush and leave the yard tidy, front and back.

Do you trim palm trees?

Yes — date and fan palms throughout the valley, from skinning and frond trimming to full removal.

What areas do you cover?

Ridgecrest and the surrounding Indian Wells Valley communities, including Inyokern, China Lake Acres, Ridgecrest Heights and Pearsonville.

Got a tree that needs to come down?

Call Ed for a free estimate. Local crew, fair prices, and a yard left cleaner than we found it.
