Frequently asked questions

A tree just came down on my house — how fast can you come?

Call us right away. For genuine emergencies like a tree on a home, car or across the driveway, we make it a priority to get out and assess the same day, then move the schedule around to clear it as quickly as we safely can.

What kinds of trees do you remove?

Everything from a single hazardous limb to large, dead or leaning trees close to the house. We do a lot of tall dead ash in this area, plus oak, maple and pine — using bucket trucks and grapple equipment to bring them down safely.

Do you grind the stump and haul away the wood?

Yes. We haul the logs and brush off your property as part of the job, and we can grind the stump below grade so you can put the lawn back. Just let us know when we give you the estimate.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the size of the tree, how close it is to the house or wires, and how tough the access is. The honest answer is we'll come look and give you a clear, no-pressure price before any work starts.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Willimantic and serve the surrounding Windham County towns — Windham, Mansfield, Coventry, Columbia, Lebanon, Chaplin and nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Give us a call.
