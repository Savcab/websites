Good to know

Common questions

Q.Do you handle trees that are hard to get to?

Yes — that's a big part of what we do. Hillside trees, trees with no equipment access, and removals over houses or through a neighbor's yard. We rope and lower pieces where there's no room to fell.

Q.How much does tree removal cost?

Every tree is different — size, lean, access, and what's underneath all matter. We give a fair, upfront price after looking at the job, so you know the cost before any work starts.

Q.Can you come out after a storm?

Yes. If a tree is down on your home, blocking the drive, or split and dangerous, call us — we take emergency and storm calls around the clock to make the property safe.

Q.Do you clean up and haul the debris away?

Always. Brush gets hauled off, logs cut to size if you'd like the firewood, and the site raked clean. We don't leave you with a mess to deal with.

Q.Do you grind the stump too?

We can. Stump grinding is available with any removal so you're not left with a trip hazard or regrowth — just let us know when we quote the job.

Q.How do I get a quote?

Call or text (304) 573-0771. A couple of photos of the tree and its surroundings help us give you an accurate price fast.
