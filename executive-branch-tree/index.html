What we do

Complete tree care, start to finish

One crew for the whole job — assess the tree, do the work safely, and leave the yard cleaner than we found it. Here's what we take on most around Sheridan.

Tree Removal

Safe, controlled takedowns of dead, dying, leaning or crowding trees — rigged and lowered piece by piece when there's a structure or fence below.

Trimming & Pruning

Crown thinning, deadwood removal, clearance from roofs and power lines, and hedge work — shaped for health, light and a stronger structure.

Large & Hazardous Trees

The tall, heavy and high-stakes jobs — an 85-foot cottonwood split over a house, a giant too close to the roof. We've got the reach and the rigging.

Stump Grinding

Grind the leftover stump and surface roots below grade so you get your lawn, garden bed or parking space back — no eyesore, no trip hazard.

Storm Damage Response

When a tree comes down in a windstorm and threatens the house, we'll come out to triage, give a bid and clear the hazard — often the same day you call.

Haul-Off & Cleanup

Brush, limbs and debris chipped and hauled, ruts raked out and the site swept down — the part too many crews skip, and the part our regulars notice.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you handle large or dangerous trees near my house?
Yes — the big, high-stakes trees are our specialty. We've taken down an 85-foot cottonwood that split over a house in a windstorm. We rig and lower heavy limbs in a controlled way so nothing comes down on a roof, fence or vehicle.
How fast can you come out for a storm-damaged tree?
When a tree is threatening a house, we move quickly — we've come out the same day a customer called to triage the hazard and give a bid. Call us at (307) 217-2058 and tell us what's going on; urgent hazards get priority.
Do you clean up afterward, or do I deal with the mess?
We clean up. Brush and limbs are chipped and hauled, the ground is raked, and the site is left tidy. Cleanup is part of the job for us — it's one of the things our long-time customers mention most.
Can you grind the stump after a removal?
Yes. After a removal we can grind the stump and surface roots below grade so you can reclaim the spot for lawn, a garden bed or a clear walkway — no leftover stump to mow around or trip over.
What are your hours, and how do I get a quote?
We're available Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The easiest way to get a quote is to call (307) 217-2058 or send the short form on this page — we'll set up an on-site look and give you a clear, fair bid.
