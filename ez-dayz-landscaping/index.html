Frequently asked questions

Do you serve both homes and businesses?

Yes. EZ Dayz handles residential yards and commercial properties across Rock Springs and Southwest Wyoming, from one-time installs to scheduled maintenance.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (307) 448-7834 or fill out the free-estimate form. We'll look at your property, talk through options, and give you an honest written quote before any work starts.

What services do you offer?

Lawn care and mowing, fertilization, weed and pest control, core aeration, dethatching and power raking, garden and bed design, sod and new-lawn installs, and ongoing grounds care.

Do you build custom plans?

Always. Instead of fixed packages, we work with you to create a plan around your specific property, goals and budget.

What areas do you cover?

Rock Springs, Green River, Reliance and the wider Sweetwater County area. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask when you call.

When can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job, but we'll give you a realistic timeline with your estimate and schedule a date that works for you.
