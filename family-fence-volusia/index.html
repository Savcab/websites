Good to Know

Fence questions, answered

How much does a new fence cost?

It depends on the material, the length of your property line, and your terrain. Chain-link is the most budget-friendly; wood and vinyl land in the middle; ornamental aluminum is typically the premium pick. We give a clear, itemized estimate after we walk your yard — free, with no pressure.

Which fence material is best for Florida?

All four hold up here when they’re installed right. Vinyl and aluminum shrug off sun and humidity with almost no upkeep. Wood gives you that classic look and stays beautiful when it’s sealed and stained — which we handle. We’ll talk through the trade-offs so you pick what fits your yard and budget.

Do you stain and seal wood fences?

Yes — and it’s something we’re known for. Staining and sealing protect a wood fence from Florida weather and keep it looking new for years. We can finish a fence we build, or come stain an existing one.

Do you repair existing fences?

We do. Leaning posts, broken panels, sagging gates, storm damage — we’ll take a look and tell you honestly whether a repair or a section replacement makes more sense.

How long does a fence install take?

Most residential fences go in within a few days once materials are on hand, depending on the size and material. If a supplier puts us on backorder, we’ll tell you up front and keep you posted on timing.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in De Leon Springs and serve homeowners across west Volusia County — DeLand, Deltona, Orange City, DeBary, Pierson, Lake Helen and the surrounding area. Call us if you’re nearby and we’ll let you know.
