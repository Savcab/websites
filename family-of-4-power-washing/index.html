The Right Way to Clean a Florida Roof

Soft wash — not pressure — on your roof

Florida sun and humidity grow black algae streaks and mildew across roofs, walls and screen enclosures. Blasting them with high pressure can strip shingle granules, force water where it shouldn't go and leave streaky damage behind.

  • Low-pressure soft wash safely lifts algae, mold and mildew at the root — gentle on shingles, tile, stucco and screens.
  • Longer-lasting results — treating the growth, not just rinsing it, keeps surfaces cleaner for longer.
  • The right pressure for every surface — soft wash where it's needed, surface cleaning where it helps.

Questions Sebring homeowners ask

Do you pressure wash or soft wash my roof?

Roofs get a soft wash, never high-pressure blasting. Low-pressure cleaning with the right solution lifts black algae streaks and mildew at the root while protecting your shingles or tile. High pressure can strip granules and damage a roof — so we don't do it.

What surfaces can you clean?

Roofs, house siding and stucco, pool cages and lanais, paver and concrete driveways and walkways, fences, decks, and commercial exteriors. If it's outside and it's dirty, ask us — we match the right method to each surface.

Are you insured?

Yes — we're insured. We also take care to protect your plants, screens and finishes while we work.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Sebring and serve the surrounding Highlands County area, including Avon Park, Lake Placid, Placid Lakes, Lorida and Venus. Not sure if you're in range? Just call and ask.

How often should I have my roof or house cleaned?

In Florida's heat and humidity, many homeowners have exterior surfaces cleaned once a year or so to stay ahead of algae and mildew. Because a soft wash treats the growth rather than just rinsing it, results last longer between cleanings.
