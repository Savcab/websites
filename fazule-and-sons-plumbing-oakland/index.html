Our story

Two decades of doing it right in Oakland

Anthony "Fazule" Lopez spent more than fifteen years learning the trade in New York City before bringing his craft to the East Bay. Twenty-plus years later, Fazule & Sons is the shop Oakland neighbors recommend to each other — the kind of plumber who shows up, explains the fix, and stands behind the work.

In 2024, that reputation earned the title of Best of the East Bay Plumbing Company. It's family-run, it's local, and it treats your home the way it'd treat its own.
