Good to know

Fence questions, answered.

How fast can you install my fence?

We aim to get you a quote fast — often the same day you call — and many installs are scheduled for the following week. Exact timing depends on fence length, material availability and weather, and we'll give you a real date at your estimate.

What types of fence do you build?

Wood and privacy fences, vinyl, aluminum/ornamental, and chain-link — plus matching gates. We'll help you pick the material that fits your goal, whether that's privacy, security, pets, or curb appeal.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. On-site estimates are free. We walk the property line with you, talk through materials and gates, and give you a clear price with no pressure.

Do you install gates too?

Yes — single walk gates, double gates and drive gates that match your fence. They're hung level and set up to latch and swing cleanly.

Can you repair an existing fence?

Often, yes. Leaning posts, storm-damaged sections, sagging gates and rotted boards can usually be repaired or replaced. Send a photo with your quote request and we'll let you know the best fix.

What areas do you serve?

Dalton and the surrounding Northwest Georgia communities — including Tunnel Hill, Rocky Face, Varnell, Cohutta, Chatsworth, Ringgold and the rest of Whitfield County. Call us if you're nearby and we'll confirm.
