What we do

Lawn & landscape care, done right.

Everything your yard needs to look its best — on a schedule that suits you, or as a one-time reset.

Lawn Maintenance & Mowing

Regular mowing with adjusted height, alternating patterns and sharp blades — plus clean edging to keep your lawn full, green and healthy.

Landscape Design & Install

Small landscape design with plant, mulch, landscape-rock and sod installation — the work that brings a tired yard back to life.

Yard Cleanups & Debris Removal

Thorough removal of debris, waste and clutter — from routine tidy-ups to a full seasonal reset that leaves the whole property neat.

Trimming & Pruning

Small palm trimming, hedge and bush maintenance, and careful pruning that encourages healthy, tidy growth.

Weeding & Bed Care

Meticulous weeding and bed upkeep that keeps planting beds clean, healthy and looking cared-for all year.

Pressure Washing

Driveways, walkways and home exteriors washed clean — an easy add-on when the crew is already on site.
