Our promise

“Create a backdrop for your memories.”

That’s the standard Zach & Jessica Darr set when they founded 1:16 Lawn Care in 2015 — transform a property, deliver an exceptional experience, and show Christ’s love in everything we do. A decade and 100 five-star reviews later, it’s still how every yard gets treated.

Our story

A family business, rooted in faith

Zach & Jessica Darr started 1:16 Lawn Care in 2015 with a simple passion: serve their community well and raise a family while they did it. In 2022 they brought that same heart to Southwest Florida, planting roots in Bonita Springs.

The name comes from Romans 1:16. Faith is central to who we are — it shapes how we treat your property, your time and our own team. We’re not the biggest company in town, and our customers tell us that’s exactly why they stay.
