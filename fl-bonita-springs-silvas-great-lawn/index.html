What we do

Full-service lawn & landscape care

From weekly mowing to full property cleanups, Silva's crew keeps Southwest Florida yards healthy, tidy and ready to show — whether it's your home or a rental you manage.

Lawn Maintenance

Reliable mowing, edging, trimming and blow-down on a schedule that keeps every yard sharp — residential and commercial.

Landscaping & Sod

Sod installation, plant and shrub beds, and fresh design work to transform tired yards into clean, finished landscapes.

Mulch & Rock

Mulch and decorative rock installations that define beds, hold moisture and give the whole property a crisp, maintained look.

Trimming, shaping and removal to keep palms and trees safe, healthy and clear of roofs, lines and walkways.
