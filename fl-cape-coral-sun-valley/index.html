Your natural choice in landscaping — for more than twenty years.

Sun Valley Landscaping is a design-build firm led by Mark Ursitti, working across Cape Coral and Southwest Florida since 2003. We start every project as a design — how the planting frames the house, how the hardscape and lighting work together, and how water moves off a flat, canal-rich lot — then build it with our own crew.

From a full front-yard redesign to a paver patio, a drainage fix or a community entrance, it's one team from the first walk-through to the final cleanup. That's how a 5.0 Google rating gets earned one yard at a time.

How we work

From a complimentary plan to a finished yard.

A clear path from the first look at your property to the day it's done and cleaned up.

On-site consultation

We come out, walk the property and talk through what you're picturing — no charge, no pressure — and put together a complimentary plan and estimate.

Design the plan

We design how planting, hardscape, lighting and drainage work together for your lot, so you can see the whole picture before anything is installed.

Build it right

Our own crew handles the install — beds, pavers, sod, irrigation and lighting — built with quality materials to hold up in our climate.

Walk it & clean up

We finish the details, clean the site down, and hand back a landscape that's ready to enjoy — with upkeep available if you want us to keep it sharp.
