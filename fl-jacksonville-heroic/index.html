01  What we do

Everything your property needs, handled by one crew

We keep it simple: show up on schedule, do the work right, and leave the place better than we found it. Here's where homeowners across Jacksonville put us to work.

Lawn Care & Maintenance

Mowing, edging, trimming and seasonal cleanups on a steady schedule, so your St. Augustine or Zoysia stays thick, even and green through Florida's long growing season.

Irrigation Systems

Sprinkler installs, tune-ups and repairs that get every zone covered — no dry corners, no soggy spots, no runaway water bill from a head spraying the driveway.

Landscape Design & Installation

Fresh beds, new sod, mulch, plantings and clean borders. We rework tired front yards and bare side lots into a finished, cared-for look that lifts the whole property.

Landscape & Holiday Lighting

Path, accent and seasonal holiday lighting that brings the yard to life after dark — and shows the house off at its best from the street.

02  Who we are

An owner-run crew that treats your yard like our own

Heroic Landscapes is a Jacksonville-based, owner-run team. We're not a national franchise rolling through the neighborhood — we live and work here, and we take it personally when a lawn on our route doesn't look right.

That's how a small crew earns 47 five-star reviews: we show up when we say we will, do the work the way we'd want it done at our own place, and clean up before we leave. Most of our new work comes from a neighbor pointing over the fence and saying, "they did mine."
