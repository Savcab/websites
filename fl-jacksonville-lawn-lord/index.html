What we do

One crew for the whole property

From a tidy weekly mow to a full sod renovation, Lawn Lord handles the outside of your home so you don't have to chase six different contractors.

Lawn Care & Maintenance

Routine mowing, edging, fertilization, aeration, and weed, fungus & insect treatments to keep St. Augustine and Zoysia turf thick and green.

Sodding & Lawn Renovation

Fresh sod laid and graded for a clean, instant lawn — ideal for bare patches, new builds, or a full front-yard reset.

Pressure Washing

Driveways, walkways, patios, fences and roofs brought back to life — stripping years of grime, mildew and algae build-up.

Tree, Palm & Hedge Trimming

Palm skinning, tree limb removal and hedge shaping done cleanly and hauled away — handled with proper lift equipment.

Mulch & Rock Installation

Fresh mulch, decorative rock and clean bed edging that frame the house and cut down on weeds and washouts.

Gutter & Roof Cleanup

Gutter clearing and roof blow-offs to keep water moving and leaves off the shingles — part of true property maintenance.
