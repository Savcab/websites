What we cover

One landscape crew for the whole property.

From weekly maintenance to a full design-and-install, you get all of it from us — on a schedule, with the same crew who already knows your grounds.

Lawn & landscape maintenance

Full-service weekly upkeep — mowing, edging, shrub trimming and weed control — so common areas, frontage and lawns stay sharp without you chasing us down.

Landscape design & install

Custom landscape design with plant, shrub and tree installation, fresh beds and new sod — chosen for Northeast Florida's soil, sun and climate.

Pavers & hardscape

Paver walkways, patios, driveways and lot work — built on a properly prepped, compacted base so it stays level and lasts through Florida rain.

Landscape lighting

Low-voltage landscape lighting that adds curb appeal and security — entrances, walkways and beds that still read well after dark.
