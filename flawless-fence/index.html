Good to Know

Fence questions, answered

Do you really finish most fences in a day?

Most residential installs — including larger lots with multiple gates — we complete start to finish in a single day. Bigger or more complex projects can take longer, and we’ll always tell you the realistic timeline up front when we quote.

What fence materials do you install?

Vinyl (privacy and picket), aluminum and ornamental, wood, and chain-link. Vinyl privacy fencing is our specialty, but we’ll walk you through the best option for your yard, budget and look.

Can you build custom and extra-wide gates?

Yes — gates are one of our specialties. We build walk gates, drive gates, and extra-wide double gates, including wheeled rolling gates sized for the exact opening you need.

How much does a new fence cost?

It depends on the material, the length of the run, and how many gates you need. That’s why we come out and give you a free, itemized estimate — so the price you see is the price for your actual project, with no surprises.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in National Park, NJ and serve homeowners throughout the surrounding Gloucester County area — Westville, Woodbury, Deptford, Brooklawn and nearby towns. Call us and we’ll confirm we cover your address.
