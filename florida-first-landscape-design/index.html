About Florida First

A local, owner-run design-build crew

We’re a Homosassa design-build landscaper, established in 1991 and serving all of Citrus County. Owner John and his crew do the design, the hardscape and the planting themselves — so the person who plans your waterfall or patio is the same person on site when it’s built.

Over three decades we’ve leaned into what works in this climate: paver patios and driveways, stone and block retaining walls, water gardens, and xeriscape plantings that shrug off Florida’s water restrictions. Free estimates, honest guidance, and a yard you’ll actually want to spend time in.
