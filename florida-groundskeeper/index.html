What we do

One crew for the design, the lighting and everything after.

From the first planting plan to the sprinklers and the evening lighting, we handle the whole yard — and we're the ones homeowners keep calling to look after it.

Design & installation

We plan and plant the whole landscape — beds, palms, sod and stone — built around how your yard actually gets used, then installed by our own crew.

Landscape lighting

Low-voltage lighting laid out to show off the house and the plantings after dark — programmable fixtures you can dim and change colors on, with a walk-through so you know how to run them.

Irrigation systems

Sprinkler and drip irrigation that gets water where the plants need it and nowhere they don't — installed, tuned and serviced so nothing dries out between visits.

Grounds care & maintenance

Ongoing mowing, trimming, mulch and bed care that keeps a finished landscape looking finished — the same crew back on a schedule that fits the property.

Design that fills in

We plant it to look right the day we finish — and better every season after.

A good bed isn't just plants in a row. We set the right palms and shrubs for the spot and the light, edge it clean against the lawn, and lay it out so it reads full and finished as it grows in.
