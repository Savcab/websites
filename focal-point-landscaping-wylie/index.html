How we work

Designed on paper, then built to last.

A straightforward path from the first look at your yard to the day you start using it.

Walk the site

We come look at the space, talk through what you're picturing, and figure out how the patio, the grade and the drainage want to work together.

Design & estimate

You get a clear plan and a written estimate — materials, layout and price laid out, no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons once we start.

Build on a base

We excavate, compact the sub-base, set the grade for drainage, and lay the pavers, stone or block by hand — the slow part, done right.

Hand it back

We finish the details, clean the site down, and leave you a patio, wall or green that's ready to use and built to stay put.
