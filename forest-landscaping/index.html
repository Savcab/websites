Frequently asked questions

Do you install both sprinklers and sod together?

Yes — it's what we do best. We design and install the irrigation system, prep and grade the soil, then lay fresh sod, all with one crew so the timing and watering line up perfectly.

How much does a new lawn or landscape cost?

Every yard is different, so we give you a free, no-pressure on-site estimate. We'll measure the space, talk through what you want, and put a clear price in writing.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Middleton and work throughout the Treasure Valley — Boise, Nampa, Caldwell, Star, Eagle, Meridian and the surrounding communities. Call us if you're nearby and we'll let you know.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Forest Landscaping LLC is a licensed and insured Idaho company, so your property and the crew working on it are covered.

Can you handle the whole project, from dirt to finished yard?

Absolutely. From bare ground to a finished landscape — irrigation, sod, paver patios, retaining walls, rock beds and plantings — you deal with one team from start to finish.
