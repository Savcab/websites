Good to know

Stump grinding questions, answered

What does stump grinding cost?

Price depends on the stump's diameter, how many you have, and the access to it. That's exactly why estimates are free — we come look, give you a clear number up front, and you decide. No surprises after the work.

How soon can you get to my stump?

Usually quickly. Reviewers describe getting an estimate the next day and the job finished within a few days of reaching out. Call or message and we'll tell you the soonest we can be there.

Do you grind the stump all the way out?

We grind several inches below ground level so the stump is gone for everyday purposes — you can reseed, re-sod, mulch, or plant over it. We can also grind surface roots flush so the lawn mows clean.

What happens to the grindings and the mess?

We haul off the grindings and rake the area smooth as part of the job — you're left with a tidy spot, not a pile. If you'd rather keep the grindings as mulch, just let us know.

Is it safe to grind near utility lines or sprinklers?

Yes — when a stump sits near a line, we have the utilities marked before grinding so nothing underground gets damaged. Point out any sprinkler heads or wires at the estimate and we'll work around them.

What areas do you cover?

Pottsville and the surrounding Arkansas River Valley — Russellville, Dover, Atkins, London, Hector and nearby Pope County communities. If you're close by, give us a call and we'll let you know.
