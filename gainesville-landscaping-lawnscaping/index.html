What we do

From a clean weekly cut to a full landscape.

We keep the regular work dialed in and take on the bigger projects too — design, sod, irrigation and stone. Here's where homeowners and businesses around Gainesville put us to work.

Lawn care & maintenance

Mowing, edging and seasonal clean-ups on a schedule that keeps your lawn sharp — the recurring visit that takes the yard off your weekend list.

Landscape design & planting

Beds, foundation plantings and full landscape installs — we plan what actually fills in and holds up in a North Florida yard, then put it in the ground.

Sod & mulch installation

Fresh sod for a bare or patchy yard and mulch that finishes the beds — laid to root in and hold, whether it's a strip along the house or the whole backyard.

Irrigation & hardscaping

Sprinkler repairs and new irrigation, plus paver paths, stone borders and retaining walls that give the green a clean, lasting edge.
