What we do

Yard care built for high-desert living

From a weekly mow to a full property cleanup, Gambel's handles the upkeep that keeps Chino Valley yards looking their best — without the green-lawn fantasy that doesn't belong out here.

Mowing & line trimming

Clean, even cuts and crisp edges along walks, fences and beds — the reliable weekly work neighbors keep calling us back for.

Desert & rock landscaping

Xeriscape upkeep, decorative rock, gravel refresh and shaping the junipers and natives so your front yard reads tidy, not overgrown.
