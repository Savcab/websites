Hardscape done properly

The base is the part you don't see — and it's everything.

Anyone can drop stone on dirt. We grade, compact and set a proper base so your patio or walkway drains right and stays flat — not sunken and lifting two seasons later.

  • Real base prep & grading — compacted sub-base and proper slope so water runs away from the house.
  • Clean edges & joints — locked-in borders so pavers and flagstone don't creep or spread.
  • Tidy job site — we haul off the spoil and leave your yard clean, not a mess of dirt piles.
