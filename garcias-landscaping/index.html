Questions we hear a lot

Do you charge for estimates?

No — estimates are free. Call or send the form and we'll come look, talk through what you want, and give you a straight price with no pressure.

How fast can you start?

For smaller jobs like a mow or trim, it's often within a few days of the estimate. Larger projects get scheduled around the scope — we'll give you a realistic timeline up front.

Do you handle both lawn care and big projects?

Yes. The same crew that mows weekly also builds patios, walls, walkways and drainage and installs sod and full landscape designs — so everything matches and one team owns the result.

What areas do you cover?

Calhoun and the surrounding Gordon County towns — Adairsville, Resaca, Fairmount, Ranger, Plainville and nearby. If you're close, just ask.

Can you take on overgrown or neglected lots?

Absolutely. Big, overgrown properties a riding mower can't handle are right in our wheelhouse — we'll get it cut back, cleaned up and back under control.
