The studio

We design the garden around the home — the right plant in the right place — then plant it, pave it and keep it thriving.

Garden Design Pros is a West Palm Beach landscape and garden design company. We handle the whole yard: design and planting, paver walkways and patios, irrigation repair, pressure washing and paver sealing, and refreshes that give a home its curb appeal back.

What we do

Design, build and refresh.

Garden & planting design

We plan the beds and pick plants suited to Florida sun and your soil, so the design fills in and thrives instead of struggling.

Pavers & patios

Paver walkways, driveways and patios installed clean and level — the hardscape that frames the planting.

Irrigation

Sprinkler installs and repairs that keep new plantings watered and the lawn even through the dry months.

Pressure wash & seal

Pressure washing and sealing for paver drives, patios and pool decks to bring back the color and protect the surface.

Rock & xeriscape beds

River-rock and low-water beds with succulents and structure that look sharp and cut down on upkeep.

Landscape refresh

Moved into a home with tired landscaping? We reset the beds, replace what's failing and clean it all up.
