What we do

Everything your yard needs, from one crew.

Garden Worx handles the whole project in-house — the plan, the planting, the stonework and the lighting — so the finished yard actually looks like it was designed to go together.

Landscape Design & Installation

Full front- and backyard transformations: drought-tolerant and traditional planting plans, sod, decomposed granite, trees and beds — designed and installed end to end.

Hardscape & Stonework

Flagstone paths, paver walkways, patios and borders that give the yard structure and hold up to everyday use, wired to fit the planting around them.

Landscape Lighting

Low-voltage path and accent lighting that makes the yard usable after dark and shows off the trees, walls and planting you just paid to install.

Irrigation, Sod & Maintenance

Efficient irrigation, fresh sod, and ongoing residential and commercial upkeep — so the space stays as green as the day the crew finished it.
