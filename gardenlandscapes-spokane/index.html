What We Do

Garden design, and everything it takes to plant it.

GardenLandscapes designs and makes gardens with great care, thought, and attention to detail — then builds them, waters them right, and sets the bones in stone.

Custom Garden Design

Gardens designed for your space, light, and how you actually use the yard — plant palettes chosen with real thought, not a truck full of the same five shrubs.

Lawn-to-Garden Conversion

Trade thirsty, high-maintenance turf for a planted garden that gives back — more color, more life, and far less to mow. Bold at first, best decision ever after.

Drip Irrigation

Old sprinklers converted to efficient drip that puts water where roots need it — less waste, healthier plants, and a lower water bill through a Spokane summer.

Hardscape & Walkways

Paths, edging, and stonework that give a garden structure and let you move through it — the hard lines that make the soft planting sing.

Planted for Every Season

A garden that changes with the year.

The best gardens are never the same twice — spring bulbs, summer bloom, grasses catching the fall light, structure that carries through winter. We plant for the whole year, not just the day the truck leaves.
