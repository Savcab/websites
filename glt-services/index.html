What We Do

Tree work, handled start to finish

From a single dead limb over the driveway to a full-grown elm crowding the house, GLT Services has the equipment and the careful approach to get it done without tearing up your property.

Tree Removal

Large and hazardous removals are our specialty — including big elms and trees growing tight against a house, fence, or power line. Sectioned down safely with a bucket truck and rigging, then hauled away.

Trimming & Pruning

Shape and thin your trees to keep them healthy, clear the roofline and power lines, and take the weight off limbs hanging over the house before the next Kansas wind storm rolls through.

Stump Grinding

Don't leave a stump behind to trip over or sprout back. We grind it down below grade so you can reseed, replant, or just have your yard back flat and usable.

Lot & Storm Cleanup

Overgrown lot, fallen limbs after a storm, or brush that's gotten away from you — we clear it, chip it, and clean up the mess so the property is ready to use again.
