02 — Our story

Rooted in Florence since 1998.

Granger Landscapes was founded in 1998 by Brandon Granger. After studying horticulture at NC State University, he returned home to Florence in 2002 to run the company full-time — and we've been designing, building and caring for landscapes across the Pee Dee ever since.

Over the years we've completed everything from small backyard gardens to large commercial and retail developments. We're a full-service company, from the design process to installing the landscape you've dreamed about — and keeping it beautiful long after.
