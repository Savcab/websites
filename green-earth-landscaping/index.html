Good to know

Questions, answered.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Cornville and work throughout the Verde Valley — Cottonwood, Sedona, Village of Oak Creek, Clarkdale, Camp Verde, Lake Montezuma and the surrounding area. If you're close by and aren't sure, just give us a call.

Do you handle both landscaping and construction?

Yes — that's our whole approach. From grading, drainage and site prep to hardscape, planting and ongoing care, one accountable crew handles the project start to finish, so you're not coordinating multiple contractors for a single yard.

Are estimates really free?

They are. We'll come out, look at your property, talk through what's realistic, and give you a clear written estimate before any work begins — no pressure and no obligation.

Can you design a low-water, desert-friendly yard?

Absolutely. Most of our Verde Valley work leans on native and drought-tolerant plantings, decorative rock and efficient drip irrigation — landscapes designed for our soil, sun and water, so they look great without a heavy water bill.

Do you take on big clean-up and haul-off jobs?

Yes — overgrown lots, new-purchase clean-ups, debris and green-waste removal with full haul-off are some of the most common jobs investors and new owners call us for. Tell us what you've got and we'll scope it out.

How do I get started?

Call or text us at (928) 634-4375, or fill out the free-estimate form on this page. We'll set up a time to walk the property and put together your estimate.
