Frequently asked questions

What does “organic” weed treatment actually mean?

It means controlling weeds and broadleaf without harsh synthetic chemicals on your lawn. Customers regularly tell us their lawn became noticeably weed-free and greener within about two weeks of the first application — results you can see, with a yard that’s friendlier for kids and pets.

Do you offer recurring mowing, or one-time visits?

Both. Most customers go with regular weekly or bi-weekly mowing through the season so their lawn always looks its best, but we’re glad to handle one-time cleanups or a single visit when that’s what you need.

How do I get a price for my lawn?

Give us a call or text at (937) 243-9330, or send the quick estimate form on this page. We’ll look at your property, talk through what it needs, and give you a fair, honest price — with no upselling.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Bellefontaine and serve the surrounding Logan County communities, including West Liberty, Huntsville, Russells Point, Lakeview, Zanesfield, and DeGraff. If you’re nearby and not sure, just ask.

When can I reach you?

We’re available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. If we’re out on a job when you call, leave a message or send a text and we’ll get right back to you.
