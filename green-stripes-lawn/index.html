What We Do

Lawn care that covers the whole yard

From the weekly cut to the seasonal cleanup, Green Stripes is a true full-service crew — so you can hand off the whole yard to one dependable team.

Mowing & Striping

Clean, even cuts on a schedule that fits your yard — finished with the crisp stripes that put the green in Green Stripes.

Fertilization & Weed Control

Treatments that feed the turf and keep weeds in check, so your grass comes in thicker, greener, and tougher through the season.

Edging & Yard Cleanup

Sharp edges along walks and beds, plus leaf and debris cleanup that leaves the whole property looking finished — not just mowed.

Flower Beds & Mulch

Bed shaping, fresh mulch, and tidy plantings that frame the lawn and lift the curb appeal of the entire front yard.

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Shaping, thinning, and trimming shrubs and hedges to keep them healthy and neat — including spacing crowded plantings the right way.

Pressure Washing

Soft, careful washing for siding, walkways, and driveways — finishing touches that make a freshly cared-for yard really shine.

Good to Know

Questions homeowners ask us

Q.How do I get a price?

Call us or fill out the form with your address and what you need. We'll come take a look and give you a free, no-pressure estimate — no commitment required.

Q.Do you offer recurring service?

Yes. Most customers put their yard on a regular schedule so it always looks its best, but we're happy to talk through what makes sense for your property.

Q.Is it really full-service?

It is. Beyond mowing, we handle fertilization and weed control, edging and cleanups, beds and mulch, shrub trimming, and pressure washing — one crew for the whole yard.

Q.What areas do you cover?

We serve Florence and the surrounding Pee Dee communities. If you're nearby and not sure whether you're in range, just reach out and ask.

Q.How soon can you start?

Our customers regularly mention how quickly we respond and schedule. Give us a call and we'll let you know our soonest availability for your area.
