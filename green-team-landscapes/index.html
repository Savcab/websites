About Green Team

Wellington's tropical landscape team since 2008

Green Team Landscapes has been designing and caring for Palm Beach County gardens for over 18 years. We specialize in tropical landscapes and equestrian properties — the plants that belong here, installed and maintained the right way so they keep looking their best.

Founder Luc Limbourg and the team handle each property from design through the seasons that follow, which is why so many clients keep us on for ongoing care long after the install is done.
