What we do

Full-service landscaping for your whole property

Most of our customers come to us with a yard that needs more than one thing. We handle the entire project — so the design, the dirt work, the drainage and the upkeep all come from the same crew that knows your property.

Landscape Design & Install

New beds, plantings, shrubs and trees laid out and installed to transform the look of your yard — planned around how your space actually drains and grows.

Lawn Care & Maintenance

Mowing, trimming, edging and seasonal cleanups that keep your property sharp week after week, without you having to chase it.

Drainage & Irrigation

French drains, grading and irrigation to move water where it belongs — so heavy rain stops pooling against the house and your plants get watered right.

Retaining Walls & Hardscape

Stacked-stone and block retaining walls that hold back slopes, level out usable space and frame your beds with a clean, finished edge.

Bed Renovation & Mulch

Old, overgrown beds cleared out and rebuilt — fresh mulch or river rock, crisp steel or stone edging, and the right plants for the spot.

Tree & Shrub Trimming

Overgrown crape myrtles, ornamental grasses and shrubs trimmed and shaped to keep your landscape healthy and looking its best.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you handle the whole project, or just one part?

The whole thing. Most of our jobs need design, dirt work, drainage and planting — and we'd rather one crew own all of it than hand you off halfway. You make one call and we take it from there.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the size and scope of the work, so we don't quote sight-unseen. We'll come walk your property, talk through what you want, and put together a clear free estimate before any work starts.

Can you fix water pooling or drainage problems?

Yes. Drainage and irrigation are a big part of what we do — French drains, grading and getting water to move away from the house instead of sitting against your foundation or drowning your beds.

Do you offer ongoing lawn maintenance?

We do. After we finish a project, plenty of customers have us keep the property maintained — mowing, trimming and seasonal cleanups so the new landscape stays looking right.

What areas do you serve?

Tupelo and the surrounding Lee County communities, including Saltillo, Belden, Plantersville, Verona, Guntown and Shannon. If you're nearby, give us a call and we'll let you know.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Green Team is licensed and insured, so you're covered and the work is done right.
