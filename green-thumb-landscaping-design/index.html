The studio

Landscaping that homeowners call the work of an artist.

Green Thumb Landscaping & Design is a design-first studio. We start by understanding how you want to live in your yard, then draw it — offering renderings and design options before a single plant goes in the ground.

From there we choose the plantings, advise on irrigation and install it all ourselves, so what gets built matches what was drawn. Reviewers describe front and back yards completely transformed, with expertise shared at every step.
