What we do

Complete tree care for Hill Country properties

From a single hazardous limb over your roofline to clearing a wooded lot — and replanting it with the right native trees — we handle the whole life of a tree.

Tree Removal

Safe takedown of dead, dying, leaning or hazardous trees — tight spaces and big live oaks alike, with the yard left clean.

Trimming & Pruning

Structural pruning, canopy thinning, deadwooding and clearance — done the way that keeps a tree healthy for decades, not just tidy for a season.

Stump Grinding

Grind that leftover stump below grade so you get your yard back — no tripping hazard, no regrowth, ready to replant.

Storm & Emergency

What sets us apart

A working native tree nursery — not just a chainsaw crew

Most tree companies only take trees down. We grow them too. Out at our place in Bertram we raise field-grown native Texas trees — live oaks, cedar elms, pecans, bald cypress and more — the species that actually belong in Hill Country soil and survive our summers.

It means when we remove a tree, we can replant the right one. And it's a big part of why folks here trust us with the trees they care about — our nursery is one reason customers single us out in their reviews.
