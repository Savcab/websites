Frequently asked questions

How much does a stamped concrete patio or walkway cost?

Every project is priced on size, site access, the finish you choose and how much base prep is needed. The honest answer is we don’t guess over the phone — we come out, measure, talk through options, and give you a clear written estimate at no charge.

What’s the difference between stamped concrete and pavers?

Pavers are individual units set in sand; stamped concrete is one continuous pour textured and colored to look like stone, brick or wood. Stamped concrete has far fewer joints, which means fewer places for weeds and shifting — and it’s typically lower maintenance over the years.

Will the concrete crack or heave in winter?

Central New York winters are hard on concrete, which is exactly why base prep matters so much. We compact a proper sub-base, build in drainage and control joints, and seal the surface — the steps that keep cracking and settling from coming back.

Do you do repairs, or only new installs?

Both. Alongside new patios, walkways and pads, we handle driveway and concrete repair and resurfacing. If you’re not sure whether something can be repaired or should be replaced, we’ll tell you straight at the walkthrough.
