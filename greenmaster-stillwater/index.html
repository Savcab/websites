Frequently asked questions

Do you install new irrigation systems, or only repair them?

Both. Through our Stillwater Sprinkler division we design and install complete in-ground systems on Rain Bird equipment, and we also repair and tune existing systems — heads, valves, leaks, controllers and coverage problems. Call us and we'll figure out which makes sense for your yard.

What does a lawn-care program include?

Our programs are built around fertilization and weed control on a seasonal schedule, and can fold in aeration, over-seeding, soil testing and pest treatments depending on what your lawn needs. We offer a few package sizes — many customers start smaller and build up as they see results.

How soon will I see a difference?

It depends on where the lawn is starting. Customers with rough lawns often tell us they saw real improvement within the first season — turf science takes a little time, but the results hold once the watering, feeding and mowing are all working together.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Stillwater and serve the city plus the surrounding Payne County communities. If you're nearby and not sure whether you're in range, just call — we'll give you a straight answer.
