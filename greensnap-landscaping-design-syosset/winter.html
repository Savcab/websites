Our Seasonal Approach

1

Fall Assessment

We evaluate your landscape to identify areas needing attention before winter—debris removal, plant protection, and hardscape preparation.

2

Cleanup & Preparation

Leaf removal, bed cleanup, perennial cutback, and protection of sensitive plantings to ensure your garden is ready for cold weather.

3

Winter Monitoring

Regular checks during the season to address any storm damage, ice buildup, or other seasonal challenges affecting your property.

4

As weather warms, we prepare beds for spring planting, remove winter protection, and restore your landscape to full beauty.
