What we tend

From weekly upkeep to the projects that reshape a yard.

We keep the regular work dialed in and take on the bigger builds too — patios, walls, water features and the trees most crews won't touch. Here's where Topeka and Lawrence homeowners put us to work.

Lawn maintenance

Mowing, edging, fertilizing and seasonal cleanups on a schedule that keeps your grass thick, even and off your weekend list.

Tree care & removal

Trimming, pruning and full removals with the right equipment — we clear hazard and overgrown trees and leave the yard cleaner than we found it.

Landscape design & hardscape

Patios, retaining walls and water features, designed and built to fit your space — the stone and structure that make a yard read as finished.

Sprinkler & irrigation

Sprinkler installs, tune-ups and repairs that put water where it needs to go, so a healthy lawn doesn't ride on the weather.

Sod & seeding

Fresh sod and overseeding to fill in thin, patchy turf and give you a full green lawn that holds up through a Kansas summer.

Outdoor living

Spaces that pull you outside after dark.

A patio isn't finished until it's somewhere you want to sit. We build the fireplaces, fire features and water elements that turn a backyard into a room — set in stone to last through Kansas seasons.
