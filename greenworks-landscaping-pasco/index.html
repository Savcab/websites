What we build

Landscapes, patios and the outdoor rooms in between.

From the first design to the last stone set, we build outdoor spaces you actually live in — because for us, customer service is always in season. Here's the work homeowners around the Tri-Cities bring us in for.

Landscape design & installation

We start with a plan for the whole yard — grading, plantings and layout — then install it start to finish, so it comes together as one space instead of a patchwork of weekend projects.

Patios & pathways

Paver and flagstone patios, walkways and sitting areas laid on a base built to stay level — the ground floor of every backyard we build.

Fire pits & outdoor kitchens

Stone fire pits, built-in grills and full outdoor kitchens in stacked-stone veneer with stone counters — the reason the backyard stays busy long after dark.

Retaining walls & stonework

Block and natural-stone retaining walls that hold a slope, carve out usable yard and work in steps where you need them — set on a footing built to last.

Irrigation & water features

Irrigation that keeps everything green through a Tri-Cities summer, plus ponds and boulder water features with lighting that turn the yard on in the evenings.

Landscape maintenance

Once it's in, we keep it looking that way — mowing, trimming, seasonal cleanups and the steady upkeep that protects the work you paid for.

The outdoor room

Backyards built to use, not just look at.

A cedar pergola over a stacked-stone fireplace turns a bare patio into a room you'll use most of the year. We design and build the whole thing — footings, stone, timber and finish — to stand up to Tri-Cities weather.
