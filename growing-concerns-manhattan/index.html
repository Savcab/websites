Our Story

Growing Concerns started in 1997 and built its name the slow way — one Manhattan lawn at a time.

The company has always measured itself by service quality and long relationships rather than size, which is why it has remained a primarily residential operation. In 2016, that focus narrowed further: rather than expanding into general landscaping, Growing Concerns committed entirely to turf management — fertilization, weed control, insect and disease prevention, and soil health.

Twenty-nine years later, the approach hasn't changed. We're still a Manhattan & Wamego company, still focused on one thing, and still measuring success one healthy lawn at a time.
