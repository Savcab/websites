Problem-solving, not mow-and-go

When the ground itself is the problem, we dig in

Plenty of lawn companies will cut your grass. Fewer will look at the bare, washing-out soil under your deck, figure out why it's happening, and actually fix the grade so it stops. That kind of work — reading where water travels and laying rock to hold ground in place — is what our customers remember us for.

  • We diagnose the cause. Erosion, runoff, and low spots get traced back to the grade — not just covered up.
  • Rock that works. Stone and riprap placed to hold soil, channel water, and still look clean and intentional.
