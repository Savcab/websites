What we do

Tree work for the White Mountains’ big timber

Tall ponderosas, crowded lots, and trees that have grown into the house — we’re set up for the jobs that need a plan, not just a chainsaw.

Our specialty

Technical & large tree removal

The 80-foot pine over your roof, the leaner near the power line, the tree too close to the house to just drop — we rig and section it down piece by piece.

Crane-assisted removals

When access is tight or the tree is simply too big to climb-and-lower, the crane lifts each section clear — faster, and far safer for everything underneath.

Trimming & pruning

Crown thinning, deadwooding, raising canopies off rooflines, and shaping for healthy growth — keeping your pines strong and your property clear.

Stump grinding

Once the tree is down, we grind the stump below grade so you can reclaim the spot — no trip hazard, no eyesore left behind.

Storm & hazard cleanup

Lightning-struck, wind-thrown, or split at the trunk — we clear the damage and haul it off so your property is safe and usable again.

Lot clearing & haul-off

Thinning an overgrown lot or clearing for a build? We take the trees, limbs, and debris and leave the ground clean and ready.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you handle really big or dangerous trees?

That’s our specialty. Tall ponderosas over a roof, leaners near power lines, and trees in tight spots are exactly the work we set up for — with rigging, a bucket truck, and a crane when the job calls for it.

How do I get a price?

Call us at (928) 205-8733 or send a quick request through the form. For most jobs we’ll come out, look at access and what’s around the tree, and give you a clear estimate — no pressure.

Do you grind the stump and haul everything off?

Yes. We can grind the stump below grade and remove the wood, limbs, and debris so you’re left with a clean, usable spot — just let us know when you book.

What areas do you serve?

Lakeside, Pinetop, Show Low, and the surrounding White Mountains communities. If you’re nearby and not sure, give us a call and we’ll let you know.

What are your hours?

We’re working Monday through Saturday, 7am to 6pm. Leave a message any time and we’ll get back to you — reviewers regularly mention how quickly we respond.
