The work that wins the hard jobs

The tree right against the house? That's exactly what we're built for.

Anyone can fell a tree in an open field. The skill is bringing one down in a tight spot — over a roofline, beside a garage, tangled in a fence — with nothing below it getting touched.

Our climbers go up, set the rigging, and lower each limb under control instead of letting it fall. It's slower and more careful by design, and it's the difference between a clean job and an insurance claim.

  • Piece-by-piece dismantling over structures and fences
  • Controlled rope-rigging so limbs are lowered, never dropped
  • Tight access around landscaping, decks and driveways
  • Wood, brush and debris cleared before we leave

Good to know

Common questions

Can you take down a tree that's right next to my house or garage?
Yes — that's the work we're known for. Instead of felling the whole tree, our climbers dismantle it in sections and lower each piece on ropes so nothing lands on your roof, garage, fence or landscaping.
Do you clean up afterward?
Always. Cleanup is part of every job — we cut and haul the wood and brush and rake the area before we leave. Homeowners regularly tell us they were surprised how tidy the yard looked when we finished.
How do I get a price?
Call or text us at (810) 516-6841 and describe the tree — a quick photo helps. For most jobs we'll come out, look at the access and risk, and give you a clear, upfront price before any work starts.
Do you handle storm damage and emergencies?
Yes. If a tree or large limb has come down on your home, driveway or power line after a storm, call us right away and we'll get out to make it safe and clear the damage.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Fenton and serve the surrounding Genesee County area, including Lake Fenton, Linden, Holly and Grand Blanc. If you're nearby and not sure, just ask.
Do you grind the stump too?
We can. Stump grinding is a separate service we offer alongside removal — we grind it below grade so you can replant or level the spot. Just let us know when you call and we'll include it in the quote.

Got a tree you're worried about?

Tell us where it is and what's around it. We'll give you a straight answer and a fair price — and take it down without touching what matters.
