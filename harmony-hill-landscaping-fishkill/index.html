Our process

How a project actually goes, start to finish.

No mystery and no surprise change orders — just what happens between your first call and the day you're using the space.

Walk the yard

We come out, see the space in person, and talk through what's realistic for the slope, the layout and the budget.

Estimate & plan

A written number before anything is dug up — real products (Unilock, Techo-Bloc, Cambridge, or natural stone), a real layout, a real price.

Excavate & build

Proper base, correct grade, drainage worked in from the start — then stone, pavers or decking, set by hand.

Final walkthrough

Site cleaned up and details finished — we walk the space with you before we call the job done.

What we offer

Everything for the outdoor space, under one roof.

We handle stone, wood and lighting ourselves — the same crew quotes it, builds it and stands behind it. Nothing gets handed off to a sub.
