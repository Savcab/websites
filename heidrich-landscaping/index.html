02 — About Heidrich

A Long Island landscaper for over 35 years.

Heidrich Landscaping has designed and built landscapes across Suffolk County and the surrounding areas on Long Island for over 35 years. We keep the whole project under one roof — design, hardscape, plantings, drainage and lawns — with licensed and insured professionals and a certified horticulturist on staff.

That means the people who plan your yard are the people who build it, and the plants that go in are chosen to thrive where they're planted.
