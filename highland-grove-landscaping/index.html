What we do

One crew for the whole yard.

Landscape design

Custom landscape plans built around your home, your soil and how you want to use the yard.

Irrigation systems

About Highland Grove

A Clermont design-build landscaper since 2016.

Highland Grove Landscaping & Nursery has been designing and building landscapes for Central Florida homeowners since 2016 — a decade of pool decks, paver hardscape, irrigation and full plantings that clients still write about years later.

Because we run our own nursery alongside the landscaping business, we control quality from the ground up: the plants are healthy, suited to the climate, and installed by the same team that designed the project.
