Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We come out to your property, walk the space with you, and provide a free, no-obligation estimate for your project. Call (210) 912-5605 or use the form below to set it up.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Design-build landscaping and hardscape — custom pergolas, chop-stone borders and walls, flagstone and concrete patios, walkways, sitting areas, new gardens and trees, sod, grading and drainage. We handle single features as well as complete backyard transformations.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Boerne and serve the surrounding Hill Country, including Fair Oaks Ranch, Bulverde, Comfort, Spring Branch, Sisterdale and the north side of San Antonio.

Do you do the work yourselves or subcontract it?

Hill Country Scapes & Design is owner-led by Jeremiah Bowman. Jeremiah and his crew plan and build the work directly, which is why our customers consistently mention reliable scheduling and clean, finished job sites.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. Smaller features like a stone border can finish in a day or two, while a full backyard build takes longer. We give you a realistic timeline with your quote and keep you updated as the work moves.
