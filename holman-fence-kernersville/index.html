What we build

Pick your fence by material

Every photo below is a fence Holman installed here in the Triad — choose the look that fits your yard and we'll walk it with you and quote it.

Wood & Privacy

Stockade, shadowbox and modern horizontal-board fences in cedar and pressure-treated pine — warm, solid privacy that takes a North Carolina summer.

Vinyl / PVC

Privacy, semi-private and picket vinyl that never needs paint or stain — clean white lines that hold their color and wipe down in minutes.

Aluminum & Ornamental

Powder-coated black aluminum that frames a yard, patio or pool without blocking the view — rust-proof and racked to follow your grade.

Picket & Yard Fence

Classic picket and scalloped runs that keep dogs and kids in without closing off the view — the friendly front-yard and backyard standard.

Split-Rail & Ranch

Two- and three-rail farm and garden fencing that runs clean over open ground and larger country lots — great paired with welded wire.

Gates & Repairs

Matching walk and double driveway gates hung square, plus repairs to existing fences — leaning posts, sagging gates, storm damage.
