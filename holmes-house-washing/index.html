Frequently asked questions

What is soft washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution to safely remove algae, mildew and grime. It cleans deeper than blasting alone while protecting siding, wood and seals from damage.

Will pressure washing damage my siding or deck?

Not the way we do it. We match the pressure to each surface — gentle on vinyl, stucco and wood — so you get a thorough clean without etching, splintering or stripping.

Is it safe for my plants and pets?

Yes. We rinse and look after the beds, plants and yard around the work area, and we're mindful of pets on site. A clean home should never cost you your garden.

How much does house washing cost?

It depends on the size of your home and what you'd like cleaned. We give a free, upfront estimate so you know the price before any work begins — no hidden fees.

How often should I have my home washed?

Most homes in our area benefit from a wash about once a year, though shaded or north-facing sides can green up faster. Many customers simply have us back each season.
