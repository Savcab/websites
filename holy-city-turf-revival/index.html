What We Do

Full-service lawn & landscape care for the Lowcountry

From reviving a dying lawn to fixing the irrigation under it, Ed and the crew handle the whole yard — honestly, and on schedule.

Turf Revitalization Program

Our signature program for tired or thinning lawns — soil-first care that rebuilds a healthy yard over the season.

Signature Service

Sod Installation & Re-Sodding

Tear out failing grass and weeds, grade and smooth the soil, and lay fresh sod for an instant, even lawn.

Irrigation & Sprinkler Repair

Main-line and sprinkler-head repair, plus low-pressure diagnosis, so every zone waters the way it should.

Drainage & Grading

Grading and drainage that move water away from the house and stop low spots from flooding your yard.

Trees, Beds & Plantings

Tree trimming, garden beds, shrubs and palms, and fresh plantings that finish a yard the right way.

Paver Stones & Hardscape

Paver stones around trees and beds, edging, and the finishing touches that give a yard structure and curb appeal.
