What we do

Full-service lawn care for Vienna yards

Mowing is just the start. Hudson Lawn Care handles the regular upkeep that keeps a property sharp from spring green-up through the last leaf of fall — one dependable crew you can count on.

Lawn Mowing

Clean, even cuts on a schedule that suits your grass — crisp edges, tidy lines, and clippings handled so the yard looks finished every visit.

Weed Eating & Trimming

String trimming around fence lines, beds, trees and walkways so the whole property looks detailed — not just the open lawn.

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Shaping and tidying shrubs and hedges so they stay healthy, full and in scale with the rest of your landscape.

Recurring Maintenance
