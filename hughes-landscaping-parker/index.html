How we work

Design, build, and maintain — one crew, start to finish

Most of what we do, we do from beginning to end. You get the same team that drew the plan installing it, and the same team keeping it alive after.

01

Design

We walk the property, listen to how you actually use the yard, and lay out a plan — plants, grades, irrigation and hardscape — suited to Colorado's climate.

02

Build

Our crew handles the install: beds and boulders, sprinkler zones, xeriscape, sod, pavers and concrete — done efficiently and left spotless.

03

Maintain

Once it's in, we keep it thriving — seasonal care, irrigation tune-ups and winter services — so the landscape looks as good in year five as year one.
