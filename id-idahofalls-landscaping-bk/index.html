Our story

Nearly 30 years, same family, same standard

B&K Lawn and Landscape is the landscaping side of B&K Professional Services, Inc. — a family-owned company founded by Brent and Karrie Martin, serving Idaho Falls and the surrounding valley since 1996.

Today the team handles residential and commercial landscaping, lawn care, sprinklers, pest control, and snow removal, and has been voted Gold Winner in the Best of Greater Idaho Falls awards two years running.
