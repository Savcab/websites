Who we are

A Belen crew that treats the yard like a design project.

We're Innovative Designs Landscapes — a young, fast-growing design-build landscaping company rooted right here in Belen, New Mexico.

Most of what gets called "landscaping" out here is a load of gravel and a few shrubs. We do the other thing: we sit down with you, read the lot and the light, and draw a plan — grading, drainage, hardscape, planting, and water features that actually fit a high-desert property. Then the same crew builds it.

We're newer than the big Albuquerque-adjacent firms, and we earn every job. That 4.9 on Google is built one finished yard at a time — and we'd rather show you the work than talk about it.
