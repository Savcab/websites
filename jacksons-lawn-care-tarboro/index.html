What we do

Straightforward lawn care, done right

We focus on the work that keeps a yard looking sharp through every North Carolina season — and we keep it simple to book.

Weekly & Biweekly Mowing

Clean, even cuts on a schedule that fits your yard. We mow, trim, and tidy so your lawn looks freshly kept every visit.

Fertilization & Weed Control

Seasonal feeding and weed treatment to build a thicker, greener lawn and crowd out the weeds that take over in summer.

Aeration & Overseeding

Loosen compacted soil and seed thin spots so roots breathe and your turf fills back in dense and healthy.

Edging & Trimming

Crisp edges along driveways, walkways, and beds, plus string-trimming the spots a mower can't reach, for a finished look.

Leaf & Yard Cleanups

Recurring Lawn Plans
