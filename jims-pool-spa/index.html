Common questions

Do you really test pool water for free?

Yes. Bring in a water sample and our wet lab will read the full chemistry. You'll leave with clear, written instructions on what to add and when.

Do you service in-ground and above-ground pools?

Both. We sell and install liners for in-ground and above-ground pools, and we repair and install pumps, filters, heaters and salt systems.

Where are you located?

Our store is at 138 Davis Rd, Suite A, in Augusta (Martinez), GA 30907 — easy to reach from across the Augusta area. The map below has directions.
