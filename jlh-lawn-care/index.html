06 — Good to know

Questions, answered.

What does JLH do, exactly?

Lawn care and mowing, landscaping (mulch beds, stone edging, plantings, clean-ups), and the heavier work — driveway grading, culvert install and replacement, and stump removal. One crew handles all of it.

Do you only mow, or do bigger projects too?

Both. Plenty of customers keep us on for weekly mowing, and we also take on grading, culverts, stump removal and grounds work that need a tractor or excavator. If it's outdoor and around the property, just ask.

How do estimates and pricing work?

Estimates are free. Every property is different, so we look at the scope and give you a fair, upfront number before any work starts — no surprises. Our reviews say it best: "very affordable" and "fair price."

What areas do you serve?

Newland and the surrounding Avery County high country — Banner Elk, Linville, Crossnore, Pineola, Elk Park, Spruce Pine and nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Call and we'll let you know.

How soon can you come out?

It depends on the season and the job, but we're easy to reach and we'll give you a straight answer on timing when you call. Recurring mowing can usually start quickly.

What's the best way to reach you?

Call or text (828) 387-1788, or send the quick form below. "Easy to contact" is something our customers mention a lot — we like to keep it that way.
