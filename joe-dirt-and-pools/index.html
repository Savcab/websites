Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle the whole project, from design to build?

Yes. Joe Dirt is a full-service design, construction and maintenance firm. The same team that designs your space builds it and can maintain it afterward, so there's no hand-off between separate contractors.

What kind of pools do you build?

We specialize in inground fiberglass pools, designed together with the surrounding paver deck, coping and landscape lighting so the finished area reads as one cohesive outdoor space rather than a pool added on later.

Do you work on commercial properties too?

We do. We serve high-end residential and commercial properties across northern Indiana — from backyard landscapes and pools to hardscape and grounds for businesses.

Can I buy soil, mulch or plants directly from you?

Yes — we stock quality soil, mulch, plants and trees at our Wabash yard, and we're happy to help you pick the right material and the best way to plant it. Give us a call for current availability.

How do I get started?

Call us at (260) 563-3478 or send the quick form below. We'll set up a consult, walk your site and talk through ideas and budget before any work begins.
