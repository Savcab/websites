About us

A younger crew, an older standard of work

JohannLandscaping is a full-service landscaping crew based on Jerome Place in Leominster, keeping properties across North Central Massachusetts sharp through every season. From weekly mowing and fresh mulch beds in the summer to leaf cleanups in the fall and dependable snow plowing all winter, it’s one crew for the whole year — residential and commercial.

The work shows up in the details: clean mowing lines, crisp bed edges, shaped hedges, and a driveway that’s clear the morning after a storm. Every review we’ve earned so far is five stars — and that’s the standard we hold on every property.
