What we build

Fence and weld work, done right the first time

From a clean cattle-panel run across a ranch to a one-off ornamental gate, we fabricate and install it ourselves. No subbing out the welding, no shortcuts where the ground gets tricky.

01

Cattle Panel & Steel Fencing

Welded steel frames with hog-wire or cattle-panel infill — the Hill Country standard. Clean lines that keep livestock and pets in and the view wide open.

Cedar & Wood Fencing

Split-rail ranch fence and horizontal cedar privacy walls. Durable post-setting built for rocky, shifting Hill Country soil.

Ranch & Rail Fencing

Ornamental Iron & Railings

Custom Gates & Entries

Good to know

Fencing & welding questions, answered

Do you do both the fencing and the welding yourselves?

Yes. We fabricate gates, railings and steel fence frames in-house and install them ourselves — so your gate and fence line are built to match and the workmanship is all on us, not a subcontractor.

What kinds of fence do you build?

Cattle panel and steel, cedar and wood (split-rail and privacy), ranch and rail, ornamental iron, custom gates, and chain-link. If you've seen a fence type around the Hill Country, chances are we build it.

Do you handle rocky or sloped Hill Country lots?

Often. Caliche, rock and slope are normal here — we set posts to hold on real terrain, not just flat ground. That's a big part of why customers call us for the trickier jobs.

How much will my fence cost?

It depends on the material, length, terrain and gates — so we give you a free, written estimate after we see the property rather than a guess over the phone. No pressure either way.

What areas do you serve?

Kingsland and the surrounding Highland Lakes / Hill Country — Llano, Granite Shoals, Horseshoe Bay, Marble Falls, Buchanan Dam, Burnet and nearby. Call and we'll let you know if we cover your spot.

Can I get an estimate on the weekend?

We're open Saturdays 10a–3p and answer calls then. Reach out anytime through the form and we'll line up a time that works.
