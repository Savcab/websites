Why desert landscaping

Beautiful here means built for the heat — and the water bill

Out here, a thirsty green lawn is a losing battle. We build yards that look sharp year-round on a fraction of the water, so you spend less time and money keeping them alive.Out here, a thirsty lawn is a losing battle. We build yards that stay sharp year-round on a fraction of the water.

Drought-tolerant by design

Low-water desert plants, cactus and agave set in gravel and decomposed granite — chosen to handle full Mojave sun.

Lower water bills

Swap thirsty grass for artificial turf, rock and smart desert beds. Your yard stops drinking through the meter every summer.

Low-maintenance, year-round

No weekly mow, no reseeding. A gravel-and-paver yard holds its look through every season with a quick seasonal cleanup.
