How a resurface or rebuild happens

From drained shell to finished surface.

Step 01

Layout & excavation

The yard is marked and excavated for the new shell, rebar, or spa footprint before any gunite goes in.

Step 02

Drain, shell & protect

The shell is drained, repaired and masked off — decking, tile and landscaping protected before any plaster is mixed.

Step 03

Gunite & plaster finish

Gunite, tile and the plaster or pebble finish go in by hand, then the pool is filled and balanced.
