The finished stone is what you notice. What makes it last is the base, the grading and the drainage you never see.

We design and build outdoor spaces the way we'd build our own — walk the site first, work out the grade and the water, then set every paver and stone on a base that holds through the freeze and thaw. Patios, walkways, retaining walls and fireplaces that still sit true years later.

Karmic Landscapes has worked across Bow, Concord and the surrounding Merrimack County towns since 2014. Most of what we build starts as a homeowner's sketch or a photo of a backyard they want to actually use — and ends as the reason the neighbors ask who did it.

How we work

01

Walk the site

We look at the grade, the drainage and how you actually want to use the space before a single stone is ordered.

03
