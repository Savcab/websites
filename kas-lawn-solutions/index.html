Our story

Built on soil knowledge and a personal touch

Our story began in 2005 with a Michigan pesticide license and a background in agriculture. In 2019 that turned into a lawn-application business serving Adrian and the surrounding area — bringing real soil knowledge to every fertilizer program instead of a one-size-fits-all spray.

We're a family-owned, local business, and we like it that way. It means we get to know our customers, show up when we say we will, and treat your lawn the way we'd treat our own — whether it's a backyard, an athletic field, or a tired gravel driveway.

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Palmyra and serve Adrian, Blissfield, Tecumseh and the surrounding Lenawee County communities for lawn care, commercial and athletic-field turf, and gravel driveway refinishing.

What makes your fertilization different?

We don't run a single blanket program for every yard. With a background in agriculture and a pesticide license dating to 2005, we read your soil first and build a 1:1 plan around what your specific lawn actually needs.

Can you really fix my gravel driveway without new stone?

In most cases, yes. Through our sister company KAS Gravel Refinishing we grind up the existing gravel, remove vegetation, eliminate potholes and re-grade it smooth — usually for far less than the cost of hauling in new stone.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — KAS Lawn Solutions is fully licensed and insured, and carries Michigan Pesticide Business License No. 460061.

Do you handle commercial properties and athletic fields?

We do. We manage turf for commercial businesses, office parks, HOAs, municipal properties and athletic fields — each plan scaled to the property's size, soil conditions and traffic.
