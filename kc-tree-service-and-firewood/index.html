Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

How do I get a price?

Call or text us at (641) 660-7521 with what you're looking at — a photo of the tree helps. We'll set up a time to look and give you a free, no-obligation quote.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes. If a storm drops a tree or leaves one hung up over your house, call us — we'll get out to clear the hazard and make things safe.

Do you clean up afterward?

Always. Hauling brush, raking up and cleaning the work area is part of the job — we leave your property tidy, not full of debris.

Can I buy firewood from you?

When we've got it seasoned and ready, yes — split hardwood by the load. Give us a call to check what's on hand and ask about pickup or delivery.

What areas do you cover?

Harvey and the surrounding towns — Pella, Knoxville, Tracy, Bussey, Otley, Oskaloosa and nearby Marion County. Not sure? Just ask.

Do you grind the stump too?

We can. Stump grinding takes the leftover down below grade so you can reseed or mow right over it — just let us know when you book the removal.
