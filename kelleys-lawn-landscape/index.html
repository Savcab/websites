Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us.

Do you design as well as install?

Yes. We plan the layout and plant selection with you first — palms, native and Florida-friendly plants, beds and sod — then our own crew installs it. You're not handed a sketch and left to find a planter.

What does an estimate cost?

Nothing. We come out, walk the property, talk through what you're after, and give you clear pricing before any work begins. Call (850) 345-7581 or use the form to set it up.

Can you replace builder's-package landscaping?

That's some of our favorite work. We can pull tired builder plantings and redesign the yard around natives and specimen palms that actually suit the coast — like the mule-palm front yard our reviews mention.

Do you move large palms and trees?

Yes. We use equipment to lift, transport and set larger specimen palms, balled and placed cleanly — installs that smaller hand-crews usually can't take on.

Do you handle ongoing lawn care?

We do. Beyond design and install, we can keep things looking sharp with regular mowing, edging and seasonal cleanups so your new landscape stays healthy.

What areas do you serve?

Crawfordville and the surrounding Big Bend — Wakulla County towns like St. Marks, Panacea, Sopchoppy and Medart, plus nearby coastal communities. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
