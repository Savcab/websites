Concrete drives & walkways

Retaining & stone walls

Good to know

Questions homeowners ask.

What kinds of stonework do you do?

Stone and brick masonry — facades, veneer, columns and walls — plus stone fireplaces and chimneys, outdoor kitchens, flagstone and paver patios, retaining walls, and concrete drives and walkways. If it’s masonry or hardscape, ask us.

How do I get a quote?

Call (830) 370-0448 and tell us what you have in mind. We’ll set up a free on-site design consult, look at the space, and talk through the stone and the budget before any work begins.

Do you work outside of Kerrville?

Yes — we’re based in Kerrville and work throughout Kerr County and the surrounding Hill Country, including Ingram, Center Point, Comfort, Fredericksburg and Boerne. Give us a call and we’ll let you know if your project is in range.

Will you help with the design?

Absolutely. We work through the design with you and make honest recommendations on materials and layout — including the choices that can save you money, like the countertop recommendation one of our outdoor-kitchen clients mentioned in their review.

Got a stone project in mind?

Tell us what you’re picturing and we’ll come take a look. Free, no-pressure design consults across Kerrville and the Hill Country.
