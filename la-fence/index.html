Acreage & equine fencing, done right

Fencing a horse pasture is a different job than a backyard. The posts have to hold against a half-ton animal, the spacing has to be safe, and the gates have to work every day in every season. That's the work L&A Fence is known for.

Our crew has enclosed multi-acre pastures, run four-rail vinyl across open fields, and built wood horse fencing right down to the interior stalls — the kind of project that takes real know-how, not just a post-hole digger.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you do large horse pasture and acreage fencing?

Yes — it's some of our favorite work. We've enclosed multi-acre horse pastures with woven wire and gates, run four-rail vinyl across open fields, and built wood horse fencing with interior stalls. Big-acreage agricultural fencing is a specialty, not an exception.

What types of fence do you install?

Horse and pasture fencing, four-rail vinyl, wood ranch rail, wood and vinyl privacy fence, chain-link, farm field fence, gates, and custom barn and stall work. If you're not sure what fits your property, we'll walk you through the options.

Are estimates really free?

They are. We come out to your property, look at what you're fencing, walk the line with you, and give you a clear estimate with no obligation and no pressure.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Allen and work the surrounding towns in Hillsdale and Branch counties — including Hillsdale, Jonesville, Litchfield, Quincy, Coldwater, and Reading. If you're a short drive outside that, call and ask.

Can you work in winter?

We do. One customer thanked us for building a large fence "in the middle of the Michigan winter." We plan around the weather and the ground to get your fence in when you need it.

How do I get started?

Call us at (517) 398-0055 or fill out the estimate form below with a few details about your project. We'll get back to you to set up a time to come take a look.
