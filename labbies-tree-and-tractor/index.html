07 — Questions
Good to know

Frequently asked

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We come out, look at the tree or lot, talk through the safest way to handle it and give you a clear quote before any work begins — no charge and no obligation.
What makes the “tractor” part different?
A lot of tree crews drop the tree and leave the heavy timber and brush for you to deal with. Our tractor lets us move logs, haul debris, clear brush and do rough grading — so you end up with a finished, usable space instead of a pile of cleanup.
Do you remove the wood and debris?
We can. Cleanup and haul-off are part of how we work — brush, limbs and downed wood loaded out so your driveway and yard are left clear. If you'd rather keep the wood for firewood, just let us know and we'll stack it.
Can you take down a tree close to my house or wires?
Tight-clearance removals near structures, driveways and lines are a big part of the job. We section trees down piece by piece and use bucket and climbing work to control where everything lands. We'll walk you through the plan during the estimate.
Do you work for other contractors?
Often. Landscaping and property crews bring us in for the tree trimming, removals and debris work they don't handle in-house. Same careful standard whether you're a homeowner or a fellow pro.
How do I get on the schedule?
Call (603) 359-2516 or send the quick form below with a little detail about the job. We'll get back to you to set up a time to come look.
