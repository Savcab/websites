Good to know

Fence questions, answered

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Every fence and gate project starts with a free, no-pressure on-site estimate. We'll walk the property, talk through materials and give you a fair written price before any work begins.

What kinds of fence do you install?

Wood and privacy fences, chain-link, ranch and agricultural fencing, vinyl and ornamental, plus gates plain or with automatic openers. We install by the material that suits your property and budget.

Do you build gates and automatic openers?

We do — walk-through gates, double-drive gates and six-foot gates, set to swing true. We can also fit and wire automatic openers as part of the project.

Can you repair an existing fence?

Yes. We reset leaning posts, replace storm-damaged sections, re-stretch chain-link and rebuild tired runs so the whole fence line looks right again.

What areas do you serve?

Lakeport and the communities around Clear Lake — Kelseyville, Lucerne, Nice, Upper Lake, Clearlake and the surrounding parts of Lake County. Call us if you're not sure whether you're in range.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job, but we keep things moving once a start date is set — some customers have had work begin the very next morning. Call for current timing.
