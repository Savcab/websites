Good to know

Common questions

Can you come out the same day for a tree that just fell?

We do our best to respond fast to fallen-tree and storm jobs — many get handled the same day. Give us a call and describe the situation and we'll tell you how quickly we can be there.

Do you grind the stump too, or just cut the tree?

We do both. Stump grinding is one of our main services — we can take the stump down below grade so you can mow right over it and the area looks finished.

Do you clean up and haul everything away?

Yes. Cleanup is part of the job. We haul off the wood, limbs, and brush and leave your yard tidy unless you'd like the wood left for firewood.

What if the tree is right up against my house or a power line?

Tight, high-risk spots are exactly where careful rigging matters. We use a bucket truck and lower sections in a controlled way so nothing hits the house, fence, or lines.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the size, location, and access — a small backyard tree is very different from a large one over the roof. Quotes are free, so call and we'll give you a clear price before any work starts.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Cornelius and serve the Lake Norman area — Davidson, Huntersville, Mooresville, Denver and the surrounding north-Charlotte communities.
