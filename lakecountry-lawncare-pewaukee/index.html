The turf-health program

We don't mow. We keep your lawn healthy.

No mowing, no plowing, no long menu — we specialize in one thing and do it right: a fed, weed-free, deep-green lawn, season after season.

Fertilization

A season-long feeding schedule that builds thick, deep-green turf from the roots up.

  • Multi-visit seasonal program
  • Turf-safe, balanced nutrients
  • Timed to Wisconsin's seasons

Weed control

Targeted broadleaf and crabgrass control that clears the weeds without tearing up the lawn.

  • Broadleaf weed control
  • Applied by the owner himself
  • Watched through the season

Core aeration

Pulling cores to open up compacted soil so water, air and nutrients reach the roots.

  • Relieves soil compaction
  • Better water & nutrient uptake
  • Pairs well with fall feeding

De-thatching

Lifting the built-up thatch layer so new growth comes in thick, green and even.

  • Removes matted thatch
  • Wakes up tired turf
  • Spring or fall timing
