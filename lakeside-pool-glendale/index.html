What we do

Full-service pool care, from weekly upkeep to total recovery.

Weekly Maintenance

Brush, skim, vacuum, empty baskets, and a full chemistry balance every visit — plus an equipment once-over so nothing slips.

Equipment Repair & Install

Pumps, filters, heaters, and valves diagnosed and fixed — or replaced with Pentair, Jandy, and Hayward gear we install and warranty.

Acid Wash

Years of stains, scale, and calcium stripped from drained plaster so the surface looks new again — done by hand, done right.

Green-Pool Recovery

Swamp to swimmable. We shock, filter, and rebalance neglected or storm-hit pools and bring the water back clear — fast.

Pool Automation

Run your pump, heater, and lights from your phone. We install and set up modern automation so your pool runs itself.
