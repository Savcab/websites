How a design happens

Four steps between
a bare yard and a plan.

Step I

Walk the property

We meet at your home and talk through what the space should do — four-season interest, privacy, a deer problem, low maintenance, or all of the above.

Step II

Photograph & measure

I take digital photographs of your site and measure the property exactly, so the plan is drawn to what's really there.

Step III

Read the site

Location, lighting, soil and moisture conditions decide what will thrive. I weigh each one before choosing a single plant.

Step IV

The design takes shape

Through visits, your design is created around your wants and needs — then rendered in color for you to review before anything is installed.
