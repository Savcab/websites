About Landscape Magic

Sedona's landscape design-build company since 2004

Landscape Magic is a complete landscape installation and property-maintenance company based in Sedona, Arizona. Two maintenance crews and three construction crews handle everything from a paver driveway to a full backyard rebuild, for residential and commercial properties across the Verde Valley.

  • Quality and workmanship built to last in Sedona's rock and sun
  • Competitive pricing with a free bid before any work begins
  • Work comes with a guarantee, and crews show up on time and finish as promised
  • Active in the community — Keep Sedona Beautiful, Sedona School District and Sedona Humane Society

What We Build

Full-service landscaping, start to finish

From a first design walk to the last paver set, Landscape Magic handles the whole job — new installs, hardscape, water features and the ongoing maintenance that keeps a Sedona property looking right.

Landscape Design & Installation

New landscape design and installation, planting and full property makeovers built for the high-desert climate.

Property & Landscape Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance from two dedicated crews — one-time cleanups or ongoing care, residential and commercial.

Pavers & Patios

ICPI and Belgard Certified paver installation — driveways, patios, pool decks and walkways.

Flagstone Patios

Natural flagstone patios and stepping paths set in red-rock mulch beds.

Red-Rock Retaining & Berm Walls

Terraced retaining and berm walls built from native red rock to hold a slope and shape a yard.

Water Features

Boulder waterfalls, ponds and fountains built stone by stone into the natural landscape.

Masonry, Stonework & Stucco

Custom stonework and veneer, masonry installation and exterior stucco.

Irrigation & Rainwater Harvesting

Irrigation system design and install, plus passive rainwater harvesting for a ~18in/yr climate.

Low-Voltage Landscape Lighting

Low-voltage lighting systems that show off stonework, plantings and walkways after dark.
