What we do

From a clean weekly cut to a whole new front yard.

We keep the regular work dialed in and take on the bigger projects too — beds, straw, mulch and full sod jobs. Here's where homeowners around Snellville put us to work.

Lawn care & mowing

Mow, edge and trim on a schedule that keeps your grass thick, even and tidy — the recurring visit that takes the yard off your weekend list. When a shady or thin patch won't fill in, we sort out how to get grass growing there.

Beds, shrubs & plantings

Flower beds and foundation plantings, from a quick refresh to a full redo. We help pick shrubs that actually thrive here and set them so the bed looks right once it fills in.

Pine straw & mulch

Fresh pine straw and mulch to finish the beds, hold moisture and give the whole yard a clean, cared-for line. Staring down an overwhelming straw job? That's exactly the kind of thing we knock out fast.

Sod & yard renewal

When a lawn is too far gone to patch, we grade it out and lay fresh sod — turning a bare or neglected yard into one with real curb appeal from the street.

The whole-yard difference

Curb appeal the neighbors actually notice.

A weekly cut keeps a yard neat — but the real change comes from the bigger work: fresh beds, new sod, clean lines all the way to the street. We take on the whole picture, so your place becomes the one people slow down for.
