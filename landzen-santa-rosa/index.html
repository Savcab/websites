Who We Are

We treat every property as a design problem first — then we build it to last.

LandZen is a licensed design-build studio based in Santa Rosa, working across Sonoma County on custom hardscape, gardens, pools, water features, and outdoor living spaces. Every project starts with a real site visit and a real conversation about how you actually want to use the space.

We’ve been doing this since 2013 — through drought years, wildfire seasons, and everything Sonoma County’s climate throws at a landscape. How We Work

Five steps, start to finish.

01 Initial Consultation

We walk the site with you, talk through your goals, concerns, and budget, and look at photos and design ideas together.

02 Design & Budget

We measure and inventory what’s there, then build a conceptual plan and a project budget with high and low options based on your goals.

03 Construction Plans

We finalize material selections, the planting plan, and construction details, then present the final plan and budget for your approval.

04 Project Construction

A designer oversees the build on-site, from the pre-construction meeting through a written punch list at your final walkthrough.

05 Property Care

Once it’s built, we can keep it that way — ongoing maintenance built around what your landscape actually needs.
