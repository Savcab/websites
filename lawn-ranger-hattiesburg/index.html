Who we are

A Hattiesburg crew that treats your yard like the front of the house.

The Lawn Ranger Landscaping helps homeowners across Hattiesburg and the Pine Belt turn ordinary yards into elegant outdoor living spaces — combining thoughtful design, seasonal color, hardscaping and steady maintenance. Owned and operated by Scott Martin, a licensed Mississippi landscape contractor and horticulturist, we handle the whole project: the plan, the plants, the patio, the drainage, and the follow-through.

From a mud-pit backyard to a finished lawn with pavers, sod and lighting, our crew brings the equipment and the know-how to get it done right — carefully, and on schedule.

What we do

Full-service landscaping, start to finish.

Everything below is work we self-perform for Hattiesburg homeowners — no subbing out the parts that matter.

Landscape design & install

Custom plans with seasonal color, perennials and beds — designed to fit your home and installed by our own crew.

Paver patios & hardscaping

Paver patios, walkways and retaining walls built to last — elegant, level and easy to enjoy for years.

Drainage solutions

Standing water and soggy yards fixed the elegant way — drainage that looks good and stays easy to service.

Irrigation & sprinklers

Sprinkler systems installed and dialed in so new sod, beds and color get exactly the water they need.

Sod, turf & lawn care

Old grass out, fresh zoysia or centipede sod in — plus ongoing maintenance to keep it green and healthy.

Landscape lighting

Low-voltage lighting that shows off trees, beds and walkways after dark — safe, warm and welcoming.
