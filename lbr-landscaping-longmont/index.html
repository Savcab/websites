What we do

Built by hand, one yard at a time

We take on the stonework and the ground under it — the parts that have to be done right the first time.

Flagstone patios & walkways

Natural sandstone and flagstone patios, paths and step-off areas — set on a proper base so they stay flat through Colorado freeze-and-thaw.

Retaining walls

Block and boulder retaining walls that hold a slope, carve out usable yard, and drain the way they should behind the stone.

Custom fencing

Cedar, hog-wire and privacy fencing built plumb and square — the kind of clean fence line that frames the whole yard.

Full yard renovation

New lawns, mulch beds, grading and clean-ups — plus HOA hardscape and sandstone installs when a whole property needs bringing back.
