Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Claypool and serve the surrounding Kosciusko County area, including Warsaw, Mentone, Silver Lake, Akron, Pierceton, Atwood and the nearby lake communities. Not sure if you're in range? Just give us a call.

Do you offer weekly or recurring mowing?

Yes. Most of our customers are on a recurring schedule so their lawn stays sharp all season, but we're happy to do one-time cuts and cleanups too. We'll set up whatever fits your property.

Do you handle both homes and businesses?

We do. We care for residential yards as well as commercial properties like offices, churches and storefronts that need dependable, professional-looking grounds maintenance.

How do I get a price?

Call us at (574) 526-2321 with your address and what you're looking for. We'll walk the property, talk through your options, and give you an honest, upfront estimate at no cost.

What are your hours?

We're available Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 7:30 PM, and Saturdays from 12:00 to 4:00 PM. If we're out on a job when you call, leave a message and we'll get right back to you.

Besides mowing, what else do you do?

Plenty. Mulch and bed installation, planting and flower beds, trimming and edging, and spring and fall property cleanups. If it keeps your yard looking great, ask us about it.
