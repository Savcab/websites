What areas do you serve?

We're based in Thomson and cover McDuffie County and the surrounding CSRA — towns like Dearing, Harlem, Grovetown, Appling, Wrens and over toward Augusta. Not sure if you're in range? Give us a call.

Both. We take weekly and biweekly maintenance accounts as well as one-time work like a single cleanup, a bed install, or a bigger landscaping project. Whatever your yard needs.
