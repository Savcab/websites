What we build

Full landscape design, hardscaping and stonework

From a bare yard to a finished outdoor space — we handle the design, the grading and the build ourselves, so the plan you approve is the project that gets done.

Landscape design & install

A full plan for the whole yard — beds, plantings, grading and layout — then we build it start to finish, not just hand you a drawing.

Paver patios & walkways

Patios, walkways and sitting areas laid on a proper compacted base so they stay level and true for the long haul — not heaved by the first hard winter.

Retaining & boulder walls

Block and natural-boulder walls that hold a grade and frame a yard — engineered to drain and built to carry the load behind them.

Bluestone & natural stone

Natural flagstone and bluestone walkways, steps and accents — set by hand for the kind of finish customers single out in their reviews.

Lakefront & shoreline work

Riprap, stone edging and waterfront beds for properties on Wawasee and the surrounding lakes — built to hold the bank and look at home by the water.

Grading, sod & rock beds

Soil grading, fresh sod and seed, and clean rock or mulch beds to finish the project and set new plantings up to thrive.

On the water

Built for life on the lake

Waterfront yards have their own rules — shifting banks, tricky access and grades that run straight to the shoreline. We work these properties around Wawasee and the surrounding lakes all season: riprap and stone edging that hold the bank, patios that step down to the water, and plantings that can take the exposure.
