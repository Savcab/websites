Frequently asked questions

How do I get a quote?

Call or text us at (770) 765-2647, or fill out the quick form on this page with your address and what you need. We'll get you a fair, upfront price — there's no cost or obligation to ask.

Do you offer recurring service or just one-time cuts?

Both. Most customers go with regular weekly or every-other-week mowing, but we're happy to do one-time cuts too — including move-out cleanups and overgrown yards that need to be brought back into shape.

What areas do you cover?

We serve Perry and the surrounding Houston County area, including Warner Robins, Centerville, Bonaire, Kathleen, Byron and Fort Valley. If you're nearby and not sure, just reach out and ask.

Do you clean up after the job?

Always. We blow off walks and drives, tidy the edges, and haul away brush and debris when that's part of the job. We want to leave your property looking clean and finished, not just mowed.

What are your hours?

Give us a call at (770) 765-2647 and we'll work around your schedule. We do our best to be flexible and, when we can, fit in urgent jobs quickly.
