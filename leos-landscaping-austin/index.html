What we build

From bare yard to finished outdoor living

Leo's designs and builds the whole outdoor space — the hardscape, the plantings, and the systems that keep it alive in the Texas heat.

Landscape Design & Install

Full front- and back-yard design and installation — beds, plantings, edging, and grading laid out to fit your property.

Paver Patios & Walkways

Paver patios, gravel paths, and mow strips built on a proper base so they stay level and drain the way they should.

Deck Construction

Wood and composite decks that turn an unused back yard into everyday outdoor living space.

Sprinklers & Sod

Irrigation systems and fresh sod installed together so a new lawn takes root and stays green through summer.

Xeriscape & Turf

Drought-smart rock beds, native plantings, and artificial turf — low-water landscapes built for Central Texas.

Retaining Walls & Firepits

Stone retaining walls, seat walls, and firepit patios that add structure and a place to gather outside.
