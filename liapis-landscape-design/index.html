A landscape is a long-term relationship. We’ve been showing up for ours since 2001.

Liapis Landscape & Design is a Dayton-based design-build and commercial grounds care company. We plan outdoor living spaces, build the hardscape and plantings that make them work, and then keep them maintained season after season — for homeowners and for the property managers who need a grounds partner they don’t have to think about.

Founded and still led today by Phil Liapis, our crew works both sides of the business: the backyard that becomes a family’s favorite room, and the commercial properties that need to look right every week of the year.

How we work

Design, build, and keep it looking right.

From the first look at your property to years of care after the work is done.

Walk the property

We look at the space — a residential yard or a commercial site — and talk through what you need it to do.

Design & estimate

We put together a clear design and a written estimate, so you know the plan and the price before anything starts.

Build it right

Grading, drainage, plantings and hardscape, installed by the same crew that quoted the job.

Keep it maintained

Once it’s built, we can keep it looking the way it did on day one — mowing, care and seasonal cleanups included.
