Our story

Nearly two decades of craftsmanship in Suffolk County

Libardi Island Landscape Design & Masonry has been registered and working on Long Island since 2008 — building the kind of stonework and landscapes that only look better with time.

Piero and the crew take on the full project, from the first design sketch through masonry, grading and planting, so the patio, the walls and the beds all read as one finished space. Every job is supervised on site, day to day.

The result is the reputation our long-time clients describe in their own words above — work that reflects the two-decade relationships behind it.
