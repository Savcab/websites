What we do

Complete tree work, start to clean finish

From a single overgrown oak to a whole lot of cleanup after a storm — here's how we help around Waxahachie.

Tree Removal

Safe, controlled takedowns of dead, dying, leaning, or in-the-way trees — including tight spots near homes, fences, and power lines. We rope it down, haul the wood, and leave the area clear.

Our most-requested job

Trimming & Pruning

Crown thinning, deadwood removal, and shaping that keeps oaks and cedars healthy, balanced, and clear of your roofline. Pruned with the tree's long-term health in mind.

Oak & cedar specialty

Stump Grinding

That leftover stump ground down below grade so you can mow over it, replant, or just be rid of the trip hazard. We grind, rake the chips, and tidy the spot.

No more eyesore

Storm & Emergency Cleanup

After North Texas weather, we clear fallen trees and hanging limbs off houses, driveways, and fences and get your property safe again. Call us — we'll get to you quickly.

Fast local response

Deadwooding & Limb Removal

Targeted removal of dead, cracked, or overhanging limbs before they come down on their own — protecting your roof, vehicles, and the people under them.

Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We'll come out, look at the tree, and give you an honest, up-front price before any work begins — no obligation. Call or text us a photo to get started faster.

Are you insured?

Yes — Liberty Tree Service is bonded and insured. It's printed right on our equipment, and it means your property is protected on every job we take on.

Do you grind the stump and clean up afterward?

Cleanup is part of the job. We haul off the wood and rake the area, and we'll grind the stump down below grade whenever you'd like that done. We aim to leave your yard tidier than we found it.

Can you handle storm damage or an emergency?

Yes. When a tree or large limb comes down on your home, driveway, or fence after North Texas weather, give us a call and we'll prioritize getting your property safe and clear as quickly as we can.

What kinds of trees do you work on?

All of the common North Texas species — we do a lot of oak and cedar work in particular, from careful pruning to full removals, plus stump grinding and limb work.

Do you work on commercial properties?

We do. Liberty handles both residential and commercial work — homes, rentals, ranch acreage, and commercial lots across Waxahachie and Ellis County.
