What we do

Landscape Design

A real plan before a shovel moves — layout, planting, rock, grading and flow that fits your lot and the way you'll use it.

Sod & Resod Rescue

Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us

My lawn got burned out / killed — can you really bring it back?

Yes — it's one of our most common jobs. We pull out the dead grass, fix the soil and prep underneath, then lay fresh sod so it actually takes. We'll resod the front, the back, or both, and tell you straight what the lawn needs to stay healthy after.

Do you just do lawns, or the whole yard?

The whole yard. We design and install full landscapes — sod, trees, shrubs, beds, decorative rock, paver patios, retaining walls and grading — and we can take on bigger outdoor builds like pool surrounds. One crew handles the project start to finish.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the yard and what you want done, so we don't guess over the phone. We come out, look at the property, and give you a free written estimate before any work is scheduled.

Where are you based and how far do you travel?

We're on East Main Street in Grantsville and work across Tooele County — Tooele, Stansbury Park, Erda, Lake Point, Stockton — and the west side of the Salt Lake Valley. If you're close but not listed, give us a call and we'll let you know.

Is xeriscape or low-water landscaping an option here?

Absolutely — it's a smart call in our climate. We do boulder beds, decorative rock and drought-tolerant planting, and we can mix in just as much (or as little) lawn as you want. We'll talk through what makes sense for your water and your budget.
