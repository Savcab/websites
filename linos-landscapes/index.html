What we do

One crew for the whole yard

From the hardscape that shapes your yard to the lawn care that keeps it sharp, Lino's Landscapes handles it start to finish — no juggling multiple contractors.

Retaining walls

Segmental block retaining walls that hold back slopes, level a yard, and add clean, lasting structure — engineered to drain and stay put.

Pavers & concrete

Paver patios, walkways and driveways — laid on a proper base with crisp edges and borders that look sharp for years.

Full landscaping

New lawns and sod, planting beds, mulch, rock and grading — a complete yard build, or a fresh start on a tired one.

Lawn care & cleanups

Mowing, weed-whacking, edging and seasonal yard cleanups — plus dirt and debris hauled off so the yard's left tidy.

Hedge & tree trimming

Hedges, shrubs and trees shaped and cut back to keep the yard healthy, open and easy to look after.

Irrigation & sod

Irrigation installs and fresh sod so a new lawn takes root and stays green through the dry Northwest summer.
