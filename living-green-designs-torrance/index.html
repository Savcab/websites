What we do

From the first sketch to the finished backyard.

We handle the whole project — design, planting and construction — so your outdoor space comes together as one considered piece, not a patchwork of trades. Here's the work we take on across the South Bay.

Landscape design

We plan the whole space first — layout, grading and water-wise, California-friendly planting that looks its best while asking less of your water bill.

Outdoor living & hardscape

Patios, pavers, flagstone, pergolas and built-in seating — the structure that turns a plain yard into rooms you actually spend time in.

Fire & water features

Stone fireplaces, fire pits, fountains and natural rock waterfalls that give a backyard a warm center to gather around.

Pools, spas & renovations

Full backyard transformations — pools, spas and the finish work that ties the whole project together into one cohesive space.

Designed to live green

Landscapes that give back more than they take.

Every project starts with the site and the climate. We lean on drought-tolerant and California-native planting, smart grading and materials built to last — so your yard looks its best while asking less of your water.
