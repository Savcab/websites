What we do

Landscapes that work with nature, not against it.

We design and build regenerative outdoor spaces — conserving water, building healthy soil, and supporting local habitat so your garden gets better every year.

Landscape Design & Turf Conversion

Full-yard design and lawn-to-garden turf conversions — often eligible for local water-district rebates.

Native & Drought-Tolerant Planting

Climate-adapted California-native gardens that thrive on less water and bring pollinators and local habitat back to your yard.

Drip Irrigation & Smart Controllers

Water-wise drip systems and smart, app-based controllers that cut waste and keep every planting healthy through the dry season.

Rainwater Harvesting

Capture and reuse rainwater to keep your landscape lush and resilient — a core part of a truly self-sustaining garden.

Hardscaping, Paths & Trellises

Flagstone paths, paver patios, raised cedar beds, and trellises that give your garden lasting structure and everyday usability.

Ongoing Garden Care

Seasonal maintenance that keeps a regenerative landscape thriving — healthy, established, and beautiful year after year.

Our approach

Rooted in regeneration.

At Living Systems Landscaping we believe a beautiful landscape should go beyond looks. We transform outdoor spaces into sustainable, thriving ecosystems — building soil, conserving water, and restoring habitat so the garden grows more resilient over time.

From native turf conversions and drip irrigation to rainwater harvesting and edible, pollinator-friendly gardens, every project is designed to stay lush and healthy on less water.
