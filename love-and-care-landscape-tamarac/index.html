Landscaping, grass cutting, mulch, tree trimming and removal, and sprinkler repair. That's the work Love & Care Landscape does for homes around Tamarac.

Grass cutting

Mowing, edging and trimming — the routine cut that keeps a Florida lawn even and tidy instead of getting away from you between visits.

Landscaping & mulch

Shrub rows, foundation beds and fresh mulch. Planting that suits the spot and the South Florida heat, then a clean mulched bed to hold it together.

Sprinkler repair

Broken heads, cracked lines and zones that stopped coming on. Sprinklers repair is on the trailer alongside the mowers, so the irrigation gets looked at too.

Tree trimming & removal

Palms and shade trees trimmed back, whole trees taken out, and the debris cleared off afterwards. Ladders and saws so you're not renting either.
