Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We come out to your property, talk through what you want to build, and put together a detailed, upfront estimate — at no cost. Call (928) 284-8866 or use the form below to set it up.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Custom paver patios, walkways and driveways; artificial turf; retaining and stone walls; water features and ponds; outdoor living areas; and landscape design with irrigation and planting. We handle both residential and commercial projects, large and small.

Does artificial turf hold up in the Arizona heat?

Properly installed turf is built for it — it stays green year-round, needs no watering or mowing, and is great for play areas and pets. The key is the base prep and installation, which is where our experience makes the difference in how it looks and lasts.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Cottonwood and work throughout the Verde Valley and Sedona area — including the Village of Oak Creek, Clarkdale, Jerome, Camp Verde, Cornville and Rimrock. If you're nearby and not sure, just give us a call.

How do I get started?

The easiest way is to call (928) 284-8866 or fill out the quote form below with a few details about your project. We'll reach out to schedule your on-site assessment and free estimate.
