Good to Know

Questions we hear a lot

Can you match my existing brick?

That's our specialty. We source the closest available brick to your color, size, and texture, copy the bond pattern, and blend the mortar so the repaired area reads as part of the original wall — including older and discontinued brick.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We'll come look at the brick or chimney, talk through what it needs, and give you a clear written estimate at no cost and no pressure. Call (573) 225-5514 or send a photo through the form.

My chimney is leaking or leaning — can you fix it?

Usually, yes. We handle crown repair, new flue caps, repointing above the roofline, spalling brick, and partial rebuilds. We'll assess whether it's a repair or a rebuild and tell you straight.

What is tuckpointing, and do I need it?

Tuckpointing (or repointing) is grinding out failing mortar joints and packing in fresh mortar. If your joints are cracked, crumbling, or recessed, it's the most cost-effective way to stop water getting in and add decades of life to the wall.

A tree or storm damaged my brick — how fast can you come?

We move quickly on storm and impact damage. We'll come assess, salvage the brick that's still sound, match and replace what isn't, and get the wall back to weather-tight. Call us as soon as it happens.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Cape Girardeau and work throughout the surrounding area — Jackson, Scott City, Gordonville, Fruitland, and nearby towns. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
