Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We come out, look at the property, talk through what you want, and give you a clear quote at no cost or obligation. Call (207) 290-7000 or use the form below to set one up.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Lincoln and serve the surrounding Lincoln Lakes / Penobscot County towns — Howland, Enfield, Lee, Mattawamkeag and nearby. If you're close, just ask.

Do you handle both lawn care and snow plowing?

We do. Maxxed Out is a full-service property crew — mowing, mulch, plantings and cleanups in the warm months, and driveway and lot plowing all winter.

Do you take on residential and commercial work?

Both. Whether it's a single home lawn or a small commercial property that needs regular upkeep and plowing, we can put a plan together that fits.

Can you set up recurring service?

Absolutely — many customers book us on a regular schedule for mowing in summer and plowing in winter so the property is always handled. We'll work out a cadence that suits you.

How do I get started?
