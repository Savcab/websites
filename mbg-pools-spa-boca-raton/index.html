Our story

A Boca Raton family business built on getting it right

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer weekly maintenance plans?

Yes. We run regular service routes across Boca Raton — cleaning, chemistry balancing and equipment checks on a set schedule. Call us for current availability in your neighborhood.

Can you fix my pump, heater or salt system?

Absolutely. Equipment diagnosis, repair and replacement — pumps, filters, heaters, seals and salt systems — is core to what we do, handled by the owner directly.

Yes — hand-set glass and stone tile for spas and waterlines is one of our specialties, and one of the things customers most often mention.

Do you do full backyard remodels?

We do. Travertine and paver decks, outdoor living areas, resurfacing and complete backyard makeovers built around your pool.
