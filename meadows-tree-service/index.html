Frequently asked questions

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We'll look at the job, talk through what's involved and give you a clear, no-obligation price before any work begins. Call (540) 241-6280 or send the quick form below.
Do you haul away the debris and clean up?
Always — it's what our customers mention most. We chip the brush, haul off the wood and can clear older debris piles while we're there, so you're left with a clean yard, not a project.
Can you take down a tree close to my house or power lines?
Yes. We use bucket trucks and careful rigging to bring down trees in tight spots near structures, sheds and lines. Tell us about the location when you call and we'll plan the safest approach.
Do you grind the stump too?
We can grind the stump below grade after a removal so you reclaim that part of your yard. Just let us know you'd like stump grinding included in your estimate.
What areas do you serve?
Staunton and the surrounding Shenandoah Valley — Waynesboro, Fishersville, Verona, Stuarts Draft and the rest of Augusta County. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call.
