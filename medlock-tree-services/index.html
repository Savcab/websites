What we do

Skilled tree care for Central Florida yards

From a single overgrown oak to a row of palms along the lake, we do the careful work that keeps your trees healthy — and handle the heavy removals when a tree has to come down.

Most requested

Tree Trimming & Crown Thinning

Selective thinning, deadwood and sucker removal, and canopy lifts that open up light and keep a tree strong — done to shape, not hacked back.

Palm Trimming

Clean palm pruning — old fronds, seed pods and boots removed — with care taken to protect the plants and screens around the base. A Florida specialty we do a lot of.

Tree Removal

Safe, efficient removal of dead, storm-damaged or overgrown trees — including big oaks tight against the house. Prompt quotes and surprisingly quick work.

Stump Grinding

Finish the job below the line. We grind the stump down so you can replant, re-sod, or just have your yard back — no eyesore left behind.

Our approach

We treat your trees like they're meant to stay

Plenty of crews show up, cut everything in reach, and leave. That's not us. When a tree can be saved, we thin and shape it so it stays healthy and looks better than before. When it genuinely has to come down, we take it down cleanly and carefully.

Either way, the work around the work matters: we protect the surrounding plants, mind the screens and structures, and leave the yard spotless when we pull out.

  • Careful thinning & pruning that keeps trees strong
  • Surrounding plants & property protected
  • Full clean-up on every job
  • Honest, no-pressure quotes — fairly priced

Good to know

Questions homeowners ask

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We come take a look, walk you through the options, and give you an honest, upfront price before any work begins — with no pressure to book.
Can a tree be trimmed instead of removed?
Often, yes — and we'd rather save a healthy tree than take it down. We'll tell you honestly whether careful thinning and pruning will solve the problem or whether removal is the safer call.
Do you trim palms?
We do — palm trimming is a big part of our work here in Central Florida. We clear old fronds, seed pods and boots, and take care to protect the plants and screens around the base.
Do you clean up afterward?
Always. Clean-up is part of every job — debris hauled away, the yard raked, and the surrounding area left spotless when we pull out.
Do you grind the stump too?
Yes. We offer stump grinding so the job is finished below the ground line — leaving you clear to replant, re-sod, or just enjoy the open space.
Which areas do you cover?
Lake Wales and the surrounding Polk County lakes region — including Babson Park, Frostproof, Dundee, Lake Hamilton, Bartow, Haines City and Winter Haven. If you're close, give us a call.
