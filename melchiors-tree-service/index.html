Good to Know

Common Questions

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the tree's size, location, and how tricky the access is — a small backyard tree is very different from a large one over a house. That's exactly why the on-site estimate is free: we look at the actual job and give you an honest, written price before any work starts.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every quote is free and given on-site, so you know the real number for your specific tree or lot — no obligation to book.

Can you come out evenings or weekends?

We can. We're open 7am to 8pm Monday through Friday, plus Saturday and Sunday, so you can schedule an estimate or the work around your own schedule instead of taking time off.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes — stump grinding is one of our core services, whether it's the stump from a removal we did or an old one that's been sitting in your yard. We grind it below grade so you can reseed or replant.

What about the branches and debris afterward?

Your call. We can haul everything off and leave the site clean, or buck and stack the wood as firewood if you'd like to keep it.

Can you help after a storm?

Yes. When a storm drops a tree or limb across a driveway, fence, or roof, we move quickly to clear the fallen wood and make the property safe again. Call us as soon as it happens.
