Sprinkler & irrigation repair

The dry patch is rarely where the problem is.

Brown grass shows up at the end of a run, not at the fault. A sprinkler zone runs in one direction — off the wall at the controller, through the valves, past the pressure regulator, out along the laterals and drip runs to whatever waters the far end of the yard. The drawing below is that system, keyed A to E; the five lettered parts are the repairs we get called for.

  • A

    Controllers & timers

    Programs, wiring and the box on the wall — including the controller that got reset in an outage and never went back to a real watering schedule.

  • B

    Valves & manifold

    A zone that won't come on, won't shut off, or keeps weeping after the timer stops. We test the manifold and replace valves that have failed.

  • C

    Pressure & coverage diagnosis

    Water that soaks the first zone and dies in the second is usually a pressure problem, not a broken head. We measure and inspect before anything gets dug up — one of our reviewers had months of pressure trouble that came down to a regulator he didn't know was there.

  • D

    Lateral lines & spray heads

    Water running across the sidewalk, a wet patch that never dries out, a bill that climbed with nothing else changing. We locate the break and repair the line or the head it feeds.

  • E

    Drip lines & emitters

    Installed, extended and repaired for plant beds and trees — the runs that split at a fitting and quietly stop watering while everything else looks fine.

Gardening & maintenance

The irrigation is half of it. We do the other half too.

The name says gardening first. Fixing the water is what most people call about, but a Menifee yard needs the rest of it as well — upkeep on the beds and the lawn, and planting and installation when a yard is being changed rather than repaired.

Doing both means the watering and the plants get set up for each other: emitters where the roots actually are, a schedule that matches what's planted, and a yard that survives an inland Riverside County summer.
