Frequently asked questions

How much does pressure washing cost?

Most jobs start around $40 for sidewalks, $80 for a deck, $100 for a driveway and $140 to soft-wash siding. Final pricing depends on the size and condition of the surface, and we always confirm a flat price with you before we begin.

Will pressure washing damage my siding or roof?

No — we use soft washing for delicate surfaces like siding, stucco and roofs. That's a low-pressure method with cleaning solution that removes algae and grime safely, and we reserve higher-pressure surface cleaning for hard concrete only.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Madison and serve Norfolk and the surrounding Madison County communities, including Battle Creek, Tilden and Newman Grove. Not sure if you're in range? Just give us a call.

Can I get a quote without an in-person visit?

Often, yes. Text us a photo of what you'd like cleaned along with a rough idea of the size, and we can usually give you a flat quote the same day. For larger or commercial jobs we may take a quick look in person first.

How do I book?

Call or text us at (402) 860-5283, or email midwestblastandclean@gmail.com. We're available Monday through Saturday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and we'll get you scheduled at a time that works.
