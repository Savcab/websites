What we do

One crew for the whole transformation

Most yards we take on need more than one trade. Miguel Landscaping handles the dirt work, the watering, the planting and the hardscape — so you're not chasing three different contractors to finish one project.

Grading & Excavation

Shaping and leveling the lot, fixing drainage and prepping a solid base before anything goes in — the part that makes everything else last.

Sprinklers & Irrigation

Full sprinkler systems designed for Utah summers — even coverage that keeps a new lawn green without wasting water.

Sod & New Lawns

Fresh sod installed over properly prepped soil for an instant, even lawn — no waiting a season for seed to fill in.

Retaining Walls & Steps

Block retaining walls and custom stone steps that hold a slope, carve out usable space and give the yard real structure.

Pavers & Walkways

Paver patios, walkways and stamped concrete laid on a compacted base — clean lines that stay flat and level for years.

Mulch Beds & Planting

Stone-edged garden beds, fresh mulch, trees and shrubs — plus raised garden boxes that finish the yard and keep it low-maintenance.

Good to know

Common questions

Do you handle the whole project, or just one part?

Both. A lot of our jobs are full-yard builds — grading, sprinklers, sod and hardscape together — but we're glad to take on a single piece like a retaining wall, a paver patio or a new lawn.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the size of the yard and what's involved. We give a free, no-pressure estimate after seeing the site so the number is real, not a guess over the phone.

How long will it take?

We'll give you a clear timeline up front and stick to it. Finishing on schedule is something our customers mention by name, and we take it seriously.

What areas do you cover?

Springville and the nearby Utah County towns — Mapleton, Spanish Fork, Provo, Orem, Payson, Salem and the surrounding area. Not sure if we reach you? Just ask.

Do you install sprinkler systems too?

Yes. Irrigation is part of most of our installs — designed for Utah's dry summers so a new lawn gets even, efficient coverage.
