How much does lawn mowing cost?

It depends on the size of your lawn and how often you’d like it mowed. We’ll come out, take a look, and give you a clear, fair quote up front — our customers consistently say our pricing is reasonable.

How often should I have my lawn mowed?

Most De Motte lawns look their best on a weekly schedule through the growing season, with bi-weekly an option when growth slows. We’ll recommend what fits your yard.

Yes. When the leaves come down we clear them off the lawn, beds and walkways so your yard stays clean heading into winter. It’s one of our most-requested fall services.
