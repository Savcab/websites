Good to know

Common questions

Do you handle emergencies and storm damage?

Yes. Broken limbs on the house, a tree across the driveway, or a hazard after a storm — call us and we'll get out as fast as we can. Urgent jobs move to the front of the schedule.

Can you remove trees close to my house or power lines?

That's a lot of what we do. When there's no room to drop a tree, we take it down section by section. We've safely removed trees overhanging roofs and power lines for homeowners across the area.

Do you do defensible-space and fire-safety clearing?

Yes — thinning, brush clearing and trimming to create defensible space around foothill homes. It's seasonal work we handle every year.

Will you clean up afterward?

Always. Brush, logs, sticks and debris are hauled off, and we leave the site completely cleaned up — it's something reviewers mention again and again.

How much will my job cost?

Every property is different, so we give a detailed, competitive estimate up front — often with the work area mapped out — before any work begins. Call for a free quote.

Can you clear a whole lot, not just one tree?

Yes. With excavator-assisted clearing, log decking and haul-off to the mill, we handle larger land-clearing projects to open up views, build pads or make room for solar.
