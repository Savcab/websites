Good to know

Questions homeowners ask first.

Do you do both the hardscape and the planting?

Yes — that's the whole point of Minor's. One crew builds the walkways, patios and walls, then installs the beds, mulch and plantings around them. You get a finished yard that reads as one design, instead of hardscape from one contractor and landscaping from another that never quite match.

Do you handle the base and grading, or just the stone on top?

The full build — excavation, base prep, grading and drainage, then the pavers or stone on top. The base and drainage are the most important part of a patio or walkway that stays level and tight, so we never cut corners there.

Can you take on a full property redesign?

Absolutely. We plan full redesigns up front — walkways, patio, retaining walls and planting beds drawn to work together — and build them in the right order so the finished property flows. We're just as happy doing a single walkway or patio.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Phillipsburg and work throughout Warren County and the towns along the Lehigh Valley border. If you're close by and don't see your town listed, just ask — there's a good chance we cover it.

How do I get a price?
