What we do

Full-service landscaping for the Mission Valley

From the first design sketch to the weekly mow, one experienced crew handles the whole property — so the work, not a template, does the talking.

Landscape Design & Install

Creative, livable yard designs — beds, trees, patios and stonework — that raise the beauty and value of your property.

Lawn Care & Maintenance

Mowing, fertilizing, weed control and clean-ups on a schedule, with attention to the details of every lawn we service.

Irrigation Install & Service

Sprinkler and drip systems designed, installed and serviced to keep your landscape green through a Montana summer.

Seasonal Color & Planting

Perennials, annual color and planting that bring lasting curb appeal to residential beds and commercial entrances alike.

Sod & New-Lawn Installation

Fresh sod and full lawn establishment for new builds and tired yards — graded, laid and ready to grow.

Retaining Walls & Hardscape

Boulder and block retaining walls, walkways and outdoor living spaces built to hold their ground on lakeside lots.

Who we are

Experienced, creative landscapers rooted in Polson
