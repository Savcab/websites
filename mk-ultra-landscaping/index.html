08 — Good to know

Frequently asked
questions.

A few things Erin homeowners ask before their first job.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Erin and serve Houston County and the surrounding towns — including Tennessee Ridge, McEwen, Cumberland City, Stewart and Waverly. If you're nearby and not sure you're in range, just give us a call.

Do you offer one-time service or only regular schedules?

Both. We do weekly and bi-weekly mowing for folks who want it handled all season, and one-time visits for cleanups, a single mow, or a landscaping project. Whatever fits your yard.

Can you haul away the junk and debris, too?

Yes. Cleanup and hauling is one of our core services — we clear brush, debris and yard clutter, load it onto our trailer, and haul it off so you're left with a clean property and nothing to deal with.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different, so we price each job to the size and scope of the work. Quotes are always free and there's no pressure — call, text or send the form and we'll get you a clear number.

How do I get started?

Easiest is to call or text us at (731) 227-3106, or fill out the quick quote form below with a few details. We'll follow up to set up a time to look at the property.
