Quincy's curbing specialists

We do one thing, and we do it beautifully.

Moonlight Curbing builds custom decorative concrete borders for homes across Quincy and the Columbia Basin. Each curb is mixed and molded right on your property in a single continuous pour — no seams, no pre-made segments — then shaped, stamped, and colored to suit your landscape.

The result is a crisp, permanent edge that keeps grass out of your beds, holds your mulch and rock in place, and frames the yard the way a good border should. Homeowners call us back for their next project, and most of our work comes by word of mouth.

Good to know

Questions homeowners ask

What exactly is decorative concrete curbing?

It's a continuous concrete border mixed and shaped right on your property in one pour — no pre-cast blocks or seams. We mold it to your bed lines and lawn edges, then stamp and color it for a finished, decorative look that holds up for years.

How long does an install take?

Most residential projects are completed in a single day, start to finish. Larger, whole-yard layouts may take a little longer — we'll give you a clear timeline with your estimate.

Can the curbing follow curves and circles?

Yes — that's the advantage of continuous curbing. Because it's molded on site, it flows smoothly around curved beds, island plantings, and tree rings just as easily as it runs straight.

What colors and patterns can I choose?

We offer a range of stamp patterns — like brick, cobble, and slate — along with integral colors and tints. We'll help you pick a combination that complements your home and existing landscape.

Will it keep grass out of my flower beds?

That's one of its main jobs. A solid concrete border creates a clean barrier that slows grass and weed creep into your beds and keeps mulch and rock from spilling onto the lawn — which also makes mowing and edging easier.

How do I get a price?

Reach out for a free estimate. We'll come look at the property, talk through what you'd like bordered, and give you accurate, upfront pricing before any work begins.
