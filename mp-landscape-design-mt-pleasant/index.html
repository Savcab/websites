How It Works

A Process That Respects Your Time

We don't do vague quotes or guesswork. Every project starts with a paid design consultation, because your outdoor space deserves serious attention before a shovel ever moves.

  1. 01

    Contact & Design Questionnaire

    Fill out our contact form or call us. A short questionnaire helps us learn your goals, style, and expectations.

  2. 02

    Intro Call & Design Retainer

    A short discovery call on priorities and budget. Moving forward books an on-site consultation and a $350 design retainer, credited toward your full design fee.

  3. 03

    On-Site Consultation

    We walk your property, sketch ideas in real time, and talk through drainage, lighting, plantings, and local building restrictions.

  4. 04

    Studio Design Phase

    A detailed 2D layout plan with weekly updates and room for feedback, complete with color palettes, planting selections, and optional 3D renderings.

  5. 05

    Final Presentation & Budget Review

    A finished plan alongside a line-item construction budget, broken down clearly so you know exactly what you're paying for.

  6. 06

    Build With MP Landscape

    The same care that went into the design carries through construction — clean, organized, and built to last.
