Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out, look at the tree and the access, and give you an honest price up front — no obligation. Call (530) 310-9467 or use the form to set it up.

Can you take down a tree close to my house or power lines?

That's a big part of what we do. Tight removals near structures, fences and lines get roped down in controlled sections rather than felled in one piece, so nothing gets damaged.

Do you grind the stump and clean up afterward?

We can grind the stump below grade and we always clean up — brush chipped, wood stacked or hauled, and the area swept. You decide how much wood you want left behind.

What areas do you cover?

Susanville and the surrounding Lassen County communities — Janesville, Standish, Litchfield, Westwood, Chester, Lake Almanor and nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

When are you available?

We work Monday through Friday, 7am to 7pm. Leave a message any time and we'll get back to you to schedule a look.

Got a tree that needs to come down?

Tell us what you're dealing with and we'll get you a straight, honest estimate. Susanville's trusted crew is ready when you are.
