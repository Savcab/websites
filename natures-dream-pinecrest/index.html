About Us

Meet the Team Behind Every Project

Nature’s Dream Landscape & Tree Service was founded in Pinecrest, FL in 2002 and is led today by co-founders Victoria Trucco and Ray Rueda.

Victoria Trucco

Co-Founder & Landscape Architect

Victoria holds a Master’s degree in Landscape Architecture and has designed hundreds of residential and commercial landscapes since founding Nature’s Dream in 2002. She is a recipient of the ASLA Achievement Award.

Ray Rueda

Co-Founder & Operations

Ray is a state-certified Irrigation Contractor and ISA Certified Arborist, and also holds licenses as a landscape commercial manager, pesticide applicator, low-voltage specialist and fertilizer applicator. He previously ran the company’s plant nursery for 12 years.
