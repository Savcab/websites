Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?
We’re based in Charlotte and serve homeowners and HOAs across the city and the surrounding communities, including Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, Mooresville, Matthews, and Mint Hill. Not sure if you’re in range? Just ask.
What services does PGA Lawn Care offer?
We’re full-service: routine lawn maintenance, fertilization and weed-control programs, landscape and hardscape design, mulch installation, garden clean-ups, tree and shrub protection, and mosquito control — plus commercial and HOA grounds management.
Do you handle both residential and commercial properties?
Yes. Alongside residential lawns we manage HOA common areas and commercial properties on a regular schedule, so property managers get the same dependable service our homeowners rely on.
