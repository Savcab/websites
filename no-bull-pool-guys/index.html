Good questions

Pool owners ask us

How often should my pool be serviced?

In the Arizona heat, most pools do best with weekly service to keep the chemistry balanced and the water clear. We'll recommend a schedule that actually fits your pool and how you use it — not a one-size-fits-all upsell.

Are you licensed?

Yes. No Bull Pool Guys is a licensed Arizona contractor, ROC #349066. You're hiring a real, accountable business — not someone working out of the back of a truck with no recourse.

Can you fix my pump, filter or heater?

Absolutely. We diagnose, repair and install pumps, filters, heaters, salt systems and automation. We'll always tell you honestly whether a repair makes sense or whether it's smarter to replace.

My pool turned green — can you help?

Yes. Our green-pool rescue shocks, filters, clears and rebalances neglected or algae-filled pools back to swim-ready — and we can keep it that way on a weekly maintenance plan.

Do you resurface and remodel pools?

We do. If your plaster is worn, your tile is dated, or your pool just needs a refresh, we handle resurfacing and remodels — pebble finishes, tile, and equipment upgrades to match.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Surprise, Buckeye, Peoria, Goodyear, Litchfield Park and Scottsdale, plus the surrounding West Valley and Phoenix metro. Call us to confirm your neighborhood.
