Good to know

Tree removal questions, answered

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We give free, no-pressure quotes. Call or text us a description of the tree (photos help), and for most jobs we'll come look in person and hand you a clear written price before any work starts.

Can you handle emergency or storm-damaged trees?

Absolutely — emergencies are a big part of what we do. If a tree is down on your house, blocking your driveway, or threatening to fall, call us anytime. We respond quickly across Lake County and make the property safe.

Do you remove the stump and clean up the debris?

Yes. We grind stumps below grade and haul off the wood, brush, and chips so you're not left with a mess. A clean, usable site is part of the job, not an upsell.

Can you take down a tree close to my house or power lines?

That's exactly the kind of work we specialize in. Tight, high-risk removals near structures and lines are rigged down in controlled pieces rather than felled whole, so everything comes down safely and under control.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Kelseyville and serve all of Lake County — including Lakeport, Clearlake, Lower Lake, Hidden Valley Lake, Cobb, and the surrounding Clear Lake communities. Not sure if you're in range? Just give us a call.
