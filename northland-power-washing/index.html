Good to know

Questions we hear from lake-country homeowners.

Will you really drive out to my cabin or hunting land?

Yes — it's some of our favorite work. We regularly wash cabins on remote lakes and have traveled a long way up north for hunting-property jobs. Tell us where it is and we'll let you know what the trip looks like.

Is pressure washing safe for my siding and cedar?

We use a low-pressure "soft wash" on siding, cedar, decks and docks, which cleans with the right solution rather than blasting force. Hard surfaces like concrete get the higher-pressure treatment they can handle. The method always matches the material.

Can I get my cabin washed on a yearly schedule?

Absolutely. A lot of our customers have us back once a year to open up the cabin for the season. We're happy to set a recurring schedule so it's one less thing on your list.

What does a wash cost?

It depends on the surfaces, the size of the property and the drive. We'll give you a clear, upfront estimate before any work starts — just call (320) 763-6593 or send a quote request.

What are your hours?

We're available 7:30am to 8:30pm, Sunday through Friday. Those long days make it easier to fit in homes, cabins and seasonal turnarounds around the lakes.
