What we do

Full-service desert landscaping, built to last

From recurring commercial grounds care to ground-up installs, every Delfino project is designed for Southern Nevada's heat, soil, and water rules.

Commercial & HOA Maintenance

Scheduled grounds care for HOAs, offices, and commercial properties — turf, beds, trees, and common areas kept contract-clean year-round.

Artificial Turf & Putting Greens

Pet-friendly synthetic grass and custom putting greens — lush, low-water, and durable through every Las Vegas summer.

Pavers & Hardscape

Driveways, patios, walkways, and patio covers laid with precision — hardscape that frames the space and holds up for decades.

Retaining Walls

Engineered retaining and seating walls that grade slopes, define beds, and add structure to challenging desert lots.

Irrigation & Lighting

Efficient drip and irrigation systems plus low-voltage landscape lighting — installed and tuned for water savings and curb appeal.

Xeriscape & Desert Conversion

Grass-to-rock conversions and full custom xeriscape designs that meet SNWA water-restriction rules and may qualify for rebates.
