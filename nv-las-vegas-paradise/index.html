What we build

Full-service outdoor living, designed and constructed in-house.

Five core specialties that turn a bare Las Vegas lot into a finished, low-maintenance outdoor space.

Landscape Design & Construction

Custom design-build from concept to completion — grading, planting, irrigation and layout tailored to your home’s architecture and the desert climate.

Artificial Turf Installation

High-quality turf built for the Las Vegas climate — low-maintenance, vibrant year-round and great for families and pets, with no watering or mowing.

Hardscaping & Fire Features

Patios, walkways, retaining walls, pavers and fire pits crafted from premium materials for durability and a clean, modern finish that lasts.

BBQ & Outdoor Kitchens

Custom built-in BBQs and full outdoor kitchens designed for the way you entertain — from casual cookouts to refined al-fresco dining.

Outdoor Lighting

Energy-efficient landscape and pathway lighting that adds safety, sets the mood and brings your yard to life after the sun goes down.

Desert & Xeriscape Plantings

Agave, yucca, cacti and accent trees arranged for water-wise, SNWA-friendly curb appeal that thrives in the Mojave without constant upkeep.
