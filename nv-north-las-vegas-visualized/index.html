What We Do

Full-Service Landscape Design & Build

One team for the whole project — from the first concept and grading to the final planting, paver and light. Here’s what we build across the valley.

Landscape Design

A custom plan for your whole yard — layout, materials and planting mapped to your goals, budget and the desert climate.

Artificial Turf

Lush, low-water lawns that stay green through Las Vegas summers — no mowing, no brown patches, no irrigation bills.

Paver Patios & Walkways

Patios, walkways and driveways that expand your usable outdoor space with clean, durable hardscape.

Retaining Walls

Structural block and stone walls that hold soil, manage slope and shape clean, multi-level yards.

Patio Covers & Pergolas

Shade structures and covered outdoor rooms that make the yard usable even at the peak of a desert summer.

Water Features

Ponds, fountains and waterfalls that add movement, sound and a sense of calm to the landscape.

Fire Pits

Built-in fire pits and gathering spaces that extend your evenings outdoors well past sunset.

Irrigation & Lighting

Efficient drip irrigation plus low-voltage landscape lighting to keep everything thriving — and beautiful after dark.

Commercial Landscaping

Design, install and refresh for commercial properties — first impressions that hold up to Las Vegas traffic and heat.

Also offered: SOD installation, hardscaping, and residential redesigns — full yards taken from concept to finished space.

Who We Are

Owner-Led, North Las Vegas Roots

Visualized Landscape is led by owner Ramses, who works directly with every client — answering questions early and often, and walking the project from first concept to final clean-up.

Based in North Las Vegas, the team designs for the realities of the Mojave: intense heat, tough soil and water restrictions. That means drought-smart desertscapes, water-wise turf and durable hardscape that still look beautiful years down the line.
