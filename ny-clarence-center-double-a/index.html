About Double A

A local crew our neighbors actually recommend

Since 2018, Double A Landscaping has been a go-to choice for residential and commercial property care in Clarence, Clarence Center and Buffalo. We began as a simple lawn-mowing service and grew, season by season, into a full-scale landscape, maintenance and property-care company.

So many of our jobs still come from word of mouth — neighbors who saw our work next door and asked who did it. That trust is something we protect on every visit, whether it's a weekly mow or a brand-new paver patio.
