What we do

Design-build landscaping, built to last.

Alliance is a full design-build crew — we plan it, install it, and stand behind it. Here is the work we actually do across Lackawanna and Western New York.

Landscape design & installation

Full tear-outs and redesigns: new beds, foundation plantings and complete yard transformations — designed with you, then installed by our own crew.

Pavers & hardscaping

Paver walkways, patios and retaining walls that frame the planting and hold up to Western New York winters — clean lines, solid base, built to last.

Sod, grading & drainage

Fresh sod for an instant lawn, plus grading, seeding and drainage work that moves water away from the house and keeps your yard healthy.

Cleanups & plantings

Spring and fall cleanups, fresh mulch, tree and shrub plantings and bed maintenance — the upkeep that keeps a finished landscape looking sharp.

Snow & ice management

Residential and commercial plowing and clearing all winter long — per-storm or for the whole season. See winter services →
