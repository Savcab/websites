What we do

Full-property care, one trusted crew.

Honest, end-to-end lawn and landscape work for homeowners across the Five Towns — no upsells, just the services your property actually needs.

Lawn Care & Mowing

Weekly or bi-weekly cuts with clean edging, trimming and blow-down — the straight stripes and crisp lines reviewers keep mentioning.

Landscape Design & Planting

Fresh beds, shrubs and seasonal color designed for your home's front-yard curb appeal and built to thrive in our coastal Nassau soil.

Organic Mosquito Control

All-natural mosquito treatments that are safe for children and pets — so the yard you pay to keep beautiful is one you can actually enjoy.

Pruning, Hedge & Tree Trimming

Shaped hedges, healthy shrubs and careful tree trimming that open up light and keep every property line looking sharp.

Spring wake-ups and fall leaf clean-ups — beds cut back, debris hauled, and your property reset for the season ahead.
