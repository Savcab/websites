What we do

One team for your entire landscape

Lucente Landscaping is a full-service design-build team. We plan, construct and care for the whole property — hardscape, plantings, water and light — so every part of your home works together.

Landscape Design & Build

Custom design and full installation — grading, beds, plantings and structure built from one cohesive plan.

Masonry & Hardscape

Bluestone patios, walkways, steps and stone walls — natural materials set to last through Westchester winters.

Outdoor Living

Fire pits, built-in kitchens, pergolas and gathering terraces that turn the backyard into another room.

Landscape Lighting

Path, accent and architectural lighting that keeps your property safe, warm and striking after dark.

Lawn Sprinklers

Efficient irrigation design, installation and seasonal start-up & winterization for a healthy lawn.

Drainage Solutions

Grading, French drains and dry wells that move water away from the home and keep the grounds dry.

Tree & Shrub Care

Pruning, shaping and seasonal maintenance that keeps mature trees and shrubs healthy year-round.

Lucente Nursery

Our own nursery — 3.5 acres and four greenhouses of annuals, shrubs, trees, topsoil and mulch behind every project.

About Lucente

Gorgeous landscapes, built and cared for by one team

We're a full-service landscaping company based in New Rochelle, serving homes throughout Westchester County. Our design-and-construction team has created some of the most beautiful landscapes in the Northeast — and because we run our own 3.5-acre nursery, the plants, stone and craft all come from the same place.

From the first on-site walk-through to the final planting and ongoing care, you work with people who take ownership of your property as if it were their own.
