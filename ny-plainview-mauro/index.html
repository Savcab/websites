Our story

The same crew you trusted as Simplicity Landscape

For over 30 years, the family behind Mauro has been caring for the lawns and gardens of the Westbury and Plainview area — first as Simplicity Landscape, and today as Mauro Landscaping & Design.

It’s a true father-and-son business. Eddie and his father Giuseppe Mauro work side by side on every property, making sure each customer gets the care and craftsmanship that earned three decades of repeat neighbors. Licensed and insured, and as hands-on as the day they started.
