Who we are

A design/build landscaping firm that earns its work the old-fashioned way — by referral, one finished property at a time.

Homeowners across the Capital Region call Brad Rose Landscaping for the full arc of a project: walking the property, shaping the design, then building the patios, walkways, retaining walls, plantings and lighting that bring it together. The result is curb appeal that holds up season after season.

From a fresh front-bed renovation to a full backyard transformation with stonework and a fire pit, every job gets the same professionalism, communication and attention to detail our reviewers keep naming by name.
