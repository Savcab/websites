The firm

A landscape practice that designs and builds its own work.

Rolling Hills Landscaping is a landscape-architecture and design-build firm based in Rye Brook. We carry a project from the first site walk through drawings, planting, and the stonework — and then stay on to steward the gardens as they mature.

Led by principal and plantsman Genaro Partida and designer Juan Luis — trained in landscape architecture at SUNY ESF — the studio works across residential estates and commercial grounds throughout Westchester County and Greenwich, Connecticut.

How we work

One studio, from first sketch to standing garden.

Because we design and build in-house, the intent of the drawing survives all the way into the soil.

1

Consultation

We walk the property, listen to how you live outdoors, and read the site — light, grade, soil, and views.

2

Design

Master plan, planting design, and construction drawings, so every bed, path, and wall is considered before we break ground.

3

Build & Plant

Our own crew installs the stonework and the plantings — the people who drew it are the people who build it.

4

Steward

Seasonal maintenance and fine gardening keep the landscape growing into the design for years, not weeks.
