Who we are

A landscape transformation company, run by the owner

Wesscapes is owned and operated by Weston “Wes” Shapiro, who has been doing landscaping work since he was twelve years old. Over the years he and his team have built out the techniques and equipment to help homeowners across Schenectady, Albany and the surrounding Capital Region reach their landscaping goals.

We’re an eco-friendly company that practices organic lawn care — no chemicals, no pesticides. And because Wes stays hands-on with every project, you get the same standard of work and the same accountable point of contact from the first quote to the final cleanup.

  • Owner-operated & hands-on
  • Design through maintenance
  • Organic, eco-friendly practices
  • Year-round, including snow
