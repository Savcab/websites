The Close to Nature approach

Gardens designed to work with Vermont, not against it

For over twenty years, Old World Garden Design has planned residential gardens around Burlington that read as if they were always meant to be there. The work begins with the site — its light, soil, slope and existing trees — and builds outward with plants that suit it, favoring native and ecologically minded choices wherever they earn their place.

The result is a garden with real structure: beds that hold their shape through the seasons, plantings that mature gracefully, and a landscape that asks less of you each year rather than more.

"An ecologically inspired landscape approach, guided by Close to Nature principles."

What we do

Design, planting, and the care that keeps it right

A focused studio practice — every garden is designed and overseen personally, from the first site walk through the seasons that follow.

Landscape design

Full residential garden design — reading the site, then planning beds, paths, structure and flow into a plan you can build in stages.

Native & ecological planting

The designer

Silvia Jope, landscape designer

Old World Garden Design has been designing and caring for Vermont gardens since 2004. The practice grew out of a simple conviction: that the best gardens are ecological ones — planned around the land, planted with intention, and given the seasonal attention that lets a design settle in and thrive.

That approach has earned recognition from the Vermont Nursery & Landscape Association, including the Honor Award in 2014 and Judges' Choice in 2015 — but the work that matters is the gardens themselves, and the homeowners who have trusted the studio for a decade or more.

Silvia Jope

Landscape Designer & Founder, Old World Garden Design
