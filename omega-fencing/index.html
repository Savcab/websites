Good to know

Fence questions, answered

A few of the things Kasson-area homeowners ask before we get started.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out, walk the property line with you, talk through materials and gates, and give you a clear written estimate at no cost and with no pressure.

Which fence material is right for me?

It depends on what matters most — privacy, upkeep, pet containment or budget. Vinyl is low-maintenance and private, wood is classic and warm, chain-link is tough and affordable, and ornamental aluminum gives a clean wrought-iron look. We'll help you weigh them on-site.

Do you install gates and double drive gates?

We do — and we treat them as the most important part of the job. Gates that match the grade and latch correctly are exactly the detail our customers call out in their reviews.

Can you repair my existing fence instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. Leaning posts, storm damage, a sagging gate or a few bad sections can usually be fixed. Send us a photo or have us take a look and we'll tell you honestly what it needs.

What areas do you cover?

Kasson and the surrounding Rochester-area towns including Mantorville, Dodge Center, Byron, Pine Island, Stewartville and Rochester itself. A short drive past that? Just ask.
