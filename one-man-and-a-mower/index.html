Good to know

Questions folks usually ask.

Do you really do all of this yourself?

Yes — that's the whole idea behind the name. When you hire One Man And A Mower, the person quoting your job is the person doing it and the person you call afterward. For bigger builds I'll occasionally bring a hand, but it's always run and finished by me.

What does an estimate cost?

Nothing. I come out, look at your property in person, and give you a clear price before any work starts. No obligation and no pressure — if it's not the right fit, that's completely fine.

Can you mow on a regular schedule?

Absolutely. Weekly or every-other-week mowing is a big part of what I do around Port Townsend, and I'll set a rhythm that keeps your lawn looking sharp through the growing season. One-time mows and cleanups are welcome too.

Do you haul away the debris?

Yes — I bring my own truck and trailer, so brush, branches, clippings and cleanup debris leave with me. You're left with a clean property, not a pile to deal with.
