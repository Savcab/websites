Good to Know

Questions homeowners ask us

What exactly is concrete landscape curbing?

It's a continuous concrete border formed and shaped right in your yard — no separate blocks or seams. It edges beds, lawns, trees, and driveways with one clean, curved or straight line.

Can you match a color or a stamped pattern?

Yes. We offer integral colors and stamped patterns — cobblestone, slate, brick-look and more — finished with a sealer, so the curb complements your home and existing hardscape.

Does curbing really cut down on yard work?

It helps a lot. A defined edge keeps grass from creeping into beds and mulch from washing into the lawn, and a flush mow strip lets the mower ride the line — so there's less trimming and edging to do.

Why are you not the cheapest quote?

Because we use top-of-the-line materials from the admixtures to the finishing sealer. If price is your only concern we may not be the right fit — but if you want an edge that still looks sharp years from now, that's us.

What areas do you serve?

Spirit Lake and the surrounding North Idaho Panhandle — Rathdrum, Athol, Hayden, Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls and the lakes country nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
