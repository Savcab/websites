What we do

One crew for the whole property

From a hazardous tree over the roof to the beds along the walkway, Orellana handles the heavy tree work and the everyday landscape care — so you keep one number for it all.

Tree Removal & Crane Work

Large, hazardous, and storm-damaged trees taken down in tight spaces — including crane removals of 70–80 ft oaks close to houses, septic, and structures.

Trimming & Pruning

Careful crown thinning, deadwood removal, and shaping that keeps mature trees healthy and your yard clear of overgrowth.

Stump Grinding

Old stumps ground below grade and cleaned up, so you get the whole footprint of your yard back for lawn or new beds.

Lawn Care & Mowing

Regular mowing, edging, and turf care that keeps your lawn striped, green, and healthy through the Virginia growing season.

Landscape Design & Planting

Planting beds, shrubs, mulch, and river-rock features designed and installed to fit your home and your slope.

Hardscaping & Stone Steps

Patios, walkways, retaining walls, and natural-stone steps that turn a tricky grade into usable, finished outdoor space.

Also offering seasonal spring & fall cleanups, mulching, snow removal, gutter cleaning, and power washing. Ask about your project →
