How it works

A simple path to a brand-new pool surface

No guesswork, no surprises — just three clear steps.

1

Free on-site assessment

We look at your pool, talk through your goals, and give you an honest, written quote — no pressure.

2

Careful prep & resurfacing

We drain, chip out the old surface, mask everything off, and apply a clean, smooth new plaster finish.
