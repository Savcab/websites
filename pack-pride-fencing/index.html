Good to know

Fencing questions, answered

How do I get a quote, and does it cost anything?

Quotes are free. Call or text us at (509) 671-3763, or fill out the form on this page. We'll come walk the property, talk through material and layout, and give you a clear written estimate — with no pressure to decide on the spot.

What kind of fence should I get?

It depends on what you want the fence to do. Vinyl is a low-maintenance choice for clean privacy. Cedar wood gives a natural, classic look. Chain-link is durable and cost-effective for yards and commercial lots. Field fence is great for dividing acreage and holding livestock. We'll help you weigh privacy, budget and the look you're after.

What wood do you use for wood fences?

Cedar — only cedar. It naturally resists rot and weather, and it holds up well in our northeast Washington climate, so your fence looks good for years.

Can you build on a rocky or sloped property?

Yes — rocky, sloped and wooded lots are exactly what we work in around here. We plan the approach for what's likely underground before we start, and we'll work with you on the right design for the terrain.

Do you install gates too?

We do. Walk gates, double-drive gates and metal driveway entries, built to match your fence and set to swing true season after season.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Newport, WA and build throughout Pend Oreille County and the surrounding border country — including Usk, Cusick, Dalkena, Diamond Lake, Sacheen Lake and nearby. If you're not sure whether we reach you, just ask.
