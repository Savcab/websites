Good to Know

Fencing questions, answered

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We'll come out, measure, talk through materials and layout, and give you a clear written quote at no cost and with no pressure. Call (208) 889-8459 or use the form to get started.

What fence materials do you install?

Wood (privacy, board-on-board, horizontal-slat), vinyl, chain-link, ornamental and aluminum, plus ranch and acreage fencing. We'll help you choose what fits your home, privacy needs, and budget.

Can you repair a fence instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. Leaning posts, sagging panels, storm damage, or a previous DIY job that didn't hold — we diagnose what failed and reinforce it properly. If a full replacement makes more sense, we'll tell you that honestly.

Do you build gates too?

Yes — walk-through and double-drive gates, including custom wood and ornamental gates, built square and hung to swing and latch right. We'll match the gate to your fence so it all reads as one project.

What areas do you serve?

Post Falls, Coeur d'Alene, Hayden, Rathdrum, Hauser, Dalton Gardens and the surrounding Kootenai County / North Idaho panhandle. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

Do you stand behind your work?

We do. We back our workmanship and review the finished fence with you before we call it done. If something isn't right, we make it right — that's how we've earned a 4.9-star reputation.
