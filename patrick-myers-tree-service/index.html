Frequently asked questions

Do you remove large or dangerous trees close to a house?+

Yes — that's the work we're known for. When a tree is too big or too close to a building, shed or power line to simply fell, our crew climbs and rigs it down piece by piece so it comes apart in a controlled way, away from anything that can be damaged.

How do I get a price?+

Call or text us at (541) 756-3197. You're welcome to text a photo of the tree so we can get an idea right away, but for removals we'll usually come look in person — access and what's nearby make a big difference — and then give you a clear estimate.

Do you handle emergency and storm-damaged trees?+

Yes. Coastal storms bring trees and big limbs down on driveways, roofs and fences. We respond to emergencies to make the site safe and clear the tree. If it's urgent, call us — that's the fastest way to reach the crew.

Do you grind the stump and clean up the debris?+

We chip the brush and haul off the debris as part of the job, and we can grind the stump below grade so you're not left with a hazard or something to mow around. Just let us know you'd like the stump handled when you call.

What areas do you serve?+

We're based in Coos Bay and work throughout the bay and the surrounding Coos County coast — including North Bend, Charleston, Eastside, Empire and nearby communities. A short drive out is no problem; just ask when you call.

When are you open?+

Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We're closed Sundays for regular work, but for genuine tree emergencies it's always best to call.
