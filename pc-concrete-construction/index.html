Good to know

Concrete questions, answered

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We walk the site, talk through what you want, and give you a clear estimate at no cost — with no pressure to book.

How big a job will you take?

Anything from a small back-patio pad to a 30×50 property slab. The same crew handles residential and light-commercial flatwork at any size.

Can concrete really hold up out here?

With the right base prep, jointing and curing for high-desert soil and heat, yes. That groundwork is exactly the part we don’t cut corners on.

Do you do decorative finishes?

We offer stamped patterns, broom and trowel finishes, color and exposed-aggregate looks — not just a plain gray pour.

Which areas do you serve?

Twentynine Palms, Joshua Tree, Yucca Valley, Landers, Morongo Valley and the surrounding Morongo Basin and San Bernardino County desert.
