Good to know

Pressure washing questions, answered

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water for hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure with cleaning solution for delicate surfaces — siding, roofs, painted wood — so they get clean without damage.

Will it damage my siding or roof?

Not when it's done right. That's exactly why we soft wash siding and roofs instead of blasting them — the method is matched to the material every time.

How much does a job cost?

It depends on the surfaces and the square footage. We give a clear, up-front estimate before any work begins — just call or use the form for a free quote.

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Both. We clean residential driveways, decks and siding as well as commercial storefronts, walkways and building exteriors around Bozeman and Belgrade.

What areas do you serve?

Bozeman, Belgrade and the surrounding Gallatin Valley including Four Corners and Gallatin Gateway. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

When's the best time to have it done?

Spring is popular for clearing off a winter of salt and grime, but exterior cleaning is worthwhile any time surfaces look dull. We're available seven days a week.
