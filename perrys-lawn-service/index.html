Questions we hear a lot

Do you deliver firewood and mulch, or just lawn care?

Both. Alongside mowing and landscaping, we deliver split firewood — bagged and stored in bins so it stays dry — and bulk mulch. We can drop the mulch off, or deliver and install it in your beds.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Clark and serve the surrounding mid-Missouri towns including Sturgeon, Centralia, Hallsville, Higbee, Moberly and north Columbia. If you’re nearby and not sure, just call — we’ll tell you straight.

How do I get a price?

Call or text (573) 682-0220, or fill out the quick form below. We’ll look at the job and give you a clear, no-pressure estimate — whether it’s a one-time cleanup, recurring mowing, or a delivery.

Yes. We handle one-time jobs and recurring weekly or bi-weekly mowing, with trimming and edging each visit. We’ll set a rhythm that keeps your lawn looking its best.
