What we do

Full-service lawn care, start to finish

From a clean weekly cut to a brand-new lawn, Piedmont handles the work that keeps your property looking its best — and keeps it that way through the season.

Lawn Maintenance & Mowing

Crisp, consistent weekly or bi-weekly cuts with clean edging and blowing — the dependable routine that keeps your yard sharp all season.

Core Aeration & Overseeding

Relieve compacted soil and thicken thin turf. We assess your lawn, pull cores, and overseed so grass comes back fuller and greener.

Fertilization & Weed Control

A healthy lawn starts below the surface. Targeted feeding and weed treatments keep your turf thick enough to crowd weeds out.

Mulch & Bed Installation

Fresh mulch, clean bed lines, and decorative stone that frame your home. Trimmed shrubs and crisp edges that make the whole yard read finished.

Sod & New-Lawn Care

Just laid new sod or starting from bare ground? We bring the watering, mowing and feeding know-how to grow it into a lawn you're proud of.

Seasonal Cleanups

Leaf removal, debris haul-off and shape-ups that reset your property between seasons — and a fast turnaround when you're getting a home market-ready.

Common questions

How do I get a quote?

Call (706) 762-8585 or fill out the estimate form. We'll look over your property — in person or by satellite — and get you a clear, upfront price, usually quickly.

Do you offer recurring mowing?

Yes. Most clients are on a regular weekly or bi-weekly schedule so the lawn stays consistently sharp, but we also handle one-time and seasonal jobs.

What areas do you serve?

Adairsville and the surrounding Bartow County communities, including Calhoun, Cartersville, Kingston and White. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

I just had sod installed — can you help?

Absolutely. New sod needs the right watering, mowing and feeding to take hold. We bring that expertise so your new lawn establishes into a healthy, lasting one.

Yes — fresh mulch, decorative stone, clean bed lines, edging and shrub trimming. We handle the details that make the whole property look finished.
