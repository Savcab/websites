Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. We come out to your property, look at the space and drainage, talk through what you’d like, and give you a clear written estimate — all at no cost and with no pressure.
What areas do you serve?
We’re based in Coldwater and serve Branch County, including Quincy, Bronson, Union City, Sherwood and the surrounding lake communities. Not sure if you’re in range? Give us a call.
What does “environmentally conscious” landscaping mean?
It means choosing the right plants for our Michigan climate and your specific spot, building healthy soil, and designing beds that drain properly. The payoff is a landscape that needs less water and upkeep and holds up beautifully over time.
Do you handle both design and installation?
Yes. From defined beds and flower plantings to retaining walls, walkways and edging, we plan it and install it ourselves — so the vision and the finished work line up.
When is the best time to schedule clean-ups?
Spring clean-ups are best booked as the snow clears, and fall clean-ups once the leaves come down. Because spots fill up fast in those windows, it’s worth reaching out early to get on the schedule.
