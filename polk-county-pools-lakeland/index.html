What we build

From bare backyard to finished paradise — we self-perform the whole build.

Our design team and project manager work closely with you to make the most of your space, your creativity, and your budget. Three ways we help homeowners across Lakeland & Polk County.

Custom Pool Construction

New gunite pools and spas designed around your yard. Our in-house crew handles excavation, rebar, plumbing and tile; Elite Gunite shoots the shell; we finish in authentic Pebbletec and frame it in stone-paver decking.

Major Renovations

We transform tired, outdated pools into something modern and extraordinary — using the same materials and techniques from our new builds to reshape decking, resurface, and bring your backyard back to life.

Design & Consultation

We can help design the pool of your dreams — even outside our usual service area. We visit your property, refine the design with you, and guide you through engineering and permitting so you are ready to build with confidence.

Finishes that last in Florida

Authentic Pebbletec, framed in natural stone.

We only offer authentic Pebbletec finishes. Pebbletec is far superior to other options — the most elegant, durable finish available, and it carries a lifetime warranty in the state of Florida.

Around the water we specialize in natural stone pavers, concrete pavers, and textured concrete, all chosen to stand up to Florida’s heavy rainfall. The result is an outdoor space that is as durable as it is beautiful.

  • Lifetime Pebbletec warranty in Florida
  • Natural & concrete pavers built for heavy rain

Our process

Built to last, step by careful step.

Most of the work happens with our own crew on your site — here is how your pool comes together.

  1. Step 01

    Excavation & rebar

    Our in-house crew leads precise measurements, excavates the pool site, and installs rebar to the engineer’s spec so the structure is built to last.

  2. Step 02

    Gunite shell

    Elite Gunite’s skilled crews transform the rebar-filled hole into the foundation of your custom pool, with our team on site ensuring precision throughout.

  3. Step 03

    Tile & plumbing

    We set each tile with care for top-quality waterproofing, then run plumbing designed in our office — pressure-tested and inspected before it’s ever buried.

  4. Step 04

    Decking & Pebbletec

    Natural stone pavers, concrete pavers, or textured concrete frame the pool, and an authentic Pebbletec finish completes your backyard paradise.
