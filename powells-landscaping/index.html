Frequently asked questions

Do you work with part-time and out-of-town owners?

Yes — it's a lot of what we do. Many Smith Mountain Lake homes are second homes, so we're set up to keep your property maintained on a schedule and stay in close communication, whether you're here every weekend or only a few times a year.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Wirtz and work all around Smith Mountain Lake, including Moneta, Penhook, Hardy, Union Hall, and the surrounding parts of Franklin and Bedford counties. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance or just one-time projects?

Both. We handle recurring care — mowing, trimming, leaf pickup, and seasonal upkeep on a regular cadence — as well as one-time work like bed renovations, mulch, plantings, and cleanups.

How do I get a quote?

Call (540) 238-6470 or fill out the short form below. We'll look at the property — on your schedule — and give you a clear, no-pressure estimate.

What does it cost?

It depends on the property and the work involved, so we quote each job individually. Clients have described our pricing as reasonable for the quality — we'll always be upfront about it before any work begins.
