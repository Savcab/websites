What we handle

Grounds care that keeps a property working year-round

From the growing season to the first snow, PQ handles the ground under your feet — and the water, turf and fence lines around it.

Turf & lawn management

Mowing, edging, mulch and bed care, weeding and seasonal cleanups — the steady upkeep that keeps a lawn looking cared-for.

Drainage & storm-water

Rerouting drain lines, grading, and storm-water fixes that move water away from the house and stop the low spots from flooding.

Snow & ice management

Plowing, shoveling and de-icing for driveways, lots and walkways — so homes and businesses stay open and safe through the storm.

Fence installation

Vinyl privacy, picket and property-line fencing installed clean and straight — a finished edge that lasts.
