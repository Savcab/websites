Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Do you really do both lawn care and docks?

Yes — that’s our whole thing. We handle regular lawn care and landscaping plus seasonal dock install and removal, so the same crew that mows your yard can also get your dock in for the season and pull it out in the fall.

When do you install and remove docks?

Dock install happens in the spring once conditions allow, and removal happens in the fall before the ice sets in. Spots in those windows fill up, so it’s best to get on the schedule early — call or send the form and we’ll line up your dates.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Brainerd, MN and serve the surrounding Brainerd Lakes area, including Baxter, Nisswa, Pequot Lakes, Crosslake and nearby communities. If you’re a short drive out, call and we’ll let you know if we can reach you.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text us at (218) 308-5676, or fill out the free-estimate form below with a few details about your property and what you need. We’ll take a look, talk through timing, and give you a straightforward estimate.

Do you take on one-time projects, or just regular service?

Both. We handle recurring lawn care and seasonal dock service, and we also take on one-off yard projects, plantings, patios, and cleanups. Tell us what you’re after and we’ll find a way to fit it in.
