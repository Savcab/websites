What we do

Turf-health work that actually shows

We're not a mow-and-blow crew. Premier Pro Scapes focuses on the ground game — leveling, aeration, and top-dressing — the work that turns a patchy, uneven yard into a lawn that stays thick and green.

Lawn Top-Dressing

Our signature service — a fine, screened layer of soil worked into your turf to smooth low spots, feed the roots, and encourage thicker, healthier growth. It's the work our reviewers keep coming back for.

Our most-requested service →

Core Aeration

Pulling plugs to relieve compaction so water, air, and nutrients reach the root zone — the essential first step before overseeding or top-dressing.

Overseeding

Fresh, quality seed matched to your turf to fill in thin, bare patches and build a denser, more even lawn season over season.

Lawn Leveling & Sanding

Filling ruts, dips, and settled areas so your yard drains right and mows clean — no more scalped high spots or soggy low ones.

Also handling sod installation and irrigation as part of a full lawn renovation. Not sure what your yard needs? Ask for a free assessment.

Why top-dressing

The difference is under your feet

Most lawn problems — the bumps, the bare patches, the water that pools after a storm — start at the soil. Top-dressing fixes them at the source instead of just covering them up.

  • Smoother, more even ground — low spots filled so your mower stops scalping the high ones.
  • Healthier roots — a nutrient-rich layer that feeds the turf and improves the soil over time.
  • Thicker, greener growth — paired with aeration and overseeding for a lawn that fills in and stays that way.
