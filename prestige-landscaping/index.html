Common questions

The things Hayden homeowners ask most about irrigation and lawn care.

When should I schedule my sprinkler blowout?

In North Idaho, plan your fall blowout before the first hard freeze — typically mid-to-late October. Waiting too long risks water freezing in the lines and cracking pipes or valves underground. Get on the schedule early; the last weeks before a freeze book up fast.

What is backflow testing and do I really need it?

A backflow assembly keeps irrigation water from flowing back into your drinking-water supply. Many jurisdictions require an annual test and report on file. We perform the test, document the results, and let you know if anything needs attention — so your system stays compliant and your water stays safe.

Do you handle both irrigation and regular lawn care?

Yes. We cover the full seasonal cycle — spring turn-on, backflow testing, sprinkler repairs, mowing and maintenance, fertilization and weed control, and fall blowout. Many homeowners keep it all on one schedule with us so nothing slips through the cracks.
