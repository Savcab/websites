Three decades on the coast

A reputation the low country keeps coming back to.

Priority Landscaping was established in 1996 on Folly Beach, and has grown into a fully licensed and insured, turnkey residential and commercial landscape company serving Charleston and the surrounding islands.

We design and install, then stay to maintain — turf, irrigation, lighting, hardscape, and the coastal drainage and erosion work that keeps a low-country property intact through the seasons. We're a local company built on a simple line we've kept since day one: "It's important to keep our money local."

Built for the tides

Landscapes that hold their ground.

On the Charleston coast, water decides everything. A beautiful yard that floods is a temporary yard — which is why so much of our work happens below the surface: grading, channel drains, catch basins, berms and plantings engineered to move tidal water where it belongs.

It's the difference between a landscape that looks good on install day and one that still looks good three decades on.
