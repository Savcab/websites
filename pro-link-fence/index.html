Good to know

Fence & gate questions, answered

Do you really do automatic and electronic gates, or just fencing?
Both. Alongside cedar, chain-link, vinyl and aluminum fencing, we build custom walk and drive gates and install automatic gate openers — and we service the electronics when they need it. If you want a gate that opens on its own, ask us about it.
What materials do you install?
Western red cedar and other wood privacy fences, chain-link, vinyl, and ornamental aluminum — plus garden and deer fencing, trellis and custom arbors. Tell us the look and the job, and we'll recommend what holds up best for your spot on the peninsula.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Port Townsend and work across East Jefferson County — Port Hadlock, Irondale, Chimacum, Port Ludlow, Marrowstone, Cape George, Discovery Bay and Quilcene — with Sequim a short drive away. Not sure if you're in range? Give us a call.
How much does a fence cost?
It depends on the material, how much fence, the terrain, and whether you're adding gates or an automatic opener. The best way to get a real number is a free on-site estimate — we'll measure, walk the line with you and give you an honest price with no surprise add-ons. Call (360) 385-9566 to set one up.
How do I get a quote?
Call or text (360) 385-9566, or fill out the estimate form above. Tell us roughly how much fence you need, what it's for, and whether gates or access are involved, and we'll get back to you to set up a time to come out.
