Good to know

Common questions

Do you really bring a crane?

Yes. For large, dead or dangerous trees that can't be safely dropped by hand, we bring a crane and lift the tree out in sections — the safest way to clear a big tree close to a house, fence or power line.

Do you grind the stump too?

We do. We can take the whole job from standing tree to a ground-out stump and a clean, plantable spot — you don't have to line up a separate stump company.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the tree's size, location and how tricky access is — a small backyard tree is very different from a big crane job. The estimate is always free and there's no obligation, so call and we'll give you a straight number.

What about the mess afterward?

Cleanup is included. We run our own grapple truck and roll-off dumpsters, so the wood, brush and chips are loaded and hauled off — we leave the site raked and clear.

Can you come out for a storm-damaged tree?

Yes — we're open seven days a week, 8am to 8pm, and respond the same day when we can for urgent hazards like a split or fallen tree. Call as soon as it happens.

Which areas do you serve?

West Bountiful and the surrounding Davis County communities — Bountiful, North Salt Lake, Centerville, Woods Cross, Farmington, Kaysville and nearby. If you're close by, just ask.
