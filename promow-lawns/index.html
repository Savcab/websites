Frequently asked questions

How much does lawn service cost?
Pricing depends on your lot size, the service and how often you'd like us out. The best way to get an exact number is a free estimate — call (573) 579-3323 or send the form and we'll give you straightforward pricing with no surprises.
Do you offer weekly and bi-weekly mowing?
Yes. Most homeowners go weekly during peak growing season and bi-weekly in the shoulder months. We'll recommend a schedule that keeps your lawn looking its best for your grass type and budget.
Do you handle commercial properties?
We do. ProMow serves both residential and commercial clients across Cape Girardeau — scheduled grounds maintenance, seasonal cleanups and snow removal, all on one dependable account.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Cape Girardeau and cover the city plus nearby communities across Cape Girardeau County, including Jackson, Scott City, Gordonville and Fruitland. If you're not sure you're in range, just ask.
Do you really plow snow in the winter?
Yes — when the snow flies, we're out plowing driveways and commercial lots. Keeping the same crew year-round means you have one reliable number to call no matter the season.
Can you design and build a patio, not just mow?
Absolutely. Alongside lawn care we design and install landscaping, planting beds and full hardscapes — paver patios, walkways, retaining and seat walls, flagstone and fire features. Ask for a project estimate.
