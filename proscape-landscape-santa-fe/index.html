Why neighbors call us

One crew for the whole property — design to upkeep.

A lot of Santa Fe landscapes get planned by one company, planted by another and then left to fend for themselves. We handle the full arc: laying out a design that fits the high-desert climate, installing it, and coming back to keep it healthy season after season.

That covers native and water-wise gardens, stonework and gravel, irrigation, and the standing weekly maintenance that keeps homes, HOAs and commercial grounds looking cared-for.

Water-wise by design

Native plantings, gravel and stone chosen for Santa Fe's dry, high-elevation summers — landscapes that look right and don't fight the climate.

We actually answer

Call for a quote and you'll hear back. Reviewers point it out — the company that returned the call and did the work promptly.

Homes to HOAs

From a single backyard install to weekly grounds maintenance under a standing HOA contract — the same crew, the same standard.

Year-round care

Planting, cleanups, irrigation set-up and winterization — the seasonal work that keeps a landscape from sliding backward.

What we do

Full-service landscaping for Santa Fe properties

Five things we're asked for most — handled start to finish.

01

Landscape design & installation

Planning and building new landscapes — layout, planting beds, stonework and flagstone paths tailored to your lot and the high-desert setting.

02

Xeriscape & native planting

Drought-tolerant, native and adapted plantings with gravel and rock — beautiful landscapes that sip water instead of guzzling it.

03

Landscape maintenance

Regular upkeep — trimming, bed care, seasonal cleanups and the ongoing attention that keeps a property looking its best.

04

Irrigation & winterization

Irrigation set-up and repair, plus fall winterization so your system comes through the cold and is ready to run again in spring.

05

Commercial & HOA grounds

Standing weekly maintenance for homeowners' associations and commercial properties — dependable grounds care on a schedule you can count on.
