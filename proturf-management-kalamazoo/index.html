We're a Kalamazoo design-build landscape and property management company — patios and walkways, planting beds, lawns and the upkeep that keeps them right. We treat every yard as a design problem first: how the hardscape meets the house, how water moves off the site, and how it will read once the plantings fill in.

Then we build it the slow way — excavation, a compacted base, the right grade and drainage, pavers set and beds planted by hand. And because Southwest Michigan winters are real, we come back through the season for mowing, clean-ups and snow.

How we work

Design, build, and kept up through the seasons.

A straightforward path from the first look at your yard to the season-to-season upkeep.

Walk the property

We come out, look at the yard, and talk through what you're picturing and how the space needs to work day to day.

Design & estimate

You get a clear plan and a written estimate — materials, layout and price laid out, no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons.

Build on a proper base

We excavate, compact the sub-base, set the grade for drainage, and lay the pavers or plant the beds by hand — the slow part, done right.

Maintain it

Mowing, clean-ups and winter plowing when you want them, so the work keeps looking right season after season.

What we do

One crew, from design to upkeep.

Outdoor work start to finish — designed, built and then maintained through the seasons. The same crew that plans your project is the one that shows up to build it and look after it.
