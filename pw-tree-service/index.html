Questions homeowners ask

Do you handle really large or hard-to-reach trees?

Yes — that's our specialty. With a crane and bucket truck we take down big hardwoods, multi-trunk trees, and trees in tight spots near houses, fences, and power lines that smaller crews can't safely reach.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the size of the tree, how many you need done, and access to the site. The honest answer is we'll come look first — the on-site estimate is free, and the price we give is the price you pay.

Do you clean up and haul everything away?

Always. Hauling off the logs, limbs, and chips and raking the area back is part of every job, not an add-on. We aim to leave your yard cleaner than we found it.

Can you clear a whole lot, not just one tree?

Absolutely. We clear wooded lots for building, fence lines, and pasture — trees, brush, and stumps — and leave the ground ready to work.

What about storm damage or a tree on my house?

Call us. Down trees, split limbs, and trees on structures are the kind of urgent work we get out for — reach us at (803) 246-0222 and we'll get you scheduled to clear it.
