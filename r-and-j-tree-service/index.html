Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you take on really big or dangerous trees?

Yes — that's our specialty. Oversized cottonwoods and elms, trees leaning on a structure, dead or storm-split trunks, and tight removals between buildings are exactly the jobs we're set up for, with a bucket truck and proper rigging.

Can you remove tree roots that got into my septic or sewer line?

We do. When invasive roots break into a septic or sewer line, we remove the offending tree and the roots causing the damage. It's heavy, specialized work — and one of the problems homeowners call us for most.

How fast can you come out?

We're open seven days a week and reachable into the evening, so most quotes happen quickly — often the same week. For storm damage or a hazardous tree, call and we'll prioritize getting out to make it safe.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the tree and the access, and give you an honest price at no cost. No obligation to book.

Do you grind the stump and clean up after?

We can grind the stump down well below ground level so there's no regrowth or tripping hazard, and we chip branches and haul off the wood and debris so your yard is left clean.

What areas do you cover?

Farmington and the surrounding Four Corners — Aztec, Bloomfield, Kirtland, Flora Vista and across San Juan County. If you're nearby and unsure, give us a call.
