What we do

Full-service lawn care and landscaping

Whether you need the grass kept tidy every week or a backyard transformed with stone, we cover the whole property — no juggling multiple contractors.

Lawn Mowing & Edging

Reliable weekly or bi-weekly mowing, crisp edging and trimming that keeps your lawn looking cared-for all season.

Sod & New Lawns

Fresh sod installation and lawn renovation that turns bare, patchy ground into thick, even turf that takes root fast.

Hedge & Shrub Trimming

Shaping, pruning and shrub care that keeps borders neat and helps your plantings stay healthy and full.

Paver Patios & Walkways

Custom paver patios, walkways and sitting areas built on a proper compacted base so they stay flat and solid for years.

Retaining Walls & Steps

Stone and block retaining walls plus garden steps that tame slopes, stop erosion and make hillsides usable.

Grading & Cleanup

Yard grading, leaf and debris cleanup, and seasonal tidy-ups that reset the property and keep water moving the right way.
