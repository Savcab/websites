Good to Know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We'll come look at the site, take measurements, and give you a clear written estimate at no cost and with no pressure. Call (208) 421-1077 or send your details through the form.

Do you work for homeowners, builders, or both?

Both. We build outdoor fireplaces, patios, walls, and stonework directly for homeowners, and we handle foundations, footings, and flatwork for local contractors and spec-home builds.

What kinds of concrete finishes do you offer?

Standard broom-finish flatwork, plus decorative options like stamped concrete (stone, brick, and slate patterns) and integral or acid stains, sealed to hold up to Idaho weather.

Can you build a complete outdoor living space?

Absolutely — that's some of our favorite work. Fireplaces, fire pits, patios, seating walls, and the stonework that ties it all together, designed and built as one project.

Will a retaining wall hold up to freeze-thaw?

When it's built right, yes. Proper base preparation and drainage behind the wall are what keep it from shifting through Idaho's freeze-thaw cycles — and that's exactly where we don't cut corners.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Kimberly and work throughout the Twin Falls area and greater Magic Valley — Twin Falls, Hansen, Filer, Jerome, Buhl, and the surrounding towns. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
