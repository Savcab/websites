Good to Know

Questions We Hear a Lot

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the tree's size, location, and how tricky the access is. That's why our estimates are free and done on-site — you'll get a clear price before any work starts, with no surprises.

Can you take down a tree right next to my house?

Yes. Tight removals are where we shine — using a crane, bucket truck, and climbing to bring big trees down in controlled sections, even when there's a roof or fence inches away.

Do you clean up afterward?

Always. We haul off the debris and leave your property tidy. Customers regularly mention how clean we leave the site — it's part of the job, not an add-on.

Do you offer payment plans?

For larger jobs, we've worked out approved terms and payment plans with customers before. Ask us — we'd rather get your tree handled than have you wait.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes, stump grinding is available so you can reclaim the space. Let us know when you book and we'll quote it together with the removal.

What if a storm just brought a tree down?

Call us. We're open seven days a week and respond quickly to wind and storm damage to clear the hazard and haul it away.
