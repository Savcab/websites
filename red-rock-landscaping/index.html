Good to know

Questions, answered.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Jones, OK and serve the surrounding northeast Oklahoma City metro — including Choctaw, Luther, Arcadia, Nicoma Park and nearby communities. Call us and we'll let you know if your address is in range.

Do you do one-time projects or only ongoing service?

Both. We design and install new flower beds and stone features as one-time projects, and we offer ongoing mowing and bed maintenance — many of our clients have us do both.

How do I get a quote?

Call (405) 394-3083 or fill out the quick form below with a little about your yard. We'll take a look and give you a clear, fair price — no pressure.

Can you start on short notice?

Often, yes. We're easy to communicate with and do our best to get you on the schedule quickly — even on short notice when we can.

What kind of landscaping work do you handle?

Flower-bed design and planting, trees and shrubs, mulch and edging, natural-stone paths and accents, boulder and water features, plus regular lawn mowing, leaf removal and seasonal cleanups.

Are you local?

Yes — Red Rock Landscaping is a local, owner-run business right here in Jones. Many of the yards we care for belong to neighbors we've worked with for over ten years.
