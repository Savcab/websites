Our approach

The finished stone is what you see. The base underneath is what makes it last.

We treat a yard as a design problem first and a build second. Before a stone gets set, we look at how the space is used, where water goes, and how each part — patio, wall, path, and planting — should fit together.

Then we build it properly: a solid base, honest drainage, and stonework laid to hold its line through Western New York winters. It's the difference between a patio that looks good on install day and one that still looks right a decade on.

What we do

Design-build, start to finish

Hardscape and planting handled by the same crew that designs it — so the pieces fit.

Flagstone & stone patios

Irregular flagstone, stone slabs, and sitting areas laid on a proper base to stay level for years.

Walkways & garden paths

Stone and paver walks with clean borders that tie the front and back of the yard together.

Retaining & raised-bed walls

Segmental block and stone walls that hold grade, level a slope, and frame the plantings above.

Landscape & planting design

Foundation plantings, perennial beds, and full planting plans laid out to suit the site and the light.

Pool decks, fire pits & outdoor living

Stamped-concrete decks, stone surrounds, fire pits, and rock gardens that turn a yard into a place to sit.

Drainage & grading

Regrading and drainage so the finished yard sheds water the right way and the hardscape stays put.

How we work

A straightforward way to get it built

01

Walk the site

02

Design & estimate

We lay out the plan — patio, walls, plantings — and give you a clear estimate before anything starts.

03
