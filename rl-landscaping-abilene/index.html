What we do

Full-service landscaping for Big Country homes & businesses

We handle the whole property — design and build, the lawn underfoot, the water that keeps it green, and the lighting that shows it off after dark.

Landscape Design & Installation

Full residential and commercial landscapes — beds, plantings, borders and new-build installs designed to fit your home and hold up in the West Texas climate.

Turf & Lawn Rebuilds

Weedy or worn-out yards leveled and re-laid with fresh sod or artificial turf — from full lawn rebuilds to clean, low-maintenance backyard putting-green looks.

Irrigation Systems

Efficient sprinkler and drip systems installed and repaired by a licensed irrigation specialist on our team — including smart, Wi-Fi controllers that save water.

Hardscape & Stonework

River rock, stone edging and curbing, pergolas and patios that add structure, cut back on mowing and give your yard clean, finished lines.

Landscape & Outdoor Lighting

Path, tree and architectural lighting that makes a property look its best after sunset — safety, curb appeal and warmth, professionally installed.

Holiday Lighting

Abilene's go-to for Christmas and holiday lights — designed, installed, maintained and taken down, so your home shines all season without the ladder.
