What we do

One team for the entire yard

RMPS handles design and construction together, so the patio, the turf, the irrigation and the planting all come from the same crew — coordinated from first sketch to final walk-through.

Full backyard design-build

Complete outdoor spaces designed and built end to end — layout, hardscape, greenery and lighting as one project.

Paver & concrete patios

Paver and poured-concrete patios, walkways and steps set on proper base to stay level through Colorado freeze-thaw.

Artificial turf

Low-maintenance, always-green turf over a graded, draining base — a lawn that survives the high-plains climate.

Irrigation systems

Efficient drip and sprinkler systems that keep new plantings and turf healthy without wasting water.

Flagstone & water features

Natural flagstone, boulder work and pondless water features that give a yard a finished, custom character.

Tree & plant installation

Trees, shrubs and beds chosen for the front range and placed to fill in and shade the space for years.
