Good to know

Pool plastering questions, answered

How do I know my pool needs replastering?
Common signs are rough or chalky surfaces, staining that won't scrub off, plaster that feels hollow or is flaking (pop-offs), visible cracks, or a finish that's just looking old and patchy. If you're seeing any of these, call us — we'll tell you whether a repair or a full replaster makes more sense.
What's the difference between plaster, pebble and quartz finishes?
Traditional plaster is the classic smooth white finish and the most affordable. Quartz adds crushed quartz for more durability and richer color. Pebble (exposed-aggregate) uses natural pebbles for the most durable, textured, high-end look. We'll walk you through colors and textures so you get the finish that fits your pool and budget.
How long does a replaster take?
Most pool replaster and resurfacing projects run a few days of on-site work, plus drain and refill time, depending on the size of the pool and any tile or coping work. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your quote — and we show up when we say we will.
Do you handle tile and coping too?
Yes. Waterline tile and stone coping are a big part of how a finished pool looks, so we replace and re-set them as part of a full resurface — or as a standalone job if that's all you need.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the size of your pool, the finish you choose, and whether you're adding tile, coping or repairs. Rather than guess, we give you a clear, upfront quote after looking at your pool — and our customers consistently tell us our pricing is fair.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Irving and serve all of the Dallas–Fort Worth area, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Grand Prairie, Arlington, Coppell, Grapevine, Las Colinas and the surrounding communities. Give us a call to confirm your neighborhood.
