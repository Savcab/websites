Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do you do both residential and commercial properties?
Yes — that's a big part of what sets Route 33 apart. We mow and maintain home lawns and also handle the grounds for local businesses, offices, clinics and rental properties. The Hocking Hills Animal Clinic is one of our commercial customers.
How do I get a quote?
Call or text us at (740) 216-9006 and tell us a bit about your property. We'll take a look and give you a clear, no-pressure estimate for the work.
What services do you offer?
Weekly mowing and maintenance, trimming and edging, mulch and landscape beds, river-rock and stone bed installs, shrub pruning, and spring and fall cleanups — for both homes and businesses.
Do you offer recurring service or just one-time jobs?
Both. We can set up recurring mowing and grounds maintenance to keep a property consistently sharp, or handle a one-time project like a cleanup or a new mulch bed.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Nelsonville and serve properties across Athens and Hocking counties — Athens, Logan, Buchtel, Glouster, The Plains and the surrounding Hocking Hills area. If you're nearby and not sure, just ask.
What are your hours?
We're available Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed Sundays. Call or text anytime during those hours and we'll get back to you.
