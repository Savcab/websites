Good to know

Fence staining questions, answered

Do you offer free estimates and color samples?

Yes. Call us for a free estimate and stain color samples — we work to get them to you quickly so you can choose your color before we begin.

Can you match the stain to my house?

Absolutely. We offer standard stain colors and can custom-mix a tone to complement your brick, trim or decor. When we stain in stages, we make sure the colors match.

Will you make a mess on my driveway or landscaping?

No — a clean job is something we take pride in. We protect driveways, concrete and landscaping, so you won't find stain drips or overspray when we're done.

Can you restore an old, gray or faded fence?

Yes. Weathered and faded fences are cleaned and prepped, then re-stained and sealed so an older fence can look new again.

Do you stain new fences too?

We do. A new fence should be stained and sealed in a timely manner to protect the wood, and we'll work with your schedule to get it done.

Do you stain more than just fences?

Yes — along with fences we stain and seal decks, pergolas and patio covers, and we offer decorative concrete staining and sealing for patios, drives and walkways.
