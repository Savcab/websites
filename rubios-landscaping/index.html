What we do

From a one-time cleanup to a whole new yard

We handle the dirty work and the finish work — the grading and gravel, the walls and walkways, and the green that makes it feel like home.

Yard Cleanups & Maintenance

Leaf raking, weed removal, trimming and seasonal cleanups — one visit or on a schedule that keeps the property sharp all year.

Most requested

Retaining Walls & Block Work

Tiered block and timber retaining walls that hold a slope, raised garden beds and curbed borders — built level and built to stay put.

Paver & Flagstone Walkways

Block-paver paths and flagstone patios for clean, mud-free access around the home — set on a proper base so they don't shift or sink.

Gravel & Xeriscape

Decorative gravel, flowing rock paths and low-water desert plantings — the right call for a New Mexico yard you don't have to fight all summer.

Lawns, Sod & Turf

Fresh sod and artificial turf installs that turn bare sand into a yard you actually want to spend time in — graded and edged clean.

Porch & Deck Refinishing

Wood porches and decks sanded down and sealed for weather protection — so the woodwork holds up to the desert sun and wind.

Questions we hear a lot

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. Estimates are free. We come out, walk the property, talk through what you want, and give you a fair written number with no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

Bosque Farms and the surrounding South Valley — Peralta, Los Lunas, Valencia, Los Chavez, Belen and nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Just call and ask.

Do you do small jobs or just big projects?

Both. A one-time cleanup or a single flower bed is just as welcome as a full backyard build. Tell us what you need.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job, but we're usually able to get out for a site visit the same week and onto the schedule shortly after.

Can you handle hardscape and planting together?

Yes — walls, walkways and patios plus gravel, sod, turf and beds. We can take a yard from grading all the way to the finished green in one project.
