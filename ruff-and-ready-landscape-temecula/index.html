Who we are

We build outdoor spaces the Temecula Valley trusts.

Ruff & Ready Landscape Construction is a design-build crew working across the Temecula Valley. We take a yard from bare dirt to a finished retreat — grading and drainage, retaining walls, hardscape, irrigation, turf, lighting and planting — handled by one team with one plan, so the details actually line up when the job is done.

It's hillside lots, hard soil, and a hot, dry climate out here. We build for it — engineered for slopes and terrain other crews turn down, and built to last.

What we build

Full-service landscape design & build

Everything an outdoor space needs — from the grading underneath to the lighting on top — delivered by one Temecula crew.

Retaining Walls

Engineered block and stone walls that hold back slopes and carve usable, level space out of hillside lots.

Drip & Irrigation

Drip, tree bubblers, and spray systems designed to keep plants healthy while respecting our water restrictions.

Drainage & Grading

Grading and drainage that move water away from the home and keep walls, patios, and turf sound.

Artificial Turf

Lush, water-wise turf that stays green through Temecula summers with none of the mowing or watering.

Concrete & Paver Hardscape

Driveways, walkways, and patios in stamped concrete and interlocking pavers, set on a base that lasts.

Fire Features & Outdoor Living

Fire pits, pergolas, and patio covers that turn a backyard into a place you actually want to spend time.

Landscape Lighting

Low-voltage lighting that keeps the design working after sunset and adds real curb appeal at night.
