What we do

One crew for the whole property

Ryan's Landscaping handles the full arc of outdoor work — the weekly upkeep that keeps a place sharp and the bigger projects that transform it. Here's what we're known for around Boyne City.

Lawn & Yard Maintenance

Reliable mowing, edging, trimming, weeding and bed upkeep on a schedule you can count on — the same crew showing up week after week.

Spring & Fall Clean-Ups

Leaf removal, debris hauling, bed cut-backs and seasonal resets that get your yard ready for the growing season — or buttoned up before the snow flies.

Landscape Design & Planting

Trees, shrubs and perennial beds planned and planted to fit your property — plus removals and transplants when the layout needs to change.

Paver Patios & Firepits

Custom paver patios, walkways and gas firepits built to last — finished with the detail and clean lines our clients keep recommending us for.

Shoreline & Sea Wall Work

Frontage repair, sea walls and erosion protection that hold up to high water — keeping waterfront properties on Lake Charlevoix safe and intact.

Snow & Ice Removal

Winter plowing and snow removal for homes and drives across the area, so you're never snowed in — the same dependable team, year-round.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We come out to your property, talk through what you're looking for, and put together a clear, no-obligation bid at no charge. Call (231) 330-0452 or use the form to get on the schedule.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Boyne City and work throughout Charlevoix County and the surrounding Lake Charlevoix communities — Boyne Falls, Charlevoix, East Jordan, Walloon Lake, Petoskey and nearby. If you're close by, just ask.

Do you handle both maintenance and bigger projects?

We do both. The same crew that handles weekly mowing and seasonal clean-ups also installs patios and firepits, plants and removes trees and shrubs, and takes on shoreline and sea wall work. One call covers the whole property.

Can you take care of my property year-round?

Absolutely. Many clients keep us on through every season — spring clean-up and planting, summer mowing and maintenance, fall leaf removal, and snow and ice removal once winter arrives.

Do you work on lakefront and shoreline properties?

Yes — it's some of the work we're known for. We've built sea walls and handled frontage repair and erosion protection for waterfront homes on Lake Charlevoix, including after high-water damage.
