Questions we hear a lot.

Still wondering about something? Call or text (860) 235-4158 and we’ll sort it out.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text us, or fill out the quote form below with your address and what you need. We’ll come out, look at the work in person, and give you a free, no-pressure price.

Do you do one-time jobs or just regular service?

Both. We take standing weekly and bi-weekly mowing accounts, and we also handle one-time projects like a bed redesign, a cleanup, land clearing or a tree removal.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Lisbon and serve the surrounding eastern Connecticut towns — Jewett City, Griswold, Norwich, Sprague, Canterbury, Preston, Plainfield and nearby. Not sure if you’re in range? Just ask.

Can you handle a whole front-yard redesign, not just mowing?

Yes — that’s some of our favorite work. We clear out tired beds, re-scape the front of the house, lay fresh mulch and stone, and bring in plantings so the whole property looks finished.

What are your hours?

We’re reachable seven days a week: Monday through Saturday from 7:00am to 5:00pm, and Sunday from 7:30am to 5:00pm.
