Questions homeowners ask

Can you remove a tree that’s right against my house or cabin?

Yes — that’s the heart of what we do. When a tree can’t be felled in one piece, we climb it and lower it in sections with rigging, keeping every cut away from your structure, deck, and fence.

Does my tree actually need to come down?

Not always. We’ll assess it first and tell you honestly — sometimes a structural prune or some deadwood removal solves the problem and saves the tree. We’d rather give you the straight answer.

Do you grind the stump and clean up afterward?

Yes. Stump grinding and cleanup are part of the job. We grind below grade, chip or haul the brush, and rake the site so your lot looks cared for when we leave.

How do I get an estimate?

Call us at (706) 410-4997 and describe what you’re dealing with. For most jobs we’ll come look at the tree in person before quoting, so the price reflects the real work.

What areas do you serve?

Pagosa Springs and the surrounding San Juan communities — Aspen Springs, Arboles, Chromo and across Archuleta County. A short drive out? Call and ask.

When are you available?

We’re reachable six days a week, Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The best way to start is a phone call.
