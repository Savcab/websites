Frequently asked questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We'll come out, look at your space, and give you a clear written estimate before any work starts — with no pressure to commit.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Tillamook and serve the north Oregon coast — Cannon Beach, Manzanita, Nehalem and the surrounding area — plus the Portland metro for select projects.

Do you do both the hardscape and the planting?

We do. Sandy's is a nursery and a landscaping crew under one roof, so the same team can build your patio or wall, plant the beds with our own nursery stock, and maintain it after.

Can you maintain my landscape after it's built?

Absolutely. Many clients have us back for ongoing mowing, pruning, weeding, fertilizing and seasonal cleanups so the work keeps looking its best.

What kind of hardscape do you build?

Stone patios, paver patios, retaining and rock walls, garden steps, walkways, fences and porches — built to handle coastal grade and weather.

Do you install new lawns?

Yes — by seed or sod. We grade and prep the site properly so a new lawn takes hold and stays healthy in coastal soil.
