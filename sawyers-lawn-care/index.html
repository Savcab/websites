Mulch & bed detailing

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an estimate, and is it free?

Estimates are always free and no-pressure. Call or text (765) 238-9421, or fill out the quick form below with your address and what you need. We'll take a look — in person or from photos — and give you a clear price.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Fountain City and serve the surrounding Wayne County towns, including Richmond, Centerville, Cambridge City, Hagerstown and Williamsburg. If you're nearby and not sure, just ask.

Do you only mow, or do you do landscaping too?

Both. Alongside regular mowing we do mulch and bed detailing, shrub and tree trimming, stone walkways and borders, cleanups, and bigger projects like grading and yard makeovers with our skid-steer.

Can you handle a one-time yard cleanup or refresh?

Absolutely. Plenty of our work is a single visit — a fresh mulch and detailing job, a spring or fall cleanup, or getting an overgrown yard back under control. You don't have to sign up for anything ongoing.

Do you plow snow in the winter?

Yes — we offer snow removal and driveway plowing through the winter, so the crew you trust with your lawn keeps you clear and moving year-round. Reach out before the season to get on the list.
