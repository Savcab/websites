Our story

An independently owned crew that treats your property like our own

For more than fifteen years, Green Grass has helped Lowcountry homeowners and property managers turn ordinary yards into outdoor spaces worth living in. We're a small, independently owned and operated company — which means the people who design your landscape are the same people who build and care for it.

From a downtown Charleston yard renovation to a multi-phase project on Daniel Island, our work is hands-on, personal and built to last in our hot, humid coastal climate.
