Choose your turf

The right warm-season sod for your yard

We install the highest-quality sod from trusted organic growers and help you pick the variety that suits your soil, sun and lifestyle. Four warm-season turfs thrive in the Lowcountry:

Zoysia

A beautiful, dense turf — including premium Palisades Zoysia. Lush underfoot and our most-requested resod.

St. Augustine

The classic deep, dark-green Lowcountry lawn. Broad-bladed, shade-tolerant and full.

Centipede

The low-maintenance choice — low input, low mowing, easygoing color for our sandy soils.

Bermuda

A very nice-looking, low-cut turf built for full sun and active yards. Tough and fast to fill in.

How we build a lawn that lasts

If you're looking for a quick fix, we're honest that Thorn isn't the company — we'd rather do it right the first time. Here's the process behind every install.

01

Free on-site quote

We come measure the area and build a detailed, in-depth quote — topsoil, sod and logistics — and advise on the turf type that best suits your lawn.

02

Soil & pH test

On request, we test your soil and correct pH — lime for acidic soil, sulfur or gypsum for alkaline — so the new sod roots into the right foundation.

03

Prep & flag

The day before, we clear the old lawn of materials, rocks and stumps, and flag irrigation heads, water lines and utilities to protect what's underground.

04

Install & care guidance

We lay fresh sod cleanly and leave you with new-grass care guidance to get it established. Ask about our sod warranty for added peace of mind.
