How we work

Plan it first, then build it right.

A clear path from the first look at your yard to the day it's finished and yours to enjoy.

Sit down & plan

We start at your table, not with a mower — we walk the yard, listen to what you're picturing, and plan the whole design before anything is planted.

Lay out the design

You get a clear plan for the yard — the beds, the plant choices, the borders and the layout — with honest suggestions for what will actually thrive down here.

Build it clean

Our crew installs it the way it was drawn — beds shaped, stone borders set, plants placed and lit — working tidy and finishing the details.

Keep it looking right

Once it's in, we can keep the yard maintained through the seasons so it stays looking the way it did the day we finished.
