What we build

Stonework that handles a Wisconsin shoreline.

Most of our work lives where the lawn meets the water — the patios, walls and steps that make a slope usable and keep the shoreline from washing away. Here's where we spend our days.

Paver patios

Tumbled-paver patios cut to fit and set on a proper compacted base, so they stay flat and level through every freeze-and-thaw. Fire-pit surrounds, seating walls and walkways to match.

Shoreline riprap

Graded stone riprap that armors a lakefront or riverbank against wave and ice damage — sized and placed to stop erosion and hold the bank for the long haul.

Natural stone steps

Quarried stone steps that turn a steep, slippery bank into a safe path down to the dock — bedded solid so they don't shift, and built to look like they've always belonged there.

Retaining walls

Block and boulder retaining walls that terrace a hillside into level, usable yard — engineered with the drainage behind them so they hold instead of bulging out a few seasons later.

Plantings & rock beds

Edging, decorative rock and the right plantings to finish a project — so the new patio or wall settles into the yard instead of standing alone in a patch of dirt.

Full yard transformations

Tying it all together — patio, steps, walls, shoreline and grading as one plan, so a raw or overgrown lake lot turns into an outdoor space the whole family actually uses.

Good questions

Before you call.

Do you only do hardscaping, or full landscaping too?
Hardscape is our specialty — patios, walls, steps and riprap — but we also handle the landscaping that finishes a project: grading, edging, decorative rock and plantings, so the new stonework settles into the yard instead of sitting alone in the dirt.
What makes lakefront and shoreline work different?
Water, ice and steep ground. Shorelines need properly sized riprap to take wave and ice action without washing out, and slopes need steps and walls bedded and drained so they don't shift. It's the kind of work we do most, so we know how to build it to hold.
How do your paver patios hold up to Wisconsin winters?
It comes down to the base. We excavate, lay and compact the right depth of base material, and build in drainage so water doesn't sit and heave the pavers in a freeze. Done that way, a patio stays flat and level for the long haul.
Is an estimate really free?
Yes. We'll come out, look at your site, and give you a written estimate at no cost and with no pressure. Every project is priced from what the site actually needs, so the on-site visit is how we get you a number that's real.
What's the best way to reach you?
A call or text to (715) 853-8754 is the fastest way to reach us. Tell us what you're thinking about and where you're located, and we'll set up a time to come take a look.
