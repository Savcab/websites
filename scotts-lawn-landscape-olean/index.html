About

Locally owned by Gordon Scott

Scott's Lawn & Landscape Services is a full-service landscape contractor that has served the Olean area for 24 years. Owner Gordon Scott leads the design-build work — garden design, hardscaping, paver patios, retaining walls and custom fire pits — across Olean, Allegany, Portville and Cuba, NY.

Reviewers describe Gordon's years of experience and horticulture background behind a full front-yard landscape renovation — the kind of design eye homeowners look for when pricing a patio or planting plan.
