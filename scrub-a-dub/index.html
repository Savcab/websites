Frequently asked questions

What is soft washing, and how is it different from pressure washing?
Soft washing uses a gentle, low-pressure spray combined with cleaning solution to break down mold, mildew and algae at the source. Because it doesn't rely on high pressure, it's much safer for siding, roofs and painted surfaces — and because it kills the growth rather than just rinsing it off, the results last longer. We use straight pressure only where it's appropriate, like concrete.
How fast can you come out?
Usually quickly. Most of our customers tell us they reached out and got a same-day reply and a look at the job soon after. Call or text (570) 854-5254 and we'll get you a price and a spot on the schedule.
Will soft washing damage my roof or plants?
No — that's the whole point of soft washing. The low pressure is safe for shingles and siding, and we take care around landscaping while we work. It's a far gentler approach than blasting a roof or wall with high pressure.
What does it cost?
Every property is different, so we give you a clear price after a quick look at the job — and we explain it before any work starts. Estimates are always free and there's no obligation. Reach out and we'll get you a number.
What can you clean besides houses?
Plenty — roofs, driveways, sidewalks, patios and pool decks, decks and fences, and even RVs, campers and boats. If it's outside and it's dirty, ask us and we'll let you know if we can help.
Do I need to be home for the cleaning?
Not necessarily. As long as we can access the areas to be cleaned and a water spigot, many exterior jobs can be done while you're out. We'll sort out the details when we set up your appointment.
