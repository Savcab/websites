Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you charge for estimates?
No. Estimates are always free. We'll come look at your property, talk through what you want, and give you an honest price with no obligation and no pressure.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Hamer and serve communities throughout Dillon and Marlboro counties, including Dillon, Latta, Lake View, Little Rock, Bennettsville, Marion, and Mullins. If you're nearby, just call and ask.
Do you do landscape lighting design, or just installation?
Both. We plan the lighting around your specific home or business — what to highlight, where the fixtures go, warm-white or color — then install it and fine-tune the look until you love it.
Can you fix a yard torn up by construction?
Absolutely — it's one of the things we do most. After a pool, addition, or new build, we regrade, lay fresh sod, repair damaged irrigation, and rebuild beds so the yard looks whole again.
Do you offer regular, recurring lawn care?
Yes. Many of our clients have us on a regular schedule for mowing, edging, trimming, and seasonal cleanups. Tell us how often you'd like us out and we'll set it up.
How do I get started?
