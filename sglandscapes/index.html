SGLandscapes is a design-build landscape team in Reedley, working with homeowners across the Fresno County valley. We handle the whole outdoor project — turf lawns, paver patios, fire pits, pergolas, concrete, rock and water features — from the first sketch to the final walkthrough.

Most of our work comes from neighbors who wanted a real plan before anyone touched the yard. So that is how we run every job: a visual design you can see, an in-person review before we break ground, and straight talk on timing and budget the whole way through.

How we work

A plan you can see, before anyone breaks ground.

No surprises and no guesswork. Here is how a project runs from the first phone call to the day we hand it back.

01

Walk the yard & talk budget

We come out, look at the space with you, and get honest about what the yard needs and what you want to spend.

02

A design you can picture

We put together a visual plan and review it with you in person, so you can see the finished yard before we build a thing.

03

Built by our own crew

Concrete, pavers, turf, rock, water and wood — installed on a proper base by the same team, kept clean and on schedule.

04

Walk it together

We finish the job, walk the whole space with you, and leave it ready to enjoy — most clients call us back for the next phase.
