What We Build

Design-Build Landscape & Concrete Services

Stamped & Colored Concrete

Integrally colored, stamped concrete patios, walkways and driveways — poured and sealed for a textured stone or wood look built for Central Valley summers.

Retaining Walls, Flagstone & Pavers

Dry-stacked ledgestone and keystone retaining walls, flagstone paths and paver patios set with polymeric sand — the structural stonework behind a finished backyard.

Drainage & Irrigation

Grading, drainage and irrigation systems designed for a summer-irrigation-dependent, drought-conscious climate — so new plantings and turf actually make it through July.

Synthetic Turf

Premium-grade synthetic turf installs for a yard that stays green year-round without a summer water bill.

Full Backyard Renovations

Ground-up backyard rebuilds — demo, drainage, hardscape, planting and turf coordinated as one project instead of a patchwork of separate contractors.

Maintenance

Ongoing maintenance to keep a finished landscape and hardscape looking like the day it was installed.
