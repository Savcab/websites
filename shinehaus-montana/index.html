Frequently asked questions

What is soft washing, and why does it matter?

Soft washing uses low pressure paired with the right cleaning solution to lift dirt, algae, moss and stains. It cleans thoroughly without the surface damage high pressure can cause to siding, paint, shingles and wood — which is why we lead with it on homes and roofs.

Can you safely clean my roof?

Yes. Roofs — especially shaded, north-facing ones — collect moss and black algae streaks here in the valley. We treat and clear them with a gentle soft-wash approach designed to protect the shingles rather than blast them.

How do I get a price?

Call us at (406) 407-1988 or fill out the quick quote form. Tell us what you'd like cleaned — a couple of photos help — and we'll give you a clear, upfront quote with no obligation.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Kalispell and serve the wider Flathead Valley, including Whitefish, Columbia Falls, Bigfork, Lakeside, Somers and Evergreen. If you're nearby but not listed, just ask — we likely cover you.

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not necessarily. For most exterior work you don't have to be home as long as we have access to the areas and an outdoor water source. We'll agree on all the details when we book.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the home and which surfaces you'd like cleaned, so we quote each job individually rather than guess. You'll always get the full price upfront before any work begins.
