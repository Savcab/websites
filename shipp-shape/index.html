Good to know

Questions, answered.

What areas do you serve?
We're based in Sylvester and serve homes and businesses throughout Worth County and the surrounding South Georgia area. If you're nearby and not sure whether you're on our route, give us a call at (229) 776-9884 and we'll let you know.
Do you handle both homes and commercial properties?
Yes. We take care of residential yards as well as commercial grounds — storefronts, offices and other business properties. The Sylvester–Worth County Chamber of Commerce is among the local businesses whose grounds we keep looking sharp.
Can I set up recurring service?
Absolutely. Most customers go with a weekly or bi-weekly mowing route to keep things consistent, but we also do one-time cleanups and seasonal projects. We'll set up whatever schedule works best for your property.
How do I get a quote?
Estimates are free. Call (229) 776-9884 or fill out the estimate request form below with a few details about your property, and we'll get you a clear price for the work.
What are your hours?
We're out working Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed on Sundays. If you reach our voicemail while we're on a job, leave a message and we'll get right back to you.
