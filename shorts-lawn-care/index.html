Good to know

Common questions.

Do you mow large yards and acreage?

Yes — acreage is a good bit of what we do. From a quarter-acre to a full acre and larger, we’ve got the equipment to keep big lawns cut clean and on schedule.

How do I get a price?

Call or text (423) 621-1643 and tell us about your yard. We’ll come take a look and give you a straight, fair estimate — free, with no obligation.

Can you mow on a regular schedule?

Absolutely. Most customers set up weekly or every-other-week mowing through the season so the lawn always stays in shape — we’ll work out the schedule that fits your grass.

Beyond mowing we handle trimming and edging, seasonal cleanups, leaf removal and general landscape upkeep. If it’s keeping your yard tidy, ask us — chances are we do it.
