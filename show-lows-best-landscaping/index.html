What We Build

Hardscape & landscaping for high-country yards

We handle the heavy, ground-up work most crews shy away from — and the planting and finish details that make a yard feel done. Here's what we're known for around Show Low.

Most Requested

Paver Patios & Walkways

Backyard patios, driveways, and walkways built on a properly prepped, compacted base so they stay level through freeze-and-thaw. Clean edges, tight joints, lasting results.

River Rock & Gravel Yards

Low-water river-rock and decorative gravel landscapes with weed barrier and clean borders — the practical, beautiful choice for mountain properties.

Natural Flagstone

Flagstone patios, steppers, and stone steps that work with the terrain — from sitting areas with a view to paths through boulders and pines.

Full Landscape Design & Install

Plantings, shrubs, mulch beds, trees, and grading to pull a whole property together — including the long-stalled projects other landscapers left unfinished.

Artificial Turf

Clean, green, no-mow lawns installed over a properly drained base — a year-round green space that holds up to kids, pets, and the high-country climate.

Fire Pits & Stone Features

Stacked-stone fire pits, planters, and seat walls that turn a backyard into a place you actually want to spend mountain evenings.

Hardscape Done Right

A base built to survive mountain freeze-and-thaw

In the White Mountains, a patio is only as good as what's under it. We don't cut corners on the part you can't see.

Proper excavation & compaction

We dig to the right depth and compact a graded aggregate base so pavers don't heave or settle.

Drainage planned in

Slopes and grading that move water away from your home and your hardscape — not toward it.

Crisp edges & tight joints

Clean edge restraint and joint sand for a finished look that stays put for years.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We come out to your property in Show Low or the surrounding area, look at the space, and give you a clear, written estimate at no cost or obligation.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Paver patios, driveways and walkways, river-rock and gravel yards, natural flagstone, artificial turf, fire pits and stone features, plus full landscape design and install.

Will pavers hold up to mountain winters?

When they're built right, yes. We excavate to depth and compact a proper aggregate base with planned drainage, which is what keeps pavers from heaving or settling through freeze-and-thaw.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Show Low and serve the White Mountains — including Pinetop-Lakeside, Lakeside, Snowflake, Taylor, Hon-Dah, and the surrounding Navajo County communities.

Can you finish a project another landscaper left undone?

Often, yes — it's a big part of what we do. Give us a call, tell us what's been started, and we'll come take a look and lay out a plan to get it finished right.
