Good to know

Questions homeowners ask

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out, walk the property with you, talk through what’s possible, and give you an honest estimate at no charge and no pressure.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Everything from natural stone retaining walls and bed renovations to planting, mulch and seasonal property cleanups. If it’s about making the yard look finished, it’s in our wheelhouse — including the heavier stone work a lot of crews pass on.

Which towns do you serve?

We’re based on M-68 in Alanson and work across Emmet County — Petoskey, Harbor Springs, Pellston, Indian River, Conway, Brutus and the nearby Little Traverse Bay area. If you’re close by, give us a call.

How do I get on the schedule?

Call us at (231) 675-5485 or send the quick form below. We’ll set up a time to come look, get you an estimate, and pick a date that works.

Do you handle the cleanup after a project?

Always. We leave the site tidy when the work is done — and we also offer standalone spring and fall property cleanups if that’s all you need.
