What we do

Everything it takes to finish a yard

New build with nothing but dirt, or an older place that needs a real plan — we handle the install end to end so you only deal with one crew.

01

Sprinkler & irrigation systems

Designed and trenched-in for full coverage, so a new lawn actually takes — zoned, timed and built to last through Idaho summers.

02

Sod & lawn installation

Soil brought in, leveled and graded, then fresh sod laid for an even, established lawn from day one — no waiting out a patchy seed season.

03

Planting beds, shrubs & trees

Bed layout, edging, decorative rock and the right trees and shrubs for the spot — the part that makes a yard look finished instead of empty.

04

Paver patios & walkways

Patios, paths and sitting areas laid on a proper base so they stay flat and level — the outdoor room where the yard actually gets used.

05

Retaining walls & grading

Segmental block walls and reshaped grade that handle slope and drainage — holding soil where it belongs and opening up usable, level ground.

06

Water features & finishing touches

Basalt columns, fire pits and the standout details that turn a tidy yard into one people stop and look at — done as part of the same install.

Good to know

Questions homeowners ask

A few of the things people want to know before they call. Have another? Reach out any time.

Do you handle a whole new-build yard from bare dirt?

Yes — that's most of what we do. We bring in and level the soil, set the sprinkler system, lay the sod, build the beds and add trees and hardscape, so the entire yard gets finished by one crew.

How much does a yard install cost?

It depends on the size of the lot and what you want in it — sprinklers and sod, beds, a patio, a retaining wall. The best way to get a real number is a quick on-site look, which is free. Call or request an estimate and we'll walk it with you.

Do you put in sprinklers, or just the lawn?

Both. A lawn only takes if it's watered right, so we design and install the irrigation as part of the job — zoned and timed for full coverage before the sod ever goes down.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Shelley and serve the Snake River Valley — Idaho Falls, Ammon, Blackfoot, Firth, Iona, Ucon, Rigby and the towns nearby. If you're close but not listed, just ask.
