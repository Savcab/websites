The detail that lasts

We sweat the part most people skip.

Anyone can drop mulch on a bed. The difference is what happens after the next downpour. On a slope, we set edging and lay river rock so the mulch and soil hold their place — instead of ending up at the bottom of the yard.

It's a small thing that saves you redoing the same bed every spring. That kind of attention is the whole reason our customers keep calling us back.

  • Edging set between mulch and rock to hold the line
  • River rock placed on slopes to slow runoff and washout
  • Beds finished clean, so the whole yard looks intentional
