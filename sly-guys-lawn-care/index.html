What we do

Full-season lawn care, done right the first time

From the weekly mow to the fall cleanup, Sly Guys keeps your property sharp, healthy and easy to come home to — so you never have to think about the yard.

Precision Mowing & Striping

Clean, even cuts at the right height with the sharp, parallel stripes our customers love — the look that says a lawn is genuinely cared for.

Edging & Trimming

Defined edges along drives, walks and beds, plus string-trimming around every obstacle so the whole property reads tidy from the curb.

Fertilization & Weed Control

Seasonal feeding and targeted weed treatment to thicken your turf, deepen the color and crowd out the weeds before they take hold.

Spring & Fall Cleanups

A fresh start in spring and a clean slate in fall — bed cleanup, debris haul-off and getting the lawn set up for the season ahead.

Leaf Removal

Regular leaf clearing through the fall so wet leaves never mat down and smother the grass you've worked all year to grow.

Weekly & Bi-Weekly Plans

Set-it-and-forget-it scheduled service on the cycle that fits your yard, with the same dependable crew showing up like clockwork.

Frequently asked questions

How much does lawn service cost?

Every yard is different, so we give you a free, no-pressure estimate based on your lawn's size and what you'd like done. You'll always know the price before we start.

How often will you mow?

Most customers go weekly through the growing season, but we also offer bi-weekly and one-time service. We'll recommend the cycle that keeps your lawn looking its best.

Do I need to be home for service?

No. Once we're on your schedule, our crew takes care of everything while you go about your day. We'll keep you posted and let you know if anything needs your attention.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Wamego and serve the surrounding Flint Hills and Manhattan area, including St. George, Belvue and the rest of Pottawatomie County. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
