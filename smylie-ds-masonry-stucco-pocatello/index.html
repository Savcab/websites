What we do

Stucco, stone & masonry — finished right

From a fresh stucco exterior to a stone-veneer entry or a structural block wall, we handle the exterior masonry that makes a home or building look finished.

Stucco & EIFS

New stucco exteriors, re-coats, and synthetic (EIFS) systems applied for a clean, durable finish on homes and commercial buildings.

Stucco Matching & Repair

Patch a crack, fix water damage, or blend an addition into your existing wall — matched so closely you won't see the seam.

Stone & Rock Veneer

Natural and manufactured stone veneer on entries, columns, wainscot, and full facades that lift a home's curb appeal.

Brick Masonry

Brick facades, accents, and repairs laid with tight, consistent joints — the classic look that holds up for decades.

Block & Retaining Walls

Concrete block walls and structural retaining walls built level and true — for grade changes, foundations, and yards.

Residential & Commercial

From a single-family remodel to a multi-story commercial exterior, we scope, prep, and finish the job the right way.

Common questions

Good to know

Can you match my existing stucco?

Yes — matching texture and color so a repair or addition blends into your existing wall is one of the things we're known for. In a lot of our finished jobs you can't tell the new stucco from the old.

Do you do both stucco and stone?

We do. Smylie D's handles stucco and EIFS, stone and rock veneer, brick, and concrete block — so we can finish a whole exterior, not just one part of it.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We take on both residential and commercial work, including multi-story stucco exteriors. Give us a call with your project and we'll scope it out.
