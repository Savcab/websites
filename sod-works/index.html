The Farm

A sod field that's part of a third-generation Montana farm

Sod Works makes up a portion of our third-generation, diversified farming operation in Broadwater County. We've spent decades learning what grows here, and we bring that same standard to every pallet of sod we cut.

Our fields sit on the south end of Canyon Ferry Lake — an ideal spot for serving the sod needs of Helena, Townsend, Butte, Bozeman and all the towns in between. A lawn is a long-term investment, so we grow a product that, with proper care, stays beautiful for years.

Visitors to the farm are always welcome. Give us a call and we'll talk through exactly what your project needs.

Our Sod

Grown for Montana, ready when you are

One product, done right — premium Kentucky bluegrass sod, plus the cutting and delivery that gets it onto your ground in great shape.

Kentucky bluegrass sod

A unique blend of five bluegrass varieties — chosen to be both visually striking and hardy enough to handle Montana's harsh winters and hot, dry summers.

Cut & delivered to you

Our fleet of three trucks, each fitted with a forklift, brings fresh-cut sod to your Montana location — offloaded where you need it, on the schedule you set.

Fresh-cut on order

With industry-leading harvesting equipment, we cut sod to fill your order — so what arrives is freshly harvested, not sitting on a lot losing its color.

For new lawns & renovations

Whether you're finishing a new build or replacing a tired lawn, fresh sod gives you a complete, mature look the day it's laid — no waiting on seed.

Straight answers on quantity

Homeowners & contractors
