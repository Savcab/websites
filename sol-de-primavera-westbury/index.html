What we do

Full-service care for your property

One local team for the whole list — for homes and commercial properties across Nassau County.

Lawn care & mowing

Clean, consistent mowing, edging, and trimming that keeps your lawn sharp all season.

Maintenance & cleanups

Spring and fall property cleanups, bed maintenance, and fresh mulch to reset the whole yard.

Fertilization & weed control

Seasonal feeding and weed control programs that build a thicker, greener, healthier lawn.

Aeration & overseeding

Core aeration and overseeding to relieve compaction and fill in thin, worn-out turf.

Tree & shrub trimming

Careful pruning and shaping that keeps trees and shrubs healthy and your beds tidy.

Snow & ice removal

Winter plowing, shoveling, and ice control for homes and commercial lots when the snow arrives.
