Good to know

Common questions.

Will pressure washing damage my siding or roof?

Not the way we do it. Delicate surfaces like vinyl siding, stucco and roof shingles get a low-pressure “soft wash” that cleans with the solution, not brute force. We save the higher pressure for durable surfaces like concrete and pavers — matching the method to the material is the whole point.

Do you do both the washing and the paver work?

Yes — that’s what sets us apart. We’re a full exterior-washing service and a paver/hardscape contractor. You can have us clean and seal an existing patio, or design and build a new one, and it’s the same crew either way.

What does paver sealing do?

After we deep-clean pavers, sealing locks in the color, helps lock the joint sand, and makes the surface easier to keep clean and more resistant to stains and weather. It’s a smart add-on whenever we restore a paver patio, walkway or pool deck.
