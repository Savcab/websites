01 — What we do

One crew for the whole yard

Residential and community properties across Montgomery County — from the first design to the mowing that keeps it looking finished. Everything below is work we do ourselves.

Landscape design & installation

New beds, sod, trees and full-yard makeovers, designed around how you actually use your space and built to last through Texas summers.

Patios, fire pits & hardscape

Paver patios, flagstone, fire pits and stacked-stone retaining walls that turn a plain backyard into a place you want to spend the evening.

Sod, mulch & flower beds

Fresh sod, crisp bed lines, mulch and seasonal color that lift your curb appeal the day the crew pulls away.

Lawn care & maintenance

Regular mowing, edging and cleanups that keep every property looking sharp between visits — one house or several.

Irrigation & sprinkler repair

Sprinkler troubleshooting and repairs so the sod and beds we install stay healthy and green year-round.

Fencing & landscape lighting

Cedar privacy fencing and low-voltage path and accent lighting to finish the yard and stretch your evenings outdoors.
