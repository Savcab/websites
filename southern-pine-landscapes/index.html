Full-property care, from the first design to every visit after.

Good to know

Questions we hear often.

Do you offer one-time projects or only recurring service?
Both. We take on one-time design and installation projects — beds, walkways, fencing, grading — and we also keep properties on a regular maintenance rotation. Many clients start with a project and stay on for the upkeep.
How does scheduled maintenance work?
We set a rotation that suits your property and the season, and we track when you're due — so you're not the one remembering to call. As our reviews mention, we'll also let you know when it's time to refresh beds, mulch or seasonal color.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Ailey and work throughout Montgomery County and the nearby Vidalia–Lyons area, including Mount Vernon, Soperton and Uvalda. If you're close to that and unsure, give us a call and we'll be straight with you about whether we can help.
How do I get a quote?
Call (912) 278-2356 or fill out the short form below with your property details. We'll set up a time to walk the property and give you an honest recommendation and estimate — no pressure.
Will the same crew that designs my yard maintain it?
Yes — that's a big part of what makes Southern Pine different. The people who plan and install your landscape are the ones who care for it afterward, so nothing gets lost in a hand-off and the design is built to be maintained.
