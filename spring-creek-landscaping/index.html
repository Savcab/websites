Trees done right

Big trees, set to live — not just dropped in a hole.

Mature evergreens are the fastest way to turn a bare Heber lot into a finished property. They're also the easiest to get wrong.

We bring in balled-and-burlapped spruce and conifers by the truckload, place them with a skid-steer so the root ball stays intact, and set them at the right depth and spacing for a privacy screen that fills in. One recent Heber backyard got five 10-foot blue spruce — "top-shelf from start to finish," in the owner's words.

  • Privacy screens & windbreaks that actually fill in
  • Equipment to move and place big root balls safely
  • Open to working with trees and material you've sourced

Good to know

Questions Heber homeowners ask

Do you just do maintenance, or full installs?

We're a design-and-install crew. We handle the whole transformation — design, trees, boulders, flagstone, beds, sod and grading — rather than weekly mow-and-go service.

What does a consultation cost?

Nothing. We come out, walk your yard and talk through what's possible at no charge. From there we put together a plan and a clear quote.

Can you plant large, mature trees?

Yes. We regularly bring in and set big balled-and-burlapped spruce and conifers — including 10-foot privacy rows — with the equipment to place them without damaging the root ball.

Will you work with materials I buy myself?

We can. We've worked with homeowners who sourced their own trees, boulders and edging to help manage cost, and built the project around them.

Do you take on new-construction yards?

Often. A lot of our work is finishing new builds — taking a bare graded lot to a complete yard with trees, stone, beds and lawn.

How do I get started?
