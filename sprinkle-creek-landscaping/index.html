About us

An Asheville landscaping company since 2007.

Sprinkle Creek Landscaping has worked out of Asheville since 2007, building landscapes across Buncombe County and the surrounding mountains. Our focus is design-build: we plan the project, then our own crew installs it.

Because so many local properties sit on a grade, a lot of what we do is structural — retaining walls, drainage and slope stabilization — before the plantings and lawn ever go in. It's the part that isn't glamorous, and it's the part that makes the rest last.

If you're planning a project or just trying to solve a stubborn slope, we're glad to come look and give you an honest estimate.
