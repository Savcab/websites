Our Story

Family-Owned Since 1989

S & S Complete Landscaping is a family-owned and operated business, started in 1989 and now spanning two generations of the same family.

We’re fully licensed with the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation, the Suffolk County Department of Consumer Affairs, and the Town of Southampton.

We’re also a proud member of the Council of Dedicated Merchants, serving Miller Place, Mount Sinai, Rocky Point, and Sound Beach.
