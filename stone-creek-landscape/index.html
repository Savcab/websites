Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide free estimates?
Yes. We provide accurate, realistic and reasonable estimates. Because no two projects are the same, we don’t do one-price-fits-all — we’ll visit your property and quote the actual scope of your project.
What kinds of projects do you take on?
High-end design-build landscaping: custom waterfalls and water features, backyard putting greens, paver patios and walkways, retaining walls, full landscape design and installation, planting, bed clean-up and mulching. Big or small, we’ll work with you.
Can a water feature run year-round in Nebraska?
Our water features are built to last and can often be enjoyed year’round. We’ll walk you through how your specific feature should be run and cared for through the seasons.
Do your retaining walls help with drainage?
Absolutely. Our retaining walls don’t just look good — they solve problems related to drainage, slumping yards and erosion, and we focus on products that integrate into the overall landscape.
How do I get started?
Call us at (308) 520-0277 or send the quick form below with a few details about your project. We’ll set up a time to see the space and talk through what’s possible.
