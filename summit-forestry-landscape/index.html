The mountain-Colorado difference

Defensible space, by a crew that knows this forest

In the Estes Park high country, wildfire is part of owning land in the trees. Creating defensible space — thinning timber, removing ladder fuels, and clearing the zone around your home — is some of the most valuable work you can do for a forest property.

  • Defensible-space clearing. Opening up the area around structures so the home has room to breathe.
  • Thinning & ladder-fuel removal. Reducing dense growth and low limbs that let fire climb into the canopy.
  • Brush & slash clean-up. Hauling and clearing what comes down so the lot is left tidy, not littered.
  • Twenty-plus years in these woods. Local know-how about how Estes Park properties actually sit and burn.
