01 — Our specialty

What is lawn painting?

Lawn painting — also called turf colorant — is a turf-safe green pigment sprayed onto your grass to restore a deep, even color. It's the same idea used to keep golf courses and sports fields looking their best, and it's one of the few services almost no other crew around Sebastian offers.

It's non-toxic once dry, looks natural right up close, and doesn't need weeks of watering and mowing to "grow in." When your lawn just needs to look alive again, colorant does it in a single visit.

  • Dormant or drought-stressed turf that's gone tan
  • Yards under watering limits or seasonal restrictions
  • Thin, patchy, or pet-worn spots that never green up
  • Curb appeal for a home sale, open house, or event

Not sure if colorant is right for your yard? We'll take a look and tell you straight — sometimes a mow and a clean-up is all it needs.

How a lawn painting visit works

Straightforward, and usually done in one appointment.

1

Walk the yard

We look at color, coverage, and any spots that need extra attention before we start.

2

Mix to match

Turf-safe colorant is blended to a natural green that suits your grass, not a fake lime tone.

3

Even application

Sprayed in light, overlapping passes for a consistent, streak-free finish edge to edge.

4

Dry & done

It dries to a natural-looking green in a few hours — no mowing needed to make it show.
