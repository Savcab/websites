The approach

Beautiful gardens that give more back than they take.

We design and build landscapes rooted in California-native and drought-tolerant plants, drawing on permaculture and water-wise principles. The result is a garden that reads as lush and considered — yet asks for a fraction of the water, and quietly supports the pollinators and soil life around it.

Every project starts with your site and how you actually want to live in it, then works outward to a planting palette suited to the Santa Barbara coast's light, wind and dry summers.

About the studio

A licensed design practice on the Carpinteria coast.

Sweet Smiling Landscapes is a Carpinteria-based landscape design studio serving the Santa Barbara and Ventura coast. We specialize in low-water, California-native and edible gardens, guided by permaculture and regenerative design — landscapes meant to look better and work harder with every passing season.

We're a licensed California landscape contractor (C-27 #1064728), and we work with homeowners across the region from first sketch through planting and beyond.
