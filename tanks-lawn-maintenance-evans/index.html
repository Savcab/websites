What we do

Six services, done right.

Everything below is work we actually do in Columbia County yards — no filler, no upsells you didn't ask for.

Mowing, Edging & Trimming

Sharp blades and dry grass for a clean cut, plus a crisp edge along drives, walks, and beds so the whole yard reads sharp, not just the grass.

Bush & Shrub Trimming

Deadwood removed, overgrowth thinned for better airflow, and shape restored so your beds look intentional again.

Ornamental Tree Trimming

Careful, timing-aware pruning that keeps ornamental trees healthy, shapely, and sized right for the space.

Pine Straw & Mulch

Fresh pine straw or mulch for weed control, moisture retention, and beds that hold their color through a Georgia summer.

Leaf Cleanup

Leaves cleared before they smother the lawn or stain your walkways and decks — a tidy yard, not a buried one.

Spring & Fall Cleanup

Beds cleared, shrubs shaped, and mulch refreshed each spring; leaves and overgrowth cleared each fall — the resets that keep a yard ahead of the season.
