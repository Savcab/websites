How we work

Design first. Then we build it.

A clear path from an idea to a finished outdoor space.

See the design

We walk the property, talk through what you're picturing, and put it into a 3D concept — so you see the pool, the patio and the lighting before anything is built.

Plan & estimate

A clear written plan and estimate — layout, materials and price — coordinated with your builder, architect or pool company when a project calls for it.

Build it right

Grading, drainage, hardscape and lighting brought together in the sequence the design calls for — the base done right so the finished space holds up.

Hand it back

We finish the details, clean the site down, and hand you a space that matches the design we showed you at the start.
