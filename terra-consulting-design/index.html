What we design & build

Services

Landscape Design & Build

A complete design carried through to installation — grading, plantings, palms, garden beds and fresh sod, drawn and built as one plan.

Hardscape & Pavers

Paver driveways, patios, walkways and pool decks — set for the long Florida seasons and finished to sit right with the home.

Irrigation Systems

Efficient irrigation planned around the planting — even coverage that keeps a new landscape thriving through a hot, wet Central Florida summer.

Landscape Lighting

Low-voltage lighting that draws out the architecture and specimen trees after dark — uplights, path lights and accents, placed with intent.

Stump Grinding

Clearing old stumps to a clean grade so a new design can start from a blank canvas — no leftover roots fighting the plan.

About the studio

A design-build studio for Central Florida.

Terra Consulting & Design is an Orlando landscape design & build studio. We work with homeowners across the metro to design a landscape and then install it — the plantings, the pavers, the irrigation and the lighting handled as one coherent project rather than a series of separate trades.

Because we don't do ongoing property maintenance, our full attention is on the design and the build: getting the composition, the grade and the details right the first time.

  • Design & installation only — one team, one plan, start to finish.
  • Serving downtown Orlando, College Park, Winter Park, Windermere, Lake Nona, Lake Mary & Sanford.
  • Every project begins with a free on-site consultation.
