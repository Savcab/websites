What we do

Full-service lawn & property care

No task is too big or small. We put in the time to keep your lawn healthy, tidy and beautifully maintained — start to finish.

Mowing & maintenance

Our commercial mowers are kept sharp and cut your grass at the right height for its type and season — so the lawn stays protected, green and healthy every visit.

Trimming & edging

Detail on every inch of the property. All the hard-to-reach spots, beds and sidewalks get trimmed and edged for the crisp, professional look you want.

Hedge, shrub & tree work

Hedge trimming, shrub pruning, and tree pruning & trimming to round out a full-service maintenance plan and keep the whole property looking its best.

Clean-ups & leaf removal

Spring clean-up, fall leaves, or just tidying up — we rake, bag and haul off leaves and debris when added to any regular lawn-care service.

Fertilization

Once we identify your grass species and soil, we match the right fertilizer combination so your lawn stays healthy and green through the Texas seasons.

Commercial & large acreage

Frequently asked questions

Have a question that isn't here? Call or text us at (254) 371-6729 — we're happy to help.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Kempner and serve homeowners and businesses across Lampasas County and the surrounding Central Texas area, including Lampasas and Copperas Cove. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

Do you handle both residential and commercial properties?

Yes — we care for residential lawns, commercial properties, and large-acreage sites. We have the commercial-grade equipment and crew to keep larger properties on a regular schedule.

What's included in your lawn-care service?

Mowing at the right height for your grass type and season, plus trimming and edging on every visit. We also offer hedge, shrub and tree trimming, seasonal clean-ups and leaf removal, and fertilization matched to your soil and grass.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (254) 371-6729, or send us a request through the form below. We'll look at your property, recommend what's best for it, and give you a fair, upfront price.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different, so we quote based on size and the services you need. Our pricing is fair and transparent — customers regularly tell us our quotes come in lower than the competition without cutting corners.

Do you offer leaf removal and clean-ups?

We do. We'll rake, bag and dispose of leaves and debris — for spring clean-ups, fall leaves, or a general tidy-up — when added to a regular lawn-care service.
