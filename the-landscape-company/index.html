What We Build

Complete outdoor living, from one collaborative team.

We don't just maintain yards — we design and build them. Every project is drawn around how you actually want to live outside, then installed by the same crew that planned it.

Outdoor Living Spaces

Patios, seating areas and full backyard retreats designed for relaxing and entertaining — the heart of every project we take on.

Water Features

Custom ponds, waterfalls and courtyard fountains that bring movement and sound to your landscape — a signature of our design work.

Xeriscapes

Boulder placement, native grasses and water-wise planting designed for Colorado's climate — beautiful year-round with far less watering.

Night Lighting

Low-voltage landscape lighting that highlights your trees, paths and architecture — so your outdoor space comes alive after sunset.

Pergolas, Patios & Walkways

Cedar pergolas, flagstone walkways and paver patios built to last — the hardscape structure that gives a yard its shape and shade.

Outdoor Kitchens

Built-in grills, counters and dining areas under the stars — designed so the whole gathering happens out back, not just the cooking.

Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle both the design and the installation?

Yes — we're a design-build landscaper. We create the plan with you and then install it with our own crew, so the finished space matches the design and nothing is lost handing off to a separate contractor.

What does the process look like to get started?

It starts with a free consultation at your property. We talk through how you want to use the space and your budget, then collaborate on a tailored design. Once you're happy with the plan, we schedule the build and our crew handles it start to finish.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Complete outdoor living spaces: patios and walkways, cedar pergolas, water features, xeriscapes and native planting, low-voltage night lighting, and outdoor kitchens. We work on both residential and commercial properties.

Do you design xeriscapes suited to Colorado's climate?

Absolutely. Water-wise xeriscaping — boulder placement, native grasses and drought-tolerant planting — is one of our specialties, designed to look great year-round on the Front Range while cutting down on watering.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Longmont and serve the surrounding Boulder County and north-Denver communities, including Erie, Frederick and Broomfield. If you're nearby and not sure, give us a call at (303) 520-1738.
