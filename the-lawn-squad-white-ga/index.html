Our Story

A family crew that treats your yard like our own.

Established in Cherokee County and founded by Adam and Windy Mitchell in January 2005, The Lawn Squad is a family-owned and operated business with an owner-operator on-site at every project. Two decades on, that hands-on standard hasn't changed.

We're a genuine design-build crew — known best for premium Zoysia sod, backyard transformations and hardscapes done right the first time. That's what "Experience the Quality" means to us.
