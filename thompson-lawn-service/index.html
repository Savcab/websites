What we do

Complete lawn care, handled start to finish

Whether you're around to watch or managing the property from a distance, Thompson Lawn Service keeps it clean, even and cared-for — every visit.

Lawn Mowing

Clean, even cuts on a schedule that fits your property — front, back and everything in between, kept consistently sharp.

Edging & Trimming

Crisp lines along walks, drives and beds, plus string trimming around trees, fences and tight corners the mower can't reach.

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Recurring Maintenance Plans

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with out-of-town or absentee property owners?
Yes — it's one of the things we do best. Plenty of our customers live in another town or state and own property in Starkville. We handle everything by phone and send you photos when the job is done, so you always know your lawn is taken care of.
How do I know the work actually got done?
We send pictures when we finish a visit. You'll be able to see exactly how the property looks without having to drive by and check.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Starkville and serve the city plus nearby communities in Oktibbeha County and the surrounding area. If you're just outside that, call us and we'll let you know if we can help.
How do I get a price?
Call us at (662) 722-8370 or send a quote request with your address and what you need. We'll look it over and give you a clear, fair price up front — no surprises.
Do you offer recurring service?
Yes. Most customers set up regular weekly or bi-weekly visits so their lawn stays consistent and they never have to think about it. We'll work out a schedule that fits your property.
What are your hours?
We're working Monday through Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday 6:00 AM to 2:30 PM. Calls and quote requests are welcome anytime — we'll get right back to you.
