What we do

Four things done well, on hill-country ground.

Thornsley takes on the work that West Virginia yards actually need — water that won’t sit, walls that hold a slope, beds that look designed, and trees handled safely.

01

Drainage & grading

French drains, regrading, and downspout runoff solved so water moves away from the house instead of pooling in the yard.

02

Landscape design & beds

Full yard makeovers — planting beds, boulders, river rock, and new lawn laid out to fit the house and the slope it sits on.

03

Patios & retaining walls

Concrete patios, decks, and stacked-stone retaining walls that turn an unusable hillside into flat, finished space.

04

Tree work & removal

Takedowns, trimming, and storm cleanup handled by a crew that knows trees — clearing what has to go and protecting what stays.
