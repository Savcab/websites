Questions we hear a lot

Do you clean both the inside and outside of windows?

Yes. We clean interior and exterior glass and wipe down frames, tracks, and sills so the whole window looks finished — not just the side facing the street.

My windows haven't been cleaned in years. Is that a problem?

Not at all — those are some of our favorite jobs. We've brought neglected glass back to clear on homes that hadn't seen a squeegee in two decades. The tougher the buildup, the more satisfying the result.

Do you also clean solar panels?

We do — for both homes and businesses. Dust and grime build up on panels and quietly cut their output, so a clean array helps them work the way they're meant to.

Do you work with businesses and commercial properties?

Absolutely. We clean storefronts, offices, retail, and commercial buildings across the Magic Valley — and many of those clients have us back on a regular schedule.
