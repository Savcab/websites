What we do

Irrigation first — and everything that keeps a landscape looking right

Sprinklers are where Tilley started, and it's still the core of what we do. Over the decades that grew into full-property landscape design, hardscape, and lighting for Grand Island homes and grounds.

01

Irrigation & Sprinkler Systems

New system design and install, repairs and head adjustments, plus seasonal start-ups and winterizing blow-outs.

02

Landscape Design & Install

Beds, grading, sod, trees, and shrubs planned for Nebraska's seasons and built to grow in evenly.

03

Paver Patios & Walkways

Interlocking paver patios, walkways, and pool surrounds set on a solid base to stay flat through the freeze-thaw.

04

Retaining Walls & Fire Features

Seat walls, retaining walls, fire pits, and stone outdoor kitchens that make a backyard usable and warm.

05

Landscape Lighting

Low-voltage lighting that shows off the house, the trees, and the walkways after the sun goes down.

06

Commercial & HOA Grounds

Irrigation and landscape upkeep for associations, townhome communities, and businesses across the area.
