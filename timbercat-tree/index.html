Good to know

Tree-removal questions, answered

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Call or text and we'll set up a time to look at the tree in person, talk through the options, and give you a clear, no-obligation price before any work starts.

Can you take down a tree close to my house?

That's exactly the kind of work we specialize in. On tight lots and around structures or power lines, we rig and lower the tree in controlled sections rather than dropping it, so your home and landscaping stay protected.

Do you grind the stump too?

We can. Stump grinding takes the leftover stump down below ground level so you can reclaim the space and avoid regrowth or a tripping hazard. Just let us know when you call and we'll include it in the quote.

What about the mess afterward?

Cleanup is part of the job. We haul off the wood and debris and rake the ground before we leave, so you're not stuck dealing with a pile of brush. If you'd like to keep the firewood, just say so.

Can you help after a storm?

Yes — downed trees and hanging limbs after a coastal storm are some of the most common calls we get. Reach out and we'll respond as quickly as we can to make the site safe and clear the damage.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Newport and serve the central Oregon Coast and Lincoln County — including Toledo, Waldport, Depoe Bay, South Beach, Seal Rock, and the surrounding area.
