A yard reads like the rock it's set on. We place stone so the rest falls into line.

Tino Landscapes is a Nebo-based landscaping and rock masonry crew, proudly serving McDowell and Burke County since 2011 — decorative stone, retaining walls, dry creek beds and the maintenance in between. We treat every job as a design question first: how the rock meets the slope, where the water wants to go, how it will look once it settles in.

Then we build it the slow way: excavation, a compacted base, the right grade and drainage, and stone set by hand. It's the part no one photographs, and it's why the work is still flat and plumb a few winters on.

How we work

Design, build, and a site left clean.

A straightforward path from the first look at your yard to the day you start using it.

Walk the site

We come look at the yard, talk through what you're picturing, and read how the rock, the slope and the water want to work together.

Plan & estimate

You get a free estimate with the materials and layout laid out plainly — no vague numbers before we start.

Build on a base

We excavate, set the grade so water moves where it should, and place the rock by hand — the slow part, done right.

Leave it clean

We tidy the site as we go — the habit our reviews call out — and hand back a yard that's ready to use the same evening.
