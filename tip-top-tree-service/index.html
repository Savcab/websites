Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We come out, look at the tree and the access, and give you a clear price with no obligation. Call (209) 454-6525 or send the quote form and we’ll set a time.
Can you handle insurance-required tree removals?
We do — we’ve removed hazard trees where the homeowner’s insurance coverage depended on getting them down. We’re licensed, bonded and insured, which is usually what insurers want to see.
What is defensible space clearing?
It’s opening up the zone around your home so a wildfire has less to feed on — limbing trees up off the ground, thinning brush and removing ladder fuels. It matters a lot for foothill properties in Calaveras County.
Do you remove the debris and grind the stump?
Yes. We haul the debris away and leave the site tidy. Stump grinding is available as part of the job if you want the stump gone below grade.
What areas do you serve?
We’re based in San Andreas and work across Calaveras County — Angels Camp, Valley Springs, Mokelumne Hill, Murphys, Arnold, Copperopolis and the surrounding foothills. Not sure if you’re in range? Just call.
What about a tree down after a storm?
Give us a call as soon as you can. A limb on the house or a tree across the driveway needs to be cleared safely — we’ll get out to assess and handle it.
