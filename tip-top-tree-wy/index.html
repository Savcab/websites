Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Can you take down a tree that's right against my house or fence?

Yes — that's the work we're best known for. We rope and lower the tree out in sections instead of dropping it whole, so the structures and fencing around it stay put. The tighter and trickier, the more at home we are.

Do you haul away the branches and logs?

We do. Cleanup is part of the job — limbs get chipped, logs are cut and handled, and we rake the area before we leave. Customers regularly tell us the yard ends up cleaner than it started.

How fast can you give me a quote?

Often the same day you reach out. Give us a call or send the estimate form and we'll get back to you to set up a time to look at the tree and give you a price.

What does it cost?

Every tree is different, so we quote each job after we've seen it. What we can promise is a fair, upfront number — our reviews consistently describe the pricing as "very reasonable" and "a good price."

What areas do you cover?

Torrington and the surrounding Goshen County communities — Lingle, Fort Laramie, Yoder, La Grange, Huntley and nearby. We're also a short drive from the Nebraska panhandle. Not sure if you're in range? Just call.

When are you open?

7:00am to 7:00pm, Monday through Saturday. Those wide six-day hours mean we can usually work around your schedule — and get to trees that can't wait.
