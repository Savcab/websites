Good to Know

Common questions

Do you really take on the trees other companies won't?

Yes — that's where we've built our reputation. Large, leaning, dead or storm-damaged trees in tight spots are exactly the work we specialize in. If another crew has turned a removal down, give us a call.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the tree's size, condition and how close it is to your home, fences or power lines. We come out, look at it in person, and give you a clear, free quote before any work starts — no surprises.

Can you come out after a storm?

We're open seven days a week, 7 AM to 7 PM, and storm cleanup is a big part of what we do on the Peninsula. Call us and we'll let you know how soon we can get to your property.

Do you clean up and haul everything away?

Yes. Cleanup and haul-away are part of the job. We can also grind the stump below grade so you're left with open, usable ground — just let us know.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Ocean Park and work up and down the Long Beach Peninsula — Long Beach, Seaview, Ilwaco, Nahcotta, Oysterville, Chinook, Naselle and the surrounding Pacific County area.
