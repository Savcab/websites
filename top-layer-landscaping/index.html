Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No — estimates are free. We'll come out, look at your yard, talk through what you want, and give you a clear written price with no obligation and no pressure.

What size jobs do you take?

Anything from refreshing a single bed to designing and installing a whole front yard. If you're not sure whether your project is a fit, just call and ask — we'll tell you honestly.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Mayer and work across the surrounding west metro — Waconia, Watertown, New Germany, Norwood Young America, Cologne and nearby Carver County towns. Outside that radius? Give us a call and we'll let you know.

When's the best time to plant or sod?

Spring and fall are ideal for most planting and sod in Minnesota, but beds, edging, and stone work can happen through the growing season. We'll recommend the right timing for your specific project.

Do you clean up when you're done?

Always. Hauling debris, sweeping, and leaving the site clean is part of the job — not an extra. You'll come home to a finished yard, not a mess.
