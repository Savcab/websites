Good to know

Common questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come out to your property, look at the actual site and grade, and give you a clear written estimate at no cost and with no obligation. The best way to start is a quick call to (319) 800-6857 or the quote form on this page.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Wellman and work across the greater Iowa City area, including Kalona, Washington, Coralville, North Liberty, Tiffin, Riverside and West Branch. If you're nearby and not sure whether you're in range, just ask — we'll tell you straight.

Can you fix a yard that holds water or floods?

That's one of the things we do most. Standing water, soggy low spots and runoff against the foundation are usually a grading and drainage problem. We install French drains, dry creek beds and downspout lines, and regrade so water flows where it should — away from your house.

What kind of retaining walls do you build?

Both engineered segmental block walls and natural-stone walls, sized to your slope. Every wall gets proper base prep and drainage behind it, because that's what keeps a wall straight and standing instead of bowing out a few years later.

Do you handle the whole project, or just part of it?

The whole thing. The same crew handles excavation, base prep, the hardscape install, any planting or rock work, and the clean-up — so there's one team accountable for the finished result, start to finish.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the project, but the fastest way to get a real timeline is to reach out. Give us a call and we'll let you know where the schedule stands and book your on-site visit.
