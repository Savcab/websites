What we do

Full-service tree care, from a single limb to a whole lot

Whether you want a tree preserved, taken down, or a property cleared, every job starts the same way — a careful look at the tree, the target, and the safest way to get it done.

Save the tree

Reduction & preservation

Crown reduction and selective pruning that bring an overgrown or risky tree down to a safe size while keeping it alive — the right call for a mature tree you'd rather not lose.

Tree removal

Safe, controlled takedowns of large, dead, leaning, or storm-damaged trees in tight residential spaces — rigged down section by section to protect what's below.

Trimming & pruning

Structural and clearance pruning to lift canopies off roofs and wires, improve form, and take the weight off limbs before they fail.

Lot & storm clearing

Clearing multiple trees for a building site, view, or community project — including big multi-tree jobs and downed trees after a storm.

Stump grinding

Grinding stumps below grade so you reclaim the space for lawn, garden, or hardscape — no trip hazard, no eyesore left behind.

Good to know

Common questions about tree work

Can my tree be saved instead of removed?

Often, yes. A large or overgrown tree can frequently be reduced to a safe size and pruned to protect its health rather than taken down. We'll assess it on-site and tell you honestly which approach makes sense.

Do you handle large or multi-tree jobs?

Yes. We do everything from a single specimen tree to clearing nine or more large trees for a lot, a view, or a community project — with the equipment to match the scale.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the tree and the access, and give you a clear price before any work starts. There's no charge for the estimate.

Are you insured?

Yes — Trees Plus is fully insured. Tree work carries real risk, so it matters that the company on your property is properly covered.

Will you clean up and grind the stump?

We chip and haul the debris and clean up the site as part of the job. Stump grinding is available too, so you can reclaim the space for lawn or garden.

Do you respond after storms?

Storm-damaged and downed trees are part of what we do. Call us with the situation and we'll get you assessed and scheduled as quickly as we can.
