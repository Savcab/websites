Why desert landscaping

Every drop counts when you're landscaping the desert

Out here, a leaking or mistimed irrigation system wastes water fast — and a yard that's overgrown or unchecked costs you just as much down the line. We keep both dialed in, so you're not paying for water or upkeep you don't need.A leaking irrigation system wastes water fast. We keep it dialed in so you're not paying for water you don't need.

Water where it's needed

A well-tuned drip system feeds your desert plants exactly what they need — no runoff, no wasted spray on gravel or hardscape.

Fewer leaks, lower bills

A cracked line or clogged emitter can waste water for weeks unnoticed. We find it fast and fix it right.

Healthy plants, less overgrowth

Regular trimming and cleanup keeps your desert trees and shrubs healthy and tidy without letting them get away from you.
