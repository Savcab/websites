Good to know

Pressure washing questions, answered

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water and is great for hard surfaces like concrete driveways and walkways. Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution to safely remove algae and mildew from delicate surfaces like siding, stucco and roofs. We use whichever your surface actually needs — that's the part that protects your home.

Will pressure washing damage my siding or roof?

Not when it's done correctly. We never blast siding or shingles with high pressure — those get a gentle soft wash. Matching the right method and detergent to each surface is exactly why people who've had bad experiences elsewhere call us back.

Do you work with businesses and commercial properties?

Yes. Triple J handles residential, commercial and industrial work — storefront sidewalks, building exteriors, dumpster pads, property-management accounts, plus fleet washing and mobile detailing. We can schedule around your hours so cleaning never interrupts business.

Are you insured?

We're fully insured on every job, residential and commercial. It gives you peace of mind that the work — and your property — are protected from start to finish.
