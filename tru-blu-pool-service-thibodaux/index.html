What We Do

Full-Service Pool Care & Repair

From a weekly chemical visit to a full green-pool rescue, Tru Blu keeps Bayou Country pools clear, balanced and ready to swim — and handles the equipment when something breaks.

Weekly Maintenance

Water tested and balanced, surface skimmed, walls brushed, baskets emptied, equipment checked — basic chemical or full-service routes available.

Green Pool Rescue

Turned green over a Louisiana summer? We clear the algae, rebalance the chemistry and bring a neglected pool back to swimmable.

Equipment Repair

Pumps, motors, filters, heaters & heat pumps, salt systems and automatic cleaners — diagnosed and repaired or replaced.

Filter & Salt Cell Service

DE and cartridge filter cleaning, sand changes and salt cell cleanings to keep your system flowing and your water clear.

Acid Wash & Tile Cleaning

Drain, acid wash and tile-line cleaning to strip stains and scale and restore the look of an aging pool surface.

Start-Up & Draining

Pool start-ups, drains and seasonal resets handled cleanly — so a new or reopened pool is balanced from day one.

24-hour cancellation notice required. Lock-out or no-show fees may apply.

Why We Chose The Name

True Blue.

True Blue (adj.) — unwavering in one’s commitment; extremely loyal.

That’s the standard Katie Brupbacher built the company on. Katie came from a background in sales and residential and commercial cleaning, then teamed up with her dad and brothers — contractors with over 25 years between them — to start Tru Blu right here in Thibodaux.

Homeowners have plenty of options when they let a company work around their home and family. So we keep it personal: only one of us services your pool, and we don’t take on more customers than we can handle. There’s never a question of who’s in your backyard.
