What we build

Hardscape & masonry services

Everything we do is outdoor stone and brick — built on a base that holds up to Maine freeze-and-thaw. Here's where most of our work falls.

Stone & flagstone patios

Irregular flagstone and fieldstone patios with mortared or sand-set joints — a natural, settled-in look that suits a Maine yard.

Paver patios, walkways & driveways

Interlocking concrete pavers laid over a compacted base — patios, front walks, garden paths and driveway aprons designed to drain and stay flat.

Retaining & seat walls

Segmental block and natural-stone walls that hold a slope, level a yard or wrap a patio in built-in seating — engineered to take the load.

Masonry & chimney repair

Cracked and broken steps rebuilt, chimneys repointed and rebuilt, and tired stonework brought back — repaired properly, not just skim-coated.

Drainage solutions

French drains, channel drains and proper grading worked into the hardscape so runoff moves away from your foundation and your new stone.

Fire pits & fire features

Stone fire pits and patio fire features that give a backyard — or a lakeside spot — a place to gather once the evenings turn cool.

Good to know

Common questions

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. We come out, look at the site and give you a written estimate at no cost. You only pay once you've approved the plan and price.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Outdoor stone and masonry: paver and flagstone patios, walkways and driveways, retaining and seat walls, fire pits, drainage, plus masonry repair like cracked steps and chimneys.

Why does the base matter so much?

In Maine, freeze-and-thaw moves anything that isn't built right. A compacted base and proper drainage are what keep a patio flat and a wall plumb for the long run — so that's where we don't cut corners.

Can you repair existing stonework?

Yes. Alongside new builds we rebuild cracked or broken masonry steps, repoint and rebuild chimneys, and fix walls that have started to fail.

What areas do you serve?

China and the surrounding central-Maine towns — Vassalboro, Winslow, Waterville, Augusta, Palermo, Albion, Windsor and nearby. Call if you're not sure you're in range.

When's the best time to book?

Hardscape season in Maine fills up fast. Reach out early in spring or summer to get on the schedule for the season — fall projects book up too.
