What we do

Tree work for north-woods yards & lake lots

From a single hazard tree hanging over the roof to clearing a whole shoreline lot — Cole brings the equipment to handle simple to complex jobs, then hauls every branch away.

Tree Removal

Safe, controlled drops — including tight spots near houses, sheds and fences. Sectioned down piece by piece when there's no room to fell it whole.

Trimming & Pruning

Shaping, deadwood and limb clearance to keep trees healthy and off your roof, lines and driveway. Clean cuts that don't leave a mess behind.

Stump Grinding

Grind the stump below grade so you can reclaim the lawn — no tripping hazard, no eyesore, no regrowth. Grindings raked out or hauled, your call.

Lot & Brush Clearing

Open up an overgrown lot, fence line or shoreline. Trees and brush removed and cleared so the space is usable again — right down to fresh sod if you want it.

Storm & Hazard Trees

Leaning, split or storm-damaged trees taken down before they come down on their own. Give Cole a call and he'll get out to look at it.

Cleanup & Haul-Away

Every job ends with the site cleaned up — branches, rounds and debris hauled off so your property looks better than when we pulled in.

Good to know

Questions before you call

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Cole comes out, looks at the job in person, and gives you a straight price with no pressure. Call or text a photo to get started.

Do you clean up and haul everything away?

Always. Cleanup is the part customers mention most in their reviews — branches, rounds and debris are hauled off and the site is left tidy before we leave.

Can you handle big or tricky trees near my house?

Yes. With a bucket lift and the right equipment, trees in tight spots come down in controlled sections instead of being felled whole — simple to complex jobs, no matter the size.

Do you grind stumps too?

Yes — we grind stumps below grade so you can reclaim the lawn. We can rake the grindings out or haul them off, whichever you'd prefer.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Dassel and cover the surrounding Meeker County area — Cokato, Litchfield, Darwin, Kingston, Watkins, Eden Valley, Howard Lake and nearby. Not sure if you're in range? Just call and ask.
