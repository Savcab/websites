What we do

One crew for the whole yard

Lawn care and landscaping under one roof — so the mowing, the beds and the bigger projects all stay on the same standard, season after season.

Lawn Maintenance

Reliable weekly and bi-weekly mowing, edging, trimming and blow-off that keeps your lawn crisp and even all season long.

Lawn Health & Turf

Fertilization, weed control, seeding and aeration to build a thicker, greener, more resilient lawn from the soil up.

Mulch & Planting Beds

Fresh mulch, clean bed edges and shrub, tree and seasonal plantings that frame your home and stay easy to maintain.

Stonework & Borders

Stacked-stone borders and low retaining walls that hold your beds, tame slopes and give the yard clean, lasting lines.

Landscape Installs

From bed layouts to grading and new plantings, we install the landscape you've been picturing — and build it to last.

Cleanups & Seasonal

Spring and fall cleanups, leaf removal, overgrowth clearing and one-time refreshes to get the property back in shape fast.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer regular lawn maintenance plans?

Yes. Most customers are on a weekly or bi-weekly mowing and upkeep schedule so the yard stays consistent. We'll set a rhythm that fits your property and budget.

Are estimates really free?

Always. We'll walk the property, talk through what you're after and give you a clear, no-pressure quote before any work begins.

Do you handle both lawn care and landscaping?

We do — that's the point of one crew. Mowing and turf health, mulch and planting beds, stonework borders and full landscape installs all come from the same team.

What areas do you serve?

Pickens and the surrounding foothills, including Easley, Liberty, Six Mile, Central, Dacusville and nearby parts of Pickens County. Not sure if you're in range? Give us a call.

How do I get on the schedule?

Call (864) 650-7802 or request a free estimate below. We're on the clock six days a week and will get back to you quickly.

Do you take on one-time projects?

Absolutely. Spring and fall cleanups, a bed refresh, fresh mulch or a new install — one-time jobs are welcome alongside our regular maintenance work.
