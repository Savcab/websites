Frequently asked questions

Do you offer weekly mowing, or just one-time visits?

Both — but reliable weekly maintenance is our bread and butter. Most homeowners put us on a set weekly schedule so the lawn stays consistently sharp; we also handle one-time clean-ups and projects.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Windsor and serve Timnath, Fort Collins, Loveland, Longmont, Greeley and Berthoud. If you're nearby but not listed, give us a call and we'll let you know.

Do you handle landscaping and sprinklers, not just mowing?

Yes. Along with lawn care we design and install landscaping, lay sod, build planting beds, and put in sprinkler/irrigation systems sized for Colorado conditions.

Can you plow snow in the winter?

We do. Our plow trucks and spreaders keep residential and commercial drives, lots and walkways clear and safe through the Front Range winter.
