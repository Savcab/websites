Good to know

Questions, answered.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in La Verkin and serve the surrounding Zion-corridor towns — Hurricane, Toquerville, Virgin, Rockville and Springdale. If you're nearby and not sure, just call and ask.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free and there's no pressure. Tell us what your yard needs, we'll take a look and give you an honest price before any work begins.

Can you take care of my yard on a regular schedule?

Absolutely. A lot of our clients keep us on a regular mowing and maintenance schedule so their yard stays sharp year-round — but we're happy to do one-time projects and cleanups too.

Do you do desert / low-water landscaping?

Yes — xeriscape and rock landscaping is a big part of what we do in Southern Utah. We design low-water yards with gravel, boulders and native plants that look great and need far less watering.

How do I get started?

Easiest way is to call or text us at (435) 216-6441, or fill out the quick estimate form below. We'll get right back to you to set up a visit.
