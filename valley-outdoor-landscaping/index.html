01 Who We Are

Built here, by people who live here.

Valley Outdoor started in 2011 and has spent more than a decade turning Quad Cities yards into places families actually use — front-entry paver walks, backyard patios with fire and shade, retaining walls that hold a slope for good, and drainage that keeps water where it belongs.

We handle the whole job with our own crew and equipment, from the first grade to the last plant. No subcontractor handoffs, no disappearing act halfway through — just the same faces from the estimate to the walkthrough.

02 What We Do

A full outdoor crew — hardscape, drainage, and lawn.

Most Quad Cities projects touch more than one trade. We do them under one roof so the grade, the stone, the water, and the plants all work together instead of against each other.

01

Paver Patios & Walkways

Patios, front walks, steps, and driveways in interlocking pavers and natural stone — laid on a proper base so they stay flat and true through Iowa freeze-and-thaw.

02

Retaining & Landscape Walls

Engineered block and boulder walls that hold a grade, carve out usable yard, and frame beds and patios — the kind of complex, ever-changing build our reviewers know us for.

03

Drainage & Grading

French drains, regrading, and downspout solutions that move water off the lawn and away from the foundation — the unglamorous work that protects everything else you build.

04

Outdoor Living

Pergolas, fire features, seat walls, and backyard putting greens — the details that turn a yard into a place you don't want to leave on a summer evening.

05

Lawn Care & Maintenance

Mowing, aeration, and season-long upkeep that keeps the stripes crisp — the ongoing care customers have trusted us with for years at a time.
