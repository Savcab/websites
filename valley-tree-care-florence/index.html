07 — Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. We come out, look at the tree, the access and what’s around it, and give you a clear price before any work starts. There’s no charge to get a quote.
Can you take down a tree right next to my house or fence?
That’s most of what we do. Tight-access trees get climbed and lowered in sections by rope rather than felled, so the limbs come down controlled — protecting your roof, fences and your neighbors’ property.
Do you grind the stump and clean up after?
We can grind the stump below grade so it’s not catching a mower or sprouting back. Either way, we haul off the debris and rake the site down before we leave — you get your yard back, not a mess.
What areas do you serve?
Florence and the surrounding Fremont County valley — Cañon City, Penrose, Williamsburg, Coal Creek, Rockvale and nearby. If you’re close and not sure, give us a call.
How do I get started?
Call or text (719) 964-8148, or fill out the estimate form below with a couple of details about the tree. We’ll get right back to you to set up a look.
