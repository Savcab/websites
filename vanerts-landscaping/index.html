Our Story

VanErt's, LLC has served Adrian, Tecumseh and the rest of Lenawee County as a commercial and residential landscaping, concrete and building contractor since 1997. What started as a one-man operation has grown into a full-service crew the community keeps calling back.

Led by Kevin VanErt, Jr., the team brings the same standard to every job — whether it's a stamped-concrete patio, a paver retaining wall, a new deck or a commercial snow contract. The goal never changes: do the work right, and make you look good.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Adrian and serve Tecumseh, Clinton, Blissfield, Onsted and communities throughout Lenawee County, Michigan, for both residential and commercial projects.

Do you handle landscaping, concrete and building?

Yes — that's what sets us apart. VanErt's does landscape design and install, paver hardscape, flatwork and stamped concrete, and building work like decks, garages and additions, all in-house.

How do I get an estimate?

Call us at (517) 265-5423 or fill out the free-estimate form on this page. We'll set up a time to visit your property, talk through your project and give you a clear, honest quote.

Do you take on commercial work?

Absolutely. Along with residential projects, we handle commercial concrete, site work and seasonal commercial snow removal for Lenawee County businesses.

Can you build a stamped or colored concrete patio?

Yes. We pour standard, colored and stamped concrete for patios, driveways and sidewalks, and also handle concrete and asphalt removal, stairs and handicap ramps.

How long has VanErt's been in business?

VanErt's has served Lenawee County as a landscaping, concrete and building contractor since 1997 — over 25 years.
