Good to Know

Common questions

Do you give written quotes before starting?

Yes. We come out, look at the tree and what's around it, and put the scope and price in writing before any work begins — so the job you agree to is the job we do.

Can you take down a tree close to my house or fence?

That's much of what we do. With climbers and a crane we remove trees in controlled sections over roofs, fences, and lines rather than felling them in one piece.

Do you clean up and haul away the debris?

Yes — cleanup is part of the job. We clear the brush and limbs and haul them off so your yard is left tidy. It's the thing our reviews mention most.

Do you grind the stump too?

We do. Stump grinding takes the stump down below grade so you can replant, level, or just reclaim the space. Ask for it when you request your quote.

What areas do you cover?

Blackfoot and the surrounding Bingham County towns — Shelley, Firth, Aberdeen, Moreland, Groveland and nearby. If you're close by, call and we'll let you know.

How do I get started?

Call or text (208) 244-9967, or send the quote request on this page with a photo of the tree. We'll set up a time to come look and give you a written quote.
