Our story

Thirty-six years of the same simple promise

Victor McIntyre started Vic's Lawn Maintenance in April of 1990. More than three decades later, it's still a hands-on, locally owned operation — the kind of place where the name on the truck is the same person who answers the phone.

That longevity is the whole pitch: in a valley where word travels, you don't last 36 years unless you show up, do good work, and treat people right. When a neighbor says “just call Vic,” this is who they mean.
