Our story

Established 1990

Vic's Lawn Maintenance has been working the yards and grounds of the Greenbrier Valley since April 1990 — that's more than 36 years of mowing, tree work and property care for the same communities, owned and run by Victor McIntyre.

Crews come and go around here. Vic's has been the steady name folks pass along to a neighbor — the kind of reliability you only earn one season at a time, for three decades running.
