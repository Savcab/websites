What we build

From bare grade to finished outdoor space

Villa's is a design-build shop — hardscape, irrigation, and planting handled by the same crew, so the details line up and the work lasts.

Paver Patios & Driveways

Interlocking paver patios, walkways, and driveways laid on a properly compacted base to hold up for decades.

Retaining Walls & Hardscape

Segmental block and natural-stone walls that hold grade on a slope and carve out level, usable yard.

Outdoor Living

Built-in grills, fire features, and stone kitchens that turn a back patio into a room you actually use.

Irrigation Systems

Design and install of sprinkler and drip systems that keep the whole property green without the guesswork.

Hydroseeding & Lawn Install

Fast, even lawn establishment on new grades and bare slopes — a thick stand of grass without sod pricing.

Landscape Design & Planting

Beds, trees, and shrubs planned for year-round color and low upkeep, matched to your soil and light.
