Good to know

Questions homeowners ask

A few of the things people want to know before booking a wash.

What's the difference between soft wash and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure and cleaning solution to safely lift mildew and grime from delicate surfaces like siding and roofs. Pressure washing uses higher water pressure for hard surfaces like concrete. We match the method to the surface — that's the part that protects your home.

Will washing damage my roof or siding?

Not when it's done right. We use the gentle, correct approach for each surface — and if something genuinely shouldn't be cleaned a certain way, we'll tell you up front rather than risk damage.

Do you handle the whole property in one visit?

Yes. House, roof, deck, gutters and driveway can be coordinated into a single visit. That whole-property, top-to-bottom clean is what we're known for around Lake Keowee.

How do I get a quote?

Call (864) 885-1601 or send the quick form on this page. We'll look over the property, flag anything that needs special care, and give you a clear, free quote — no pressure.

Which areas do you serve?

Seneca, West Union, Walhalla, Salem, Clemson and the Lake Keowee area across Oconee County. If you're close by and don't see your town, just ask when you call.

Do you clean pollen and pine needles too?

We do. Pine needles get blown off roofs and out of valleys, and heavy pollen comes off siding, decks, rails and even patio furniture as part of the wash — it's exactly the detail our customers mention most.
