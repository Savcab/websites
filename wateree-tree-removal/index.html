Common questions

Can you take down a tree right next to my house?

Yes — that’s a big part of what we do. We use a bucket truck and rigging to lower limbs and sections under control, so trees crowded against roofs, fences, pools and power lines come down without damage.

Do you haul everything away?

Yes. Cleanup and haul-off are part of the job, not an add-on we forget about. Logs, brush and debris are loaded out the same day — you won’t be left with a pile to deal with.

How much does it cost?

Every tree is different — size, access and what’s nearby all matter. Estimates are free, so call us and we’ll come look and give you a straight, upfront price before any work starts.

What about storm damage or an emergency?

Call us any time. A tree on the house or blocking the driveway can’t wait, so we respond to storm and hazard situations 24/7 and get a crew out to make it safe.

Do you grind the stump too?

We can. Once the tree is down we’ll grind the stump below grade if you’d like, so you can reclaim that part of the yard. Just mention it when you call.

Are you insured?

Yes — we carry insurance and it’s noted right on our trucks. Tree work is heavy, risky work, and we make sure you’re protected before we start.
