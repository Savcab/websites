Questions we hear a lot

Do you take on large-acreage clearing?

Yes — it's some of our favorite work. Overgrown lots, fence-line cottonwoods, and full-parcel clearing are right in our wheelhouse. We've cleared two acres of cottonwoods in two days, so don't hesitate to ask about a bigger job.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the size of the tree, how close it is to buildings or power lines, and access to the site. That's why we come out and look first, then give you a clear estimate before any work starts — no guessing over the phone.

Can you handle really tall trees?

Absolutely. The big cottonwoods out here can be massive, and we bring the right equipment — including a crane when a job calls for it — to take them down safely, even when they're tight against a house.

Do you grind the stump too?

We can. Stump grinding is an add-on to most removals — just let us know and we'll include it in your estimate so you can replant, build, or just mow without dodging the stump.

What about the mess afterward?

Cleanup is part of the job. We haul off the debris and leave the site tidy. As more than one reviewer has put it, when we're done you'd hardly know the tree was there.
