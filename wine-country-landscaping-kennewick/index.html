The finished stone is what you notice. The base we build under it is why it stays flat and level, season after season.

We're a Kennewick-based design-build crew building patios, walls, concrete and drainage across the Tri-Cities. We treat every job as a small design problem first — how the hardscape meets the house, how water moves off the site in our dry, freeze-thaw climate, and how it will look once the yard has grown in.

Then we build it the slow way: excavation, a compacted base, the right grade and drainage, and stone, block or concrete set by hand. It's the part no one photographs, and it's why the work is still flat and level a few seasons on.

How we work

Designed, built, and a site left clean.

A straightforward path from the first look at your yard to the day you start using it.

Walk the site

We come look at the yard, talk through what you're picturing, and figure out how the hardscape, the grade and the drainage want to work together.

Plan & estimate

You get a clear written estimate — materials, layout and price laid out, with no vague numbers and no surprise add-ons once we start.

Build on a base

We excavate, compact the sub-base, set the grade for drainage, and lay the block, stone or concrete by hand — the slow part, done right.

Hand it back

We finish the details, clean the site down, and leave you a patio, wall or driveway that's ready to use and built to stay put.

What we build

One crew, the whole build.

Hardscape, concrete and the planting around it — start to finish. The same crew that walks your yard and prices the job is the one that builds it.
