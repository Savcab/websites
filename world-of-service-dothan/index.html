02 — Our story

A Dothan landscape company since 1994.

Since we opened our doors in 1994, we've treated every property like it matters — because to the people who live and work there, it does. Landscaping isn't only an expression of your taste; it's an investment that deserves careful attention to detail.

For more than three decades, founder and president Alfredo Rodriguez and our team have designed, overseen, and maintained landscapes for homes and businesses across the Wiregrass. Our objective is simple: custom outdoor spaces that stay beautiful.
