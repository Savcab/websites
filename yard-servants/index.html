Frequently asked questions

Have a question we didn’t cover? Give us a call — we’re happy to talk it through.

How much does lawn care cost?

Every yard is different, so we price by the property and the work involved — but our estimates are always free and upfront, and customers consistently call us “reasonable.” You’ll know the cost before we ever start.

Do you offer one-time service or only recurring?

Both. We do one-time cleanups, washes and projects, as well as weekly or biweekly mowing and ongoing maintenance plans. Tell us what fits your schedule and budget.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Silver Lake and serve the surrounding Kosciusko County area — Claypool, Mentone, North Manchester, Akron, Warsaw and nearby. If you’re close by, just ask and we’ll let you know.

Can you handle landscaping and pressure washing too?

Yes — that’s the whole idea. Beyond mowing, we do planting beds, mulch, retaining walls, house and walkway pressure washing, leaf removal and seasonal cleanups. One crew for the whole yard.

Do you really give a discount for referrals?

We do. Refer a friend or neighbor and you’ll get 10% off — up to two referrals. It’s our way of saying thanks for spreading the word.
