06 — Good to know

Questions, answered.

A few things people ask before they call. Anything else, just reach out.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No — estimates are free. Call or send the form, we'll come look at the property, listen to what you want, and give you an honest price with no obligation.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Bainbridge and serve Decatur County and the surrounding towns — Climax, Attapulgus, Brinson, Whigham, Cairo, Donalsonville and the Lake Seminole area. If you're nearby and not sure, just ask.

Do you do both homes and businesses?

Yes. We handle residential yards, larger acreage, and commercial property — from a weekly mow to a full landscape or irrigation install.

Can you install a sprinkler / irrigation system?

That's one of our specialties. We design and install in-ground irrigation sized to your property, including multi-zone systems for larger lawns, so everything gets watered evenly.

How do I get started?
