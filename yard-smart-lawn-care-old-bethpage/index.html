Why neighbors choose us

Same hands, same standards, year after year.

Yard Smart is a Long Island landscaping and lawn-care company rooted right here in Old Bethpage. We do the ordinary things well — a clean, even cut; edges that look intentional; beds that get better each season — and we show up when we say we will. That consistency is what turns a first mow into a seven-year handshake.

  • An eye for detail. The finish matters to us as much as it does to you — the whole property should look cared for, not just cut.

  • One team for everything. Weekly maintenance, a new front bed, landscape lighting, seasonal clean-ups — you're not chasing three different crews.

  • Local, and staying that way. We work the Old Bethpage and Oyster Bay neighborhoods week in and week out — the same streets, season after season.
